Sebastian Castellio's Latin Bible (Basel, 1551) in a reader's edition: Gaffiot 2016 orthography with full macrons,
modern paragraphs, poetry set as verse, quotation marks, and verses numbered as in the King James Version. Edited by
Nathan Anderson.

**This release**: the complete New Testament, Genesis and Exodus. New since v2.0:

- Exodus 21–40. Wikisource has no Exodus 31–40, so those chapters were read line by line from the pages of the
  1738 Leipzig edition. At Exod 40:20 every printing has the bars fitted to the altar (*āræ*) where the Hebrew has
  the ark; the text keeps Castellio's word, and a footnote gives the Hebrew.
- Castellio's text as printed. On the website, any chapter of the New Testament, Genesis or Exodus can be switched
  to the text as his printer set it, with its own spelling, punctuation and paragraphs: Basel 1573 for the New
  Testament, Leipzig 1738 for the Old. Every word the OCR could not read was read on the page images, and each
  verse links to its page.
- Corrections. Readings checked on the pages of Castellio's own editions (Exod 8:19 *occalluit*, 21:6 *sūbulā*,
  John 4:29 *fortassis* and others); spellings brought into line with Gaffiot (*ānulus*, *Ismaēl*, *sēdecim*,
  *sēmet ipsum*); the classical number forms (*duodēvīgintī*); and *inquit* set off by commas throughout.
- On the website, verse 1 now has a number, the preface is left-aligned on a phone like the chapters, and the
  italic *æ* no longer looks like *œ*. The PDF can now print Hebrew in the footnotes.

The New Testament follows the Basel edition of 1573, with Castellio's last lifetime edition (1556) as the reference;
the Old Testament follows the Leipzig edition of 1738. The chapters taken through the pipeline since November 2026
are approved for now; the editor's full read-through is still to come, and corrections will appear in later releases.

**Files**: `castellio.epub` (with macrons) and `castellio-sine-macronibus.epub` (without); `castellio.pdf` (A5, for
print); `castellio.txt` and `-sine-macronibus.txt` (plain text); `castellio.tsv` and `castellio.json` (one row per
verse, with and without macrons); `castellio-site.zip` (the website, also at https://njand.github.io/biblia-castellionis/).
