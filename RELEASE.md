Sebastian Castellio's Latin Bible (Basel, 1551) in a reader's edition: Gaffiot 2016 orthography with full macrons,
modern paragraphs, poetry set as verse, quotation marks, and verses numbered as in the King James Version. Edited by
Nathan Anderson.

**This release**: the complete New Testament, with Genesis and Exodus 1–20. New since v1.0: Galatians through
Revelation, and spelling made consistent across the whole text (*cum* for *quum*, *quandoquidem*, and Gaffiot's
spellings such as *cōntiō*, *condiciō*, *cāritās*, *umquam*). The New Testament
follows the Basel edition of 1573, with Castellio's last lifetime edition (1556) as the reference; the Old Testament
follows the Leipzig edition of 1738. The chapters taken through the pipeline since November 2026 are approved for now;
the editor's full read-through is still to come, and corrections will appear in later releases.

**Files**: `castellio.epub` (with macrons) and `castellio-sine-macronibus.epub` (without); `castellio.pdf` (A5, for
print); `castellio.txt` and `-sine-macronibus.txt` (plain text); `castellio.tsv` and `castellio.json` (one row per
verse, with and without macrons); `castellio-site.zip` (the website, also at https://njand.github.io/biblia-castellionis/).
