# Slips in the settled chapters, found while reading Exodus 1–20

Proposed 2026-11-08. The Exodus readers compared their words with your settled forms (`data/gold/`) and found five
markings there that the grammar of the verse rules out. Each was checked in its verse. Nothing is applied: these are
settled chapters, so they wait for your Decision line.

**Decision:** accept all (Nathan, 2026-11-09)

1. **Gen 8:5** *cujus mēnsīs diē prīmā* → *mēnsis*: genitive singular of *mēnsis* with *cujus* (*mēnsīs* is the
   ablative plural of *mēnsa*).
   **Apply:** `Gen.8.5` la: `cujus mēnsīs` → `cujus mēnsis`
2. **Gen 13:9** *ūniversa tibi rēgiō patet* → *regiō*: *regiō* (from *regō*) has a short e in Gaffiot and the
   lexicon; your text has *regiō* elsewhere. The same at Luke 21:23 (item 5).
   **Apply:** `Gen.13.9` la: `tibi rēgiō patet` → `tibi regiō patet`
3. **Gen 22:14** *mōns ā Jōva videndō* → *ā Jōvā*: ablative after *ā*, as *ā Jōvā vidente* in the same verse and
   your 11 other *ā Jōvā*.
   **Apply:** `Gen.22.14` la: `mōns ā Jōva videndō` → `mōns ā Jōvā videndō`
4. **Gen 24:8** *jūrisjūrandī religiōnē solūtus* → *religiōne*: ablative singular of *religiō* (no long final e).
   The settled form had fed the pipeline's *religiōnē* at Exod 12:14, which the reader corrected.
   **Apply:** `Gen.24.8` la: `religiōnē solūtus` → `religiōne solūtus`
5. **Luke 21:23** *Nam hæc rēgiō graviter* → *regiō* (as item 2).
   **Apply:** `Luke.21.23` la: `hæc rēgiō graviter` → `hæc regiō graviter`
