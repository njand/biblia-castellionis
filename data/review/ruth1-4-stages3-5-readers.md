# Ruth 1–4, phase 2: the readers' and proofreaders' notes

Two readers (1–2, 3–4), a names reader and two proofreaders (1–2, 3–4), 2026-11-19, under one brief (Judges' and
Deuteronomy's rules; scratch `ruth/brief.md`, summarized in `ruth1-4-edition.md`). Their rows are in
`data/decisions/Ruth.layout.tsv` (56) and `Ruth.forms.tsv` (110); the names reader's table is `ruth1-4-names.md`; the
notes reader's files are `ruth-notes.md` and `ruth-notes-1554.md`. Internal notes, condensed from their reports by
Claude; where a call was changed after them, it says so.

## Reader 1: Ruth 1–2

**Rows**: 34 layout, 28 forms; `check.py` 0 errors. All 45 verses read beside the KJV, the BSB (with its layout
column), the Hebrew and 1738's printed text; every flag in `ruth1-4-ch01.md`, `-ch02.md` gone through.

### Quotation structure

- **Quoted**: Naomi 1:8–9 (opened after 1738's colon *sīc admonuit:*, closed at *nancīscāminī*), 1:11–13 (closed at
  *urget*), 1:15; Ruth's vow 1:16–17; Naomi 1:20–21; 2:2 *Ī sānē*; Boaz and the reapers 2:4; Boaz 2:5; the foreman
  2:6–7 (still speaking in 2:7: *hīc*, *manet*); Boaz 2:8–9, 2:11–12; Ruth 2:10, 2:13; Naomi 2:19a, 2:20, 2:22; Ruth
  2:21. *Inquit* set off by commas, the quotation closed and reopened (decision 65): 1:16, 2:11, 2:19, 2:20, 2:21, 2:22
  (2:19's colon after *inquit* made a comma); 2:5 *“Cūja est ista puella?” inquit.*, as the settled *“Scīsne Græcē?”
  inquit.*
- **Reported, unmarked** though the BSB quotes: the daughters-in-law 1:10; the women 1:19 (*num ea Noemis esset*);
  Ruth's request 2:2; Boaz's invitation 2:14 and orders 2:15–16; Ruth's answer 2:19b (*docetque … nōmen esse*). The
  speech inside 2:7 (*ut sibi licēret*) and 2:21 (*ut … hærērem*) is reported: no ‘…’. 24 “ and 24 ”.

### Paragraphs

1:6 removed (inside the period begun at 1:5b *Mulier autem*; a sentence that starts mid-verse gets none, as Judg 19:29);
added 1:11 (1:1–13 would run to about 270 words, decision 52; the BSB breaks), 1:14 (1738's indent at *At Rutha*, moved
to the sentence start), 1:18 (1738's indent; it closes the poetry); 2:3, 2:4, 2:8, 2:17 (1738's indents and the BSB's),
2:14 (1738's indent at *Illa ubi*, moved to *Deinde*). No paragraph per speaker. Kept 1:1, 1:19.

### Poetry

**1:16–17, Ruth's vow, set as verse** on the BSB's eight lines, opening mid-verse after *At Rūtha:* (as 1 Cor 1:19):
Castellio's paired members follow the Hebrew clause for clause, his colons mark the halves, the elliptic *commūnis
Deus*. 1:20–21 prose (the BSB sets none).

### Capitals and punctuation

*Mōabīticum/-ō* capital (1:1, 1:2, 1:22, 2:6), as the settled text; 1:20 *Omnipotēns* capital, God's title (Shaddai),
as the settled Gen 49:25 *ab Omnipotente* (1738 lower case); vocatives set off (1:11, 1:12, 2:8 *Audin', fīlia?*);
1:18 the colon before the main verb *dēsiit* a comma (as Judg 1:4, 2:10); 2:13 *domine* lower case (Ruth to Boaz).
Otherwise 1738's punctuation, which the draft follows throughout 1–2.

### Forms

Ablatives: 1:1 *ortā*, 1:10 *ipsā*, 1:13 *vestrā* (*causā*), 1:14 *eā*, 1:15 *frātriā* (#2), 2:11 *terrāque*, 2:13
*hūmānā*, 2:20 *vīvīs*. Corrected: 1:13 *manus* (subject of *urget*), 1:17 *moriēris*, 1:17 *ūna* (with *mors*), 2:15
*nōn modo* (settled 14 : 2), 2:18 *cibātūs* (genitive), 2:7, 2:8 *hīc* (adverb), 2:7 *vēnit*, 2:17 *lēgit* (perfects).
Perfect subjunctives: 2:8 *īverīs*, *discesserīs* (prohibitions), 2:11 *præstiterīs*, *contulerīs* (indirect
questions), 2:12 *recēperīs* (causal relative). No source: 1:16 *dīversāberis*, *dīversābor* (as the settled
*dīversātur*), 2:12 *cumulātissimō*. Confirmed: 1:6 *levāsse*, 1:22 *nurus*, 2:9 *metent*.

### Doubts (left as printed)

1:9 *quiētem suum* (1738 *quietem suum*; *quiēs* feminine); 1:17 *ut ūna mē mors ā tē dīvellet* (future indicative;
1738 *diuellet*); 2:19 *nōmen esse Boozus* (nominative in acc. and inf.; probably Castellio's idiom). For the names
reader: *Rūthā* in the nominative (done there).

## Reader 2: Ruth 3–4

**Rows**: 21 layout, 21 forms; `check.py` 0 errors. All 40 verses read the same way.

### Quotation structure

- **Quoted**: Naomi 3:1–4 (after *sīc locūta est:*, closed at *ostendet.*), Ruth 3:5, Boaz and Ruth 3:9, Boaz
  3:10–13, Naomi 3:18, Boaz 4:3–4 (closed at *ego sum.*), the kinsman 4:4 (*Tum ille: “Ego redimam,” inquit.*), Boaz
  4:5, the kinsman 4:6, Boaz's declaration 4:9–10, the people and elders 4:11–12, the women 4:14–15 (1738's semicolon
  before the speech, *dicebant; gratias*, a colon). Where 1738 has *X: words, inquit:* the colon after the speaker is a
  comma, as the settled Judg 6:23: 3:9 *Cui illa,*, 3:10 *Et Boozus,*, 4:6 *At propinquus,*. Without *inquit*, or with
  it after the words, 1738's colon stays.
- **Reported, unmarked** though the BSB quotes: 3:14 *præcēpit, ut cavēret*; 3:15 *jussitque, ut*; 3:16–17 Naomi's
  question and Ruth's account, with Boaz's words in it (*negāns, vacuæ veniendum esse*); 4:1 *jussit accēdere*; 4:17
  *dictitantēs, Noemī nātum esse fīlium*.

### Paragraphs

Kept 3:1, 4:1, 4:13, 4:18; added 3:6 (a new scene; 1738 indents; chapter 3 was 326 words in one), 3:16 (Ruth back with
Naomi; BSB and 1738), 4:7 (the aside on the sandal; BSB and 1738; 4:1–12 was 296 words). Not followed: 1738's indents at
3:14 (*changed by proofreader 2: added*), 4:11 and inside 4:3 (new speakers), the BSB's 4:14.

### Poetry

None; 4:18–22 prose.

### Capitals and punctuation

3:4 the colon between the *cum* clause and *observātō* a comma (as Judg 1:4); 3:11 *animō, fīlia:* (vocative set off;
1738 no comma); 4:3 *Mōabīticō* capital; 4:3 *Fundum agrī Elimelechī, cōnsanguineī nostrī,* for 1738's *Fundum agri,
Elimelechi consanguinei nostri* (its comma split *agrī* from its genitive; a clarity call). Kept as 1738: the
semicolons of 3:12–13, 4:11 *Testēs sumus, faxit*, the parenthesis at 4:13.

### Forms

Ablatives: 3:1 *cum eā*, 3:2 *in āreā* (place where; 3:14 *in āream* motion), 3:18 *rē perfectā*, 4:10 *patriāque*,
4:11 *in cūriā*, 4:12 *ex hāc puellā*, 4:13 *Jōvā … dante*. 3:3 *lavā* (imperative of *lavō*); 3:6 *socrūs* (genitive);
3:16, 4:1 *vēnit* (narrative perfects); 4:11 *quæ domum tuam vēnit* (perfect, the KJV's "that is come"; a judgment
call); 4:5 *habenda* (nominative with *Rūtha … uxor*). Confirmed with a reason: 3:4 *adītō*, 3:15 *mēnsus*, 4:3
*reversa*, 4:4 *indicēs*, 4:6 *redimitō*, 4:7 *tōta*, 4:11 *præclāra*, *parēs*.

### Doubts

4:15 *cui amantissima tuī nurus peperit* (*quem* expected; 1738 and Wikisource *cui*). Two doubts about the printed
text (3:2 *pull is*, 3:7 *QuumqueBoozus*) are 1738's own slips, checked on the page in phase 1; the draft corrects them
(`ruth1-4-draft-fixes.md`).

## Proofreader 1: Ruth 1–2

Read every verse of the preview against the BSB, the Vulgate, the Hebrew and 1738; the draft's words match 1738's
throughout once the approved rules are applied. **Rows**: 3 layout, 1 forms; 0 errors. All accepted:

- 1:1 the semicolon after *Jūdææ* a comma (it cut *vir quīdam* off from *profectus est*; the ablative absolute between
  commas, as 1:18; Wikisource has a comma).
- 2:8 *Ne iveris* capital after the complete question *Audin', fīlia?* (as 1:11 *comitēminī? Numquid*).
- 2:17 the comma before the parenthesis moved after it (*lēgerat (fuit … hordeī),*), before the main verb in 2:18.
- 2:8 *Audīn* (*audīsne* shortened, the ī of *audīs* long; the settled *Ain* is unmarked, but *ăĭs* is short).

Checked and left: quotation marks, the poetry of 1:16–17, the paragraphs; *Cūja* unmarked (Gaffiot *cŭjus*),
*dīgressa*, *trītūrātum*, *dīvellet*, *socrū*, *socruī*, *sequāre*, *perāctam*, *pāne*, *assēdisset*, *cēpit*.
Castellio's possible slips: 1:9 *quiētem suum* (perhaps with *marītum* by hyperbaton), 1:17 *dīvellet*; **2:23**
leaves out the Hebrew's last clause וַתֵּשֶׁב אֶת־חֲמוֹתָהּ ("and dwelt with her mother in law"; 1738 and Wikisource
end at *facta messis est*).
