## 15:29 *Ionem* (Ijon)

The initial *I* is right: a vowel, not *J*. The Hebrew is עִיּוֹן (*ʿIyyôn*, the vowel *i* first), and the settled
1 Kgs 15:20 has the same town in the same phrase, *subēgit Iōnem et Danem, et Ābēlmaacham* (preview here *Iōnem*, as
settled). The draft's i/j step was right to leave it. No change.

## Draft errors (for draft fixes §3)

The draft's words agree with 1738's printed text word for word in 15–17 (the read lines compared). One u/v error of
the draft's own, three times (1738 *Sepharuaim-*, Hebrew סְפַרְוַיִם, the *u* a consonant; the draft's u/v step left
it, as *triumuir*):

- `2Kgs.17.31` la: `diis sepharuaimensibus filios` → `diis sepharvaimensibus filios`

My rows keep *Sepharuaim-* (17:31's row capitalized: *Sepharuaimensibus*, which merge.py's capital fallback
covers). The same slip is in the draft at 19:13 (*rex urbis Sepharuaimi*; r7's chapters). *triumuir* 15:25 is
already in §3. No printer's slips found in 15–17.
