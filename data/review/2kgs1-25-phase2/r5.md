## Draft errors (for draft fixes §3)

The draft's words agree with 1738's printed text word for word in 11–14 (checked by script, u/v, i/j and æ folded),
apart from 13:14 *equites* and 13:15 *praecepit*, already fixed. One i/j error of the draft's own:
- **11:8 *introjverit***: 1738 *introiuerit* (= *introiverit*, from *introeō*); the draft's i/j step made the *i* a *j*:
  `2Kgs.11.8` la: `qui intra septum introjverit, moriatur` → `qui intra septum introiverit, moriatur`. My row keeps
  *introjverit*.

11:16 *Illi, injectis* is already spaced in the draft. No printer's slips found in 11–14 besides the two fixed.

## Spelling gaps (for the rules; not changed)

- 13:18 *sumsisset*: 1738's *-ms-* for Gaffiot's *sūmpsī* (`sumsisset → sumpsisset`, if the rules take it; as r4's
  *comsit*).
- 11:9 *adsumtisque*: both the *ad-* and the *-mpt-* gaps already noted (settled *assūmpt-*).
- 13:10, 14:21 *sexdecim* is *sēdecim* in the preview (a rule); 13:23 *misertus* → *miseritus* (approved rule) and
  14:7 *adpellavit* → *appellāvit*, 13:18 *adloquens* → *alloquēns*, 13:23 *adspernatus* → *āspernātus* applied.

## Doubts and judgments

- **12:1**: `\p` at the chapter (the model) rather than a paragraph at 11:21 with 12:1 `\nb`; 12:1–3 is a 38-word
  paragraph opening in lower case.
- **12:4** (or 12:6) added; **13:9** for the draft's 13:10; 13:14–25 left one paragraph (224 words).
- **11:6 *Suriam*** capitalized; ***domūs*** genitive.
- **12:5 *diœcēsī*** (ablative *-ī*) against the preview's *diœcēsi*.
- **13:19 *cecīdissēs*, *victūrus***: the sense (Elisha's *pulsāssēs … cecīdissēs*: had you struck five or six
  times, you would have cut the Syrians down) and the Hebrew הִכִּיתָ ("you would have struck") settle *cædō* and
  *vincō*.
- Asterisks and footnotes: 1738 has none in 11–14 (the transcription's list: 3:7, 4:37, 6:25, 6:33, 20:13; footnotes
  at 2:12, 3:14, 4:13, 9:11, 18:17 on). The BSB's version notes in 11–14, if any, are for notes readers A and B.
