## Doubts (the words not changed)

- **7:16 *væniitque*, 7:18 *vænirent***: 1738's *væn-* for *vēneō* (be sold). No rule, no settled precedent (the
  settled text has *vēneant*); the preview keeps *væniitque* and leaves *vænirent* unmarked. My rows keep 1738's
  spelling (*væniitque*, *vænīrent*). If the edition should write Gaffiot's *vēn-*: a spelling rule `vaeniitque →
  veniitque`, `vaenirent → venirent` (then *vēniitque*, *vēnīrent*). For Claude.
- **Spelling gaps**: 7:13 *cōnsūmtīs* and 7:14 *sūmtīs* left by the preview beside its *sūmptō* (7:8, 8:8) and *ēmptum*
  (7:1): the *-mpt-* rules are word-by-word and lack these two (`sumtis → sumptis`, `consumtis → consumptis`). 8:12
  *adfectūrus* left unassimilated (the *ad-* rules again, as r1 and r2 found).
- **6:22 *occīdās licet***: Castellio makes a question (the BSB's “Would you kill those you have captured …?”) a
  statement (“those you take with your own sword you may kill”); his construal, left.
- **7:6 *Fēcerat enim Dominus***: *Dominus*, not Castellio's usual *Jōva*, as printed (the Hebrew has אֲדֹנָי here, not
  the Tetragrammaton): left.
- **8:18 *familia,(nam***: printed with no space; the layout row adds one (a space only).
- **Asterisks** (1738's numbering 6:25, 6:33, no footnote) and any note: for notes reader A.
- **Judgments**: the paragraphs at 6:13 (or 6:15) and 7:12; 8:5 *supervēnit* and *hīc*; 6:27 *subvenit* (present).
