## 9:16 *decubabat*

Not a misprint, in my view. *dēcubō, -āre* is a real if rare verb: `python -m pipeline latin macron decubo` returns
Morpheus's *dēcubō* and Gaffiot's entry *dēcŭbō, āre*; the tool knows no inflected form, which is why *decubabat* came
back "no source" (as *cubābat* is known for *cubō*). *ubi Jorāmus dēcubābat* = "where Joram lay (abed)", the Hebrew
שֹׁכֵב "lying"; 1738 and 1750 agree. Castellio chose a compound of *cubō* for the wounded king lying in bed (*decumbēbat*,
from *dēcumbō*, would mean "was taking to his bed"). Left as printed; my forms row gives *dēcubābat*. The notes reader
may still look at 1554.
