## Doubts and slips (the words not changed)

- **2:19 *quemaddum*** (1738 *quemad-/dum* across a line end; the printed text the same): a printer's slip for
  *quemadmodum*, the syllable *mo* lost at the line break. Recommend a draft fix (§3): `quemaddum videret` →
  `quemadmodum videret`. The preview leaves it flagged *unknown*; no forms row written.
- **3:9 *circujvissent*** (draft): 1738 prints *circuiuissent* (read line 432/1/78; `text/orig/2KI.usfm`), i.e.
  *circuivissent*; the draft's i/j step made the second *i* a *j*. A draft fix (§3): `circujvissent` → `circuivissent`
  (then a form *circuīvissent*; not written, since the row must spell the draft's word).
- **3:16 *alueo*** (draft and preview *alueō*): 1738 *alueo* = *alveo* (3:17 *alveum*); the draft's u/v step left it.
  A draft fix (§3): `hoc alueo frequentes` → `hoc alveo frequentes`.
- **Spelling gaps** in the preview, for the approved *ad-* rules (not this reading's call): 1:6 *adscendissēs* and
  1:16 *adscendistī* left beside 1:4 *ascendisset*; 2:20 *adferrī*, *adlātō*, 3:7 *adfutūrum*, 3:20 *adfluēbat* left
  as printed (1 Kgs settled *affers*, 1:42).
- **1:6** *ut tū … mitterēs*: the man's message is reported (*nūllusne esset*) but turns to the second person; read as
  Castellio's mixing, left unmarked inside the messengers' direct speech.
- **3:10 *hīc*** is a judgment (adverb against pronoun); the Hebrew has only "and the king of Israel said".
- No other printer's slips found in 1–3 beyond 1:1 *Moabibitæ*, already fixed.
