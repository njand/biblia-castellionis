# 2 Kings 13–18: proofreader p3 (2026-11-23)

I read every verse of 13–18 (190 verses) in `text/draft/2KI.preview.usfm` (built 06:42 from the merged decision files and
the draft fixes §1–4) against 1738 (`text/orig/2KI.usfm`), the BSB with its layout, the KJV's ¶s, and the readers'
reports r5–r7 and `names.md`.

**Words.** A script compared the preview's words with 1738's, folding case, macrons, æ/ae, i/j and u/v, and checked
each difference against the approved rules of `data/rules/orthography.tsv`. Every difference is an approved rule or a
listed draft fix:
- **Draft fixes:** 13:14 *equitēs* and 13:15 *præcēpit* (§1); 14:11 and 14:13 *Bēthsemem* (§4); 17:24, 17:31, 18:34
  *Sepharvaim-* (§3) match 1738 once u/v is folded.
- **Rule changes:** *quum, quumque → cum, cumque*; *eis → iis*; *sexdecim → sēdecim*; *simulac → simul ac*; *misertus
  → miseritus*; *adloquens, adspernatus, adpellavit, adfectus, adserti, adspectu, adscendi → all-, asp-, app-, aff-,
  ass-, asp-, asc-*; *decimoquinto, quartodecimo → decimō quīntō, quārtō decimō*; *petiit → petīvit*; *caussa → causa*;
  *obedi- → obœdī-*; *brachio → bracchiō*; *quamdam → quandam*.

I found no omission or misreading. The punctuation differs from 1738 only at 13:15 (the draft fix), 13:17 (*(inquit
ille)* made commas, r5) and 18:28 (colon before *inquit* a comma, r7), all readers' rows.

**Macrons: 6 forms rows** (`p3-forms.tsv`):
- 13:3 ***Hazælī***, the dative, as p2 asked (8:8, 12:18).
- 13:7 ***perditīs***: the ablative absolute *reliquīs ā Syriæ rēge perditīs, et … redāctīs*; the preview had the
  indicative's *perditis*.
- 14:16 ***vītā***: *Jōæ vītā dēfūnctō*; the preview had *vīta*, the only one of the book's *vītā dēfūnctō* left
  unmarked.
- 15:16 ***pervāstāvit*** and 18:25 ***pervāstandum***: Gaffiot *pervāstō* and the settled *vāst-*; the preview had
  *pervast-* (M only).
- 18:2 ***fīliā***: *mātre nōmine Abī, Zacharīæ fīliā*, in apposition to the ablative, as the preview's 22:1, 23:31,
  23:36 and 24:8 have it.

**Layout: 1 row** (`p3-layout.tsv`):
- 18:36: a paragraph at ***Ad hæc dicta hominēs tacuērunt***, where the narrative resumes after the Rabshakeh's
  second speech closes at 18:35. 18:26–37 ran to 270 words, over 1 Kings' measure; now 18:26–35 226 and 18:36–37 44.
  The BSB breaks there; no KJV ¶ or 1738 indent. r7's alternative, 18:28, would leave 18:17–27 at 276.

Everything else stands:
- **Quotation marks:** balanced in 13–18 (12 “, 12 ”; 2 ‘, 2 ’, the king of Assyria's words inside the Rabshakeh's
  speech, 18:29–30 and 18:31–35). The speeches over verses close where they should (14:9–10, 18:19–25, 18:29–35).
  *inquit* is set off with commas (13:17, 18:27, 18:28). Reported speech is unmarked as r5–r7 set it.
- **Capitals:** *Isrāēlīticās* 13:25, *Isrāēlīticum* 14:27, *Thapsēnsēsque* 15:16, *Nephthālēnsem* 15:29,
  *Isrāēlīticōrum* 16:3, *Samarītāna* 17:24, 17:26, *Sepharvaimēnsibus* 17:31, *Mēdicæ* 18:11, *Syriacē*,
  *Jūdaicē* 18:26, 18:28, and 18:34's peoples. Lower case after a question mark is kept as printed at 18:20, 18:21 and
  18:34.
- **Paragraphs:** all open at Castellio's sentence starts, apart from the chapter break at 16:1 (`\p` on 1738's
  *Jūdææ rēgnum obtinuit*, after 15:38's comma, as r6 set it on the model of 1 Kgs 3:1 and p2's 12:1). The largest
  are now 17:7–18 (238), 18:26–35 (226), 13:14–25 (225), 18:17–25 (212) and 16:10–20 (209).
- **Poetry:** none, as the BSB sets none in 13–18.

**Left as they stand:**
- 13:22 *īnfestāvit*: Gaffiot *īnfēstō* and most settled books have *īnfēst-*, but the settled 1 Samuel and 1 Kings
  (1 Kgs *īnfestāvit*) and Numbers have *īnfest-*. 2 Kings follows 1 Kings. For Nathan: one form across the Bible?
- 17:4 *Suam* (So, Hebrew סוֹא): unmarked; the names reader did not list it, and Castellio's *u* is not the Hebrew's
  holem, so I left it.
- The readers' judgments: r5's 13:9 paragraph and 13:14–25 as one; r6's 15:22, 16:1, 17:5 out and 17:7, 17:19, 17:34
  in; r7's 18:26. They are sound.

them:

  approved). 15:9 *adstrīnxerat* (`adstrinxerat → astrinxerat`, Gaffiot's headword *astringō*, if decision 37 takes
