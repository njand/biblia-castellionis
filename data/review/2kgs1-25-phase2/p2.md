# 2 Kings 7–12: proofreader p2 (2026-11-23)

I read every verse of 7–12 (164 verses) in `text/draft/2KI.preview.usfm` (built 06:42 from the merged decision files and
the draft fixes §1–4) against the BSB with its layout, the KJV, the Vulgate and 1738 (`text/orig/2KI.usfm`), with the
Hebrew where the readers cited it.

**Words.** A script compared the preview's words with 1738's, folding case, macrons, æ/ae, i/j and u/v, and checked
each difference against the approved rules of `data/rules/orthography.tsv`. Every difference is an approved rule or a
listed draft fix:
- **Draft fixes:** 8:26 *Ambris* (§4.1). 7:2, 7:17, 7:19 *triumvir*, 9:25 *triumvirō*, 10:25 *triumvirīs, triumvirī*,
  10:23 *Jōnādābō* and 11:8 *introīverit* (§3) match 1738 once u/v and i/j are folded. 8:1 *indīcī* is 1738's *indîci*
  without its accent (§1.6).
- **Rule changes:** *quum, quumque → cum, cumque*; *eis, eisque → iis, iisque*; *emtum, sumto, sumtoque, peremtis →
  -mpt-*; *quatuor → quattuor*; *adloquitur, adpellavit → all-, app-*; *serviuisse → servisse*; *obeditis →
  obœdītis*; *epistola → epistula*; the phrases *simulac → simul ac*, *ne dum → nēdum*, *tamdiu → tam diū*.

I found no omission or misreading. Every punctuation difference from 1738 in 7–12 (7:2, 8:13, 9:22, 9:25, 10:15) has a
reader's layout row; 8:18 *familiā, (nam* only adds 1738's missing space.

**Macrons: 2 forms rows** (`p2-forms.tsv`):
- 8:8 and 12:18 ***Hazælī***, the dative (*jussit rēx Hazælī, ut …*; *Hazælī Syriæ rēgī missīs*). The preview had
  *Hazæli*. A third-declension dative ends in long *-ī*, as the settled *Dāvīdī* (55×), *Pharaōnī* (19×), *Samuēlī*,
  *Isrāēlī*; Hazæl's other cases are *Hazælem, Hazælis, Hazæle*. The stem stays unmarked, as the names reader left it.
  **For p3:** 13:3 *dēdidit eōs Hazæli Syriæ rēgī* is the same dative.

  *eā* 12:14; *bonā fidē* 12:15; *ab Hierosolymā* 12:18; *factā conjūrātiōne* 12:20.

`macron-check` on 7–12 flags only settled conventions (*Jōva, Jorām-, Jojāda, Ēlīam, usque, sēque, Dane, ne*). Its 142
unknown words are names, æ/œ words and forms checked above.

**Layout: no rows.** Everything stands:
- **Quotation marks:** balanced in 7–12 (40 “, 40 ”; one ‘…’, the oracle nested in 9:6–10). Speeches over verses close
  where they should (7:3–4, 9:1–3, 9:6–10, 9:25–26, 10:2–3, 10:9–10, 11:5–8). *inquit* is set off with commas
  throughout. Reported speech is unmarked as the readers set it.
- **Capitals:** *Isrāēlīticōrum* 7:13, *Achābicæ* 8:27, *Jezrælēns-* 9:10, 9:36, 9:37, 10:1, *Isrāēlīticīs* 10:21,
  *Suriam* 11:6. Lower case after a question mark is kept as printed at 9:18, 9:19 and 9:32.
- **Paragraphs:** all open at Castellio's sentence starts. 12:1 opens on 1738's *vidēlicet* with `\p`, as r5 set it on
  the model of 1 Kgs 3:1. The largest are 12:4–16 (254 words), 7:3–11 (222), 9:1–10 (210), 10:18–27 (209) and 9:14–21
  (205).
- **Poetry:** none, as the BSB sets none.

**Left as they stand:**
- **12:4–16 runs to 254 words**, at 1 Kings' measure. r5's alternative, 12:6 *Sed annō rēgnī*, gives 83/209 but
  leaves the order and its failure apart. I see no reason to split it.
- **11:1 *māter Athalia: vidēns***: 1738's colon between the subject and its participle is kept, as printed.
- **The readers' judgments:** r3's 7:12 paragraph and its moves to 7:17 and 8:24; r4's 9:11, 9:22 and 10:8 paragraphs and the one inside
  10:12; r5's 12:4 paragraph and 12:1 `\p`. They are sound.

**Spelling gaps.** p1 listed the nine *ad-* words of 1–6; the gaps below are new. All were noticed by r3–r5, and the
preview keeps them as 1738 prints them:
- ***-mt-* for *-mpt-***. The approved rules go word by word and lack these: 7:13 *cōnsūmtīs* (`consumtis →
  consumptis`); 7:14 *sūmtīs* (`sumtis → sumptis`); 9:3 *sūmtāque* (`sumtaque → sumptaque`; the settled text has
  *sūmptāque*); 11:9 *adsūmtīsque* (`adsumtisque → assumptisque`; the approved rules have *adsumptis → assumptis*).
- ***ad-* unassimilated**, with the related forms approved: 8:12 *adfectūrus* (`adfecturus → affecturus`); 10:6
  *adferte* (`adferte → afferte`); 10:8 *adlāta* (`adlata → allata`; *adlatam → allatam* is approved). 10:19
  *adfuisset* is a compound of *sum* and stays.
- ***-ms-* for *-mps-***: 9:30 *cōmsit* (`comsit → compsit`, if the rules take it as they took *sumsit → sumpsit*).

**For Nathan** (raised by readers, not decided here):
- 7:16 *væniitque*, 7:18 *vænīrent* (*vēneō*, be sold). The settled text has *vēneant*. If the edition writes
  *vēn-*, the rules need `vaeniitque → veniitque` and `vaenirent → venirent` (r3).
- 9:11 *limphātus* against Gaffiot's *lymphātus* (r4).

I found no printer's slips beyond the draft fixes.
