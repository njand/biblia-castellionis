# 2 Kings 13–25: footnotes from Castellio's 1554 annotations, reader B

Proposed 2026-11-23, phase 2 of decision 85, by the method of `notes-1554-a.md` and
`../1kgs1-22-phase2/notes-1554-b.md`. Every 1554 annotation on 2 Kings 13–25 was read on the page and sorted as (a) a
deliberate departure from the Hebrew as read today, (b) a word no dictionary has, (c) commentary (no note), or (d) a
place where the annotation shows the settled text differs from what he meant. Decisions 53 and 56; brief.md, "brief
Latin footnotes"; the bar of `../ot-notes-process.md` §2–3. Chapters 1–12 are reader A's. The notes proposed from this
are in `notes-b.md`.

## 1. The annotation, sorted

| Verse (KJV) | Canvas, column | Lemma (as printed) | Summary | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 20:13 (*Quibus dēlectātus Ezechīās*) | 833, 362 | *Quibus delectatus Ezechias.)* | וישמע *malè legitur pro* וישמח, *ut historia & translatio uetus, & liber Paral. ostendit*: the Hebrew's "he listened" misread for "he rejoiced" | a in form, **with both modern Bibles** (`notes-b.md` §A) |

Notes on the reading:
- The NRSVue "Hezekiah welcomed them" (note "Gk Vg Syr: Heb *When Hezekiah heard about them*") and the BSB "And
  Hezekiah received the envoys" leave the Hebrew as he does; the Clementine Vulgate *Lætatus est*, Brenton's LXX
  *ἐχάρη*. No note.
- *liber Paral.* as printed (1573: *liber Paralipom.*, canvas 262, search API). The word he reads, וַיִּשְׂמַח, is the WLC's at
  **Isa 39:2**; 2 Chr 32:31, which tells of the Babylonian envoys, has no such clause (WLC). Whether he meant Isaiah is
  not settled here; the reference is reported as printed.
- The lemma matches the preview's words (*Quibus dēlectātus Ezechīās*). No (b) or (d).

1573 prints the annotation in the book (canvas 262, search API: *13 Quibus delectatus Ezechias.( … male legitur pro
… ,vt historia & translatio uetus liber Paralipom. ostendit.*), as for reader A's and 1 Kings'.

## 4. Castellio's *G.*, *L.* … *H.* marks in 1554, and readings checked

- **No marks** in 1554's chapters 13–25 (canvases 212–220, read at 1200 px), as none in 1738 (`../2kgs1-25-transcription.md`).
- **19:13 *rex urbis Sepharuaimi***: 1554 *Vbi ſunt rex Hemathæ, rex Arpha-\|di, rex urbis Sepharuaimi, Henæ & Auæ?*
  (canvas 216, column 408); 1573 *rex vrbis Se pharuaimi* (canvas 261, search API). 1750's *ciuitatis* is not
  Castellio's reading here; 1573's canvas 503 has the parallel *rex ciuitatis Sepharuaini* (presumably Isa 37:13;
  unverified).
- **24:15–16 *fabrisque … omnibus denique***: 1554 *omnibusq; militibus numero ſeptẽ mil-\|lium, fabriſq; & opificibus
  mille, omnibus de-\|nique quorum uires erant ad bellum idoneæ* (canvas 220, column 415, zoom `z220-24_15.jpg`); 1573
  *fabrisque & opificibus mille … 16 omnibus denique quorum vires* (canvas 265, search API). 1738 is Castellio's text;
  1750 differs.
- **20:12 *Baladam***: 1554 *miſit Berodacus Baladam, Baladanis | filius* (canvas 217, column 410, zoom
  `z217-20_12.jpg`); 1573 *Berodacus Baladam, Baladanis filius* (canvas 262, search API). Castellio's form in every
  printing seen (`names.md` call 3). His Isaiah has *Merodacus Baladan* (1573, search API; another canvas, not placed).
- **25:6, 25:20 *Reblatham***: 1554 *& Reblatham ad Babyloniæ regẽ addu-\|xerunt* and *Nabuzardan Reblatham ad Baby-\|loniæ
  regem duxit* (canvas 220, columns 415, 416); 1573 the same (canvas 265, search API). 23:33's *apud Re-|blam* is
  1554's too (canvas 219, column 414). Castellio's two forms (`names.md` draft fix 5).
- **25:23 *Netophatita***: 1554 *Saraias Thanehumethi Netophatita, &* (canvas 220, column 416, zoom `z220-25_23.jpg`);
  1573 the same (canvas 266, search API), whose 1 Chr 11:30 has *Netophatita* twice (canvas 273). (`names.md` draft fix
  4.)
- **20:13** *Quibus dele-|ctatus Ezechias, oſtendit legatis omne rerum | ſuarum promptuarium* (canvas 217, column 410):
  the text his annotation defends; *promptuarium* (1738 *promtuarium*).
- **23:29** *Aegypti re-|gi, aduerſus Aſſyriæ regem in expeditionem* (canvas 219, column 414); **25:19** *& So-|phrem
  exercitus ducem* (canvas 220, column 416; 1573 canvas 265); **25:3** *nono | die menſis* (canvas 220, column 415);
  **16:6** *recuperauit Syris, eoq; colonia Syrorũ deducta eſt* (canvas 214, column 403): as 1738 (`notes-b.md` §C, §F).
