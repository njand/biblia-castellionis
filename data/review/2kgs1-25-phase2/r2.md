- **No printer's slips** in 4–5: the draft's words agree with 1738's printed text word for word, u/v and i/j

- **5:26 *ut emās*** stands in the text as Castellio's *G. L. ut emas H.* (the Greek and the Latin add it); its note,
  if any, is for notes reader A.

- **5:12 *Quum ita conversus, discēderet succēnsēns:*** keeps 1738's comma between *conversus* and *discēderet*.
- **4:13**: 1738's footnotes *f)* (*jussit illī*: Elisha speaks through his servant) and *g)* sit in a verse left as
  reported speech; they are for the notes readers.

  appōnit* 4:43–44, and *adfuit* 4:36, *adferrī, adfundī* 4:41, *adfuerim* 5:26 unassimilated beside *aggressa* 4:27,
  *alloquitur* 4:15, 4:29, *allocūta* 4:30: the *ad-* rules again, as r1 found.

  5:15, 5:25); *Baalsālisā*.
