# r8: 2 Kings 20–22 (verse reader, 2026-11-23)

67 verses read beside the draft, the preview, the KJV, the BSB (layout) and 1738's printed text
(`text/orig/2KI.usfm`, leaves 454–455). 1738's paragraphs in 20–22: its chapter openings (20:1; its chapter 21 at our
20:21 *Postquam ad majorum*; its chapter 22 at our 21:26 *Amone*) and two indents, 20:12 *Sub id tempus* and 21:16
*Praeterea*. Rows: **27 layout** (`r8-layout.tsv`), **59 forms** (`r8-forms.tsv`). A script checked every layout
row's words and stops against the draft. Only the capitals and quotation marks differ, plus the four stop changes
named below. Every forms row's word appears exactly once in its verse's preview.

- **Quoted**:
  - 20:5–6, the Lord's charge to Isaiah (*“Revertere, … nūntiātō. Sīc dīcit Jōva, …: ‘Tuās precēs … tuēbor.’”*).
  - 20:9, Isaiah's answer (*“Hoc tibi signum …?”*).
  - 20:10, *“Facile est,” inquit Ezechīās, “…”* (1738's parentheses made commas).
  - 20:14, *“Quid dīxērunt …? aut unde …?” Cui Ezechīās: “Procul …”*.
  - 20:15, two speeches (*“Quid autem …?” “Omnia, …,” inquit, “nihil …”*).
  - 20:16, *Et Ēsajās, “Audī Jōvæ dictum,” inquit, Ezechiam alloquēns.*
  - **20:17–18**, the Lord's word in Isaiah's mouth: *“‘Scītō … rē,’ inquit Jōva. ‘Quīn et … spadōnēs.’”*. This
    nesting follows the settled 2 Sam 12:7 (*“‘Ego tē,’ inquit Jōva …, ‘…’*) and r7's 19:33.
  - 20:19, *Et Ezechīās, “Nōn recūsō, …,” inquit, “dummodo …”*. The BSB's *For he thought* is part of Castellio's
    spoken answer.
  - 21:11–15, the Lord through the prophets: *“Quandoquidem … : proptereā sīc dīcit Jōva …: ‘Ego sum … diem.’”*
  - **22:15–20, Huldah**:
    - *illa iīs sīc fāta est, “Sīc dīcit Jōva …: dīcite hominī, ā quō ad mē missī estis: sīc dīcit Jōva: ‘Ego in
      istum locum … restinguenda.’ Rēgī autem Jūdææ … ita referētis: sīc dīcit Jōva Deus Isrāēlītārum. ‘Quod ad ea
      pertinet, … exaudīvī,’ inquit Jōva. ‘Itaque … illātūrus.’” Hæc rēs postquam relāta rēgī est:*
    - Her charge (*ad mē*: to Huldah) stays in her own words. The Lord's first-person words are nested after the second
      formula, as r7's 19:6.
    - **Alternative**: the BSB opens ‘ after the first formula (*‘Dīcite hominī … restinguenda.’*).
  - **Reported**, unmarked where the BSB quotes: 20:1, 20:2–3 (the prayer, *ut recordārētur*), 20:8, 21:4, 21:7–8,
    22:3–7, 22:8, 22:9–10, 22:12–13.
- **Stops changed**: 20:10 parentheses → commas. In 20:15, 20:16 and 20:19, *X: words, inquit:* became
  *X, “Words,” inquit, “…”* (decision 65; 1 Kgs 1:42).
- **Stops kept** (judgments): 22:15's comma before Huldah's quotation (r7 made 19:20's semicolon a colon; a comma reads
  as well), and 22:18's full stop after *Isrāēlītārum* before *‘Quod* (a colon is the alternative).
- **Paragraphs**:
  - **Kept** the draft's paragraphs at 20:1, 20:12, 21:10, 22:3, 22:8 and 22:14.
  - **21:1 and 22:1** (moved chapter breaks): the draft's `\nb` becomes `\p`, as r5's 12:1 and r6's 16:1.
  - **20:20 taken out** (KJV ¶, BSB heading, no 1738 indent). 20:20–21 is 40 words and ends in the comma that runs into
    21:1, so it stays in 20:12's paragraph (207 words), as the settled 1 Kgs 14:29–31 before its moved break.
  - **21:16 added**: 1738's indent and the BSB's b m, where the narrative resumes after the oracle.
  - **21:19 moved to 21:18** (*Manassī vītā dēfūnctō …*), where Amon's sentence begins, as r6's 15:22.
  - The resulting paragraph lengths are 203 / 207 · 173 / 116 / 38 / 122 · 32 / 84 / 123 / 211 words.
  - **Alternative**: drop 21:18 and leave 21:16–26 as one paragraph of 160 words. Not added: the KJV's ¶ at 20:8,
    20:14, 21:17, 21:23 and 22:15.
  - The draft's mid-verse line breaks were removed (21:1, 21:8, 21:19, 22:1, 22:8, 22:18, 22:20).
- **Poetry**: none (the BSB sets none in 20–22).
- **Capitals**: only at the starts of quotations. 1738 already capitalizes the names and adjectives here
  (*Basechatensi*, *Amorræorum*).

## Draft errors

None. The draft's words agree with 1738's printed text in 20–22, word for word with u/v and i/j folded. A search for
vowel-u-vowel, initial *u*/*I* before a vowel and *uu* found only *quum*, *suum* and *perpetuum*. 1738's margins at
20:19 *Et Ezechias* and 21:15 *quoniam me* agree with the KJV's verse starts.

## Spelling gaps (not changed)

- **Within one passage**: 20:5 *adscendēs*, but 20:8 *ascēnsūrum* (draft *adscensurum*). The preview's rules treat
  *adscens-* and *adscend-* differently. This is r1's *adscend-* question.
- **21:14 *adspernatus***: Castellio's spelling of *aspernor* (from *abs-*, not *ad-*), so the *ad-* rules don't reach
  it. Should it become *aspernātus*?
- **20:13 *promtuarium*** (*-mpt-*, already noted).
- **22:6 *latumiis*** (Gaffiot *lautumiæ*). Keep it as Castellio's?
