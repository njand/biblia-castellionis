## Draft errors

- **23:11 *Pharuarim***: 1738 prints *Pharuarim* (leaf 457, left column, line 49 in the read lines), which in 1738's
  u-for-v printing stands for *Pharvarim*. The Hebrew has בַּפַּרְוָרִים (*parwārîm*, with a waw), as Sepharvaim
  (r6's fix, §3). The Vulgate has *Pharurim*, the LXX φαρουρειμ, so Castellio's own form is a judgment. Proposed:
  `apud Pharuarim,` → `apud Pharvarim,`. My rows keep the draft's *Pharuarim* (23:11 has no layout row).
- No other: a search of 23–25 for consonant-u-vowel and initial *u*/*I* before a vowel found only Latin words
  (*fuit*, *polluit*, *mortuus*, *patruum* …). 25:17 *cubitus* (1738 *eubitus*) is already corrected in the draft.

## Spelling gaps (not changed)

- *ad-* before *s*: 23:3 *adstāns*, 25:5 *adsecūtī* keep *ad-*, while the rules give *ascendit* (23:2), *aggressus*
  (25:1) and *allocūtus* (25:28). The first two are perhaps as decided (*adstō*, like *adsistō*; *adsequor* in the
  settled *adsecūtus*). 23:16 *adferenda* is r1's *adfer-* question.
- 25:19 *adseclis* → *adseculīs* by the approved *adsecul-* rule: right.

## For the notes readers

No footnotes, asterisks or marks of Castellio's in 23–25 (`../2kgs1-25-transcription.md`). The wording doubt at
**24:15–16**: 1738 *fabrīsque et opificibus mīlle, omnibus dēnique, quōrum …*; 1750 *fabrīs … omnibusque dēnique*.
1738 reads well as it stands: *-que* carries on 24:15–16's list of ablatives (*et Joachīne rēge, … omnibusque
mīlitibus …, fabrīsque …*), and *omnibus dēnique* sums it up. I would keep 1738.
