## *Ezechīās*: decided for the book

**Ezechīās** (the preview's): the settled Matthew 1:9–10 has *Achazus Ezecīam. / Ezecīās Manassem.* (Nathan's
macrons, long *ī*, the Gospel spelling without *h*), W *Ezechīās*, and the same Greek *-ίας* names are settled long
throughout: *Ozīās*, *Jōsīās*, *Zacharīās*, *Ēlīās*. Against: M *Ezechiās* and Gaffiot *Ezĕchĭās*; but the settled
*Āzariās* (Gaffiot *Āzarĭās*) is the exception, its Hebrew having no vowel there (עֲזַרְיָה), while Hezekiah's
(חִזְקִיָּה) has one, as Uzziah's and Josiah's. So *Ezechīās, -īæ, -īam, -īā*; my rows add the accusative the
preview left unmarked (18:17, 18:32, 18:37, 19:9) and r6's 16:20 *Ezechīās* stands.

## 19:5 *Ezechiani*: not a misprint (draft fix §1, 4, to reverse)

1738 prints *Hæc postquam Ezechiani Esaiæ retulerunt* (leaf 452, second column: *-ani* plain, beside the same
page's 18:37 *Ezechiam, scissis vestimentis*, which ends in *m*). I think the word is Castellio's: *Ezechiānī*,
Hezekiah's men, the subject of *retulērunt* (the Hebrew and KJV *the servants of king Hezekiah came to Isaiah*), as
his *Ambriānī* (1 Kgs), *Abnēriānōrum*, *Adarezeriānī* (2 Sam), *Abīezeriānī* (Judg). With *Ezechiam* the
sentence has two objects (*hæc*, *Ezechiam*) and no subject. 1750's OCR also reads *Ezechiani* here (and 1738's OCR
the same words in Isa 37:5), but both OCRs also read 18:37's *Ezechiam* as *Ezechiani*, so they prove nothing; the
page image is the evidence. Proposed:
- `2Kgs.19.5` la: `postquam Ezechiam Esajae` → `postquam Ezechiani Esajae` (macrons *Ezechīānī*, the stem as
  decided above; no forms row written while the draft has *Ezechiam*). The transcription's misprint list
  (`2kgs1-25-transcription.md`) would drop 19:5. My rows do not touch 19:5.
