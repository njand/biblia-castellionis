# 2 Kings 1–12: footnotes from Castellio's 1554 annotations, reader A

Proposed 2026-11-23, phase 2 of decision 85, by the method of `../1kgs1-22-phase2/notes-1554-a.md` (and
`../2sam1-24-phase2/notes-1554-a.md`, `../ruth-notes-1554.md`). Every 1554 annotation on 2 Kings 1–12 was read on the
page and sorted as (a) a deliberate departure from the Hebrew as read today, (b) a word no dictionary has, (c)
commentary (no note), or (d) a place where the annotation shows the settled text differs from what he meant. Decisions
53 and 56; brief.md, "brief Latin footnotes"; the bar of `../ot-notes-process.md` §2–3. Chapters 13–25 are reader B's.
The notes proposed from this are in `notes-a.md` (reader A, 2026-11-23).

**Where the annotations are** (MDZ bsb10196330, read 2026-11-23). Canvas 833 (pages 361–362): the left column ends 1
Kings' annotations (*EX CAP. XXII.*, 22:30); the right column (page 362) opens ***IN IIII. LIB. REGVM.*** and holds
all of them: *EX CAP. III.* (one), *EX CAP. IIII.* (one), *EX CAP. VI.* (two), *EX CAP. XX.* (one, reader B's), then,
two-thirds down, ***IN LIBROS PARALIPOMENON.*** with *EX CAP. II.* So **the annotations on 2 Kings are five, all in
column 362**, about a third of a column, with no preface and no woodcut; four are on chapters 1–12. Read on the 1200-px
page `c833-full.jpg` (cached) and the zooms `z833-2k3-6.jpg` (the heading to 6:25) and `z833-2k6-20.jpg` (6:33),
fetched here.

**A reader's hand.** Beside the 3:7 annotation, running into the outer margin, a manuscript note in an old hand, not
printed: *qui Eliæ manibus fundebat aquam. hoc est, qui minister fuit Eliæ* (as I read it; the abbreviations expanded),
a gloss on 3:11 (*is quī Ēlīæ manibus fundēbat aquam*). Not Castellio's; recorded only so that no later reader takes it
for an annotation.

**The 1554 text of 2 Kings 1–12** is on canvases 204–212: 1 Kings 22:51–53 (*Ochozias Achabi filius Israelitarum
regnum apud Samariam iniit anno decimoseptimo …*) at the top of the left column of canvas 204 (column 383), then *REGVM
LIB. IIII.*, *CAPVT I.* with a pictorial initial *M* (*MORTVO Achabo*); chapter 12 (*CAPVT XII.*, *AGebat Ioas annum
septimum*, the KJV's 11:21) half-way down the right column of canvas 211 (column 398); chapter 13 begins in the left
column of canvas 212 (column 399). Pages read at 1200 px: `t204.jpg`–`t206.jpg` (cached), `t207.jpg`–`t212.jpg` (fetched
here; `t213.jpg` fetched too, reader B's), with zooms `z207-5_26.jpg`, `z207-6_25.jpg`, `z209-9_16.jpg`. Canvas *n*
holds columns 2*n*−25 and 2*n*−24. Where no zoom is named, the page was read at 1200 px.

**1573** (MDZ bsb10141277): through the search API only (OCR snippets); no page image read.

**Quotations** follow the page: u/v and i/j as printed, a tilde for a nasal, *s* for ſ, *q;* for the *-que* sign.

## 3. The 1554 text's own margins, and 1738's footnotes

1738 prints five lettered footnotes in chapters 1–12 (`%` rows of `data/witnesses/read-1738/2Kgs1-25.tsv`). **Three are
1554's marginal glosses; the two at 4:13 are not in 1554**:

| Verse | 1738 (`read-1738/2Kgs1-25.tsv`) | 1554 margin (canvas, column) | 1573 (search API) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2:12 | *d) columen atque præsidium.* (leaf 430) | *a columen \| atq; præſi-\|dium.* beside *currus & e-\|quites*, under the side-head *Elias in cœlum raptus. Eccl.48* (204, 384) | canvas 249: *a columen … atq; præſidi[um]* (OCR, the gloss run into the text) |
| 3:14 | *e) administro.* (leaf 431) | *a admini-\|ſtro.* beside *cui ego ᵃ appareo* (205, 385) | not found (queried *administro*: one hit, canvas 216, another book) |
| 4:13 | *f) Elisæus eius linguæ ignarus, vtebatur interprete famulo.* (leaf 433) | **none**: the margin of *iuſſit illi ut ex ea quæreret* (206, 387) is empty | probably: canvas 250's OCR has *… cubaret, gnarus, vte- 12 iuſsit Gihezi famulo* (fragments of the gloss read into the text; *ignarus* and *interprete* not found as words there) |
| 4:13 | *g) nihil foris cum quoquam negotii habere.* (leaf 433) | **none** (206, 387) | probably: canvas 250's OCR *Illa reſpondet, ſe inter & nihil foris* (the gloss's first words beside *inter ſuos ſe continere*; *negotii* not found) |
| 9:11 | *b) insanus.* (leaf 439) | *a inſanus* beside *ᵃ lim-\|phatus* (209, 394) | not found (*insanus*: hits in Proverbs only) |

So 4:13's two glosses were added after 1554, most likely in 1573, as 1 Kings 15:10 *auia* (1573, not 1554); the 1573
attribution rests on OCR fragments and is **unverified** (no 1573 page image read). The other margins of canvases
204–212 are side-heads (*Ochozias ægrotus Beelzebubum consulit*, *Eliſæus aquæ medetur*, *Pueri Eliſeum deridentes, ab
urſis diſcerpti*, *Samariæ fames tãta, ut matres filios comederent* …) and cross-references (*Eccl.48*, *1.Mac.2*,
*1.Paral.21*, *2.Paral.22*, *3.Reg.19*, *3.Reg.21*, *3.Reg.16*, *2.Paral.23*); I saw no other lettered gloss in these
chapters that 1738 lacks. None of the five meets the bar (`notes-a.md` §D).

## 4. Castellio's *G.*, *L.* … *H.* mark in 1554, and readings checked

- **5:26** *Scilicet tempus | eſt accipiendi argentum? accipiendi ueſtes, G. | L.ut emas H.oleas, uineas, balantes,
  boues, ſer-|uos aut ancillas?* (canvas 207, column 389, zoom `z207-5_26.jpg`; the *L.* at the line's start, read on
  the 1200-px page): the mark is Castellio's own, from 1554 at the latest; 1573 has it (canvas 252, search API).
- **9:16 *decubabat***: 1554 *Ipſe conſcenſo curru, | Iezrael petit, ubi Ioramus decubabat, ad quẽ | uiſendum uenerat
  Ochozias Iudæę rex.* (canvas 209, column 394, zoom `z209-9_16.jpg`); 1573 *vbi Ioramus decubabat* (canvas 254, search
  API). Castellio's word in every printing seen: *dēcubō*, not a misprint (as reader r4 and the draft fixes §1.7).
- **6:33** *ecce deſcendit ad eum nuncius, quem | ſubſecutus rex, ita loquitur* (canvas 208, column 391): the addition
  his annotation defends.
- **3:27** *Hac tanta atrocitate permoti | Iſraelitæ, ab eo diſcedunt* (canvas 205, column 386): as 1738.
- **8:16 revised after 1554**: 1554 *Anno autem quinto Iorami Achabi filij Iſ-|raelitarum regis, regnãte in Iudæa
  Ioſaphato, | Iudææ regnum inijt Ioramus* (canvas 209, column 393), "while Jehoshaphat was reigning in Judah"; 1573
  (canvas 254, search API: *cum regnaſſet in Iudæa Ioſaphatus*) and 1738 (*quum regnasset*), "after Jehoshaphat had
  reigned". The Hebrew's וִיהוֹשָׁפָט מֶלֶךְ יְהוּדָה is the old chronological knot (the NRSVue drops it); the later wording avoids
  making the two reigns overlap. By Castellio or a later editor is unknown (1556 not checked).
- **10:30 *trinepotes***: 1554 the same (canvas 211, column 397); 1573's 15:12 *trinepotes … id quod euenit* (canvas
  258, search API).
- **12:20** *Iozabaro Semaæ filio, & Iozabado Someris filio* (canvas 212, column 399); 1573 *Iozabaro Semanæ* (canvas
  257, search API; *Semanæ* perhaps the OCR's).
- Also seen in passing: 3:26 *ſeptingentis*, 5:5 *decem argenti talenta, & ſex aureorum millia*, 8:21 *Itaq; fugerunt
  milites domum*, 9:27 *etiam feriri iuſsit in curru*, 10:1 *ad principes Iezraelenſes ſenatores*: as 1738.

## For `docs/editions.md` (added by this reader in "2 Kings in 1554 and 1573")

- 2 Kings in 1554: *REGVM LIB. IIII.* on canvas 204, column 383, after 1 Kings 22:51–53; chapters 1–12 on canvases
  204–212 (chapter 12 at the KJV's 11:21, column 398).
- The annotations *IN IIII. LIB. REGVM*: five, all in canvas 833, column 362 (3:7, 4:37, 6:25, 6:33, 20:13); the
  Chronicles annotations follow in the same column. 1738's five footnote-less asterisks stand at their lemmas.
- 6:33 *quem subsecutus rex*: an addition from Josephus, by Castellio's own statement.
- 8:16 *regnante* (1554) → *cum regnasset* (1573, 1738).
- 1738's 4:13 glosses are not in 1554; the 1573 OCR suggests they are 1573's (unverified).
- A manuscript gloss on 3:11 in the MDZ copy's annotation pages (canvas 833).
