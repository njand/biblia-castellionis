# 1 Samuel 1–31, phase 2: the readers' and proofreaders' notes

Eight verse readers (1–3, 4–7, 8–10, 11–14, 15–17, 18–20, 21–25, 26–31), a names reader and two notes readers (1–15,
16–31), 2026-11-20, under one brief (Ruth's, Judges' and Deuteronomy's rules; scratch `1sam2/brief.md`, summarized in
`1sam1-31-edition.md`); then four proofreaders (1–8, 9–16, 17–24, 25–31) who had not seen the readers' reasons. Their rows
are in `data/decisions/1Sam.layout.tsv` and `1Sam.forms.tsv`; the names reader's table is `1sam1-31-names.md`; the notes
readers' files are `1sam-notes-a.md`, `-b.md`, `1sam-notes-1554-a.md`, `-b.md`. Internal notes, condensed from their
reports by Claude; where a call was changed after them, it says so.

## r1: 1 Samuel 1–3 (51 layout, 40 forms; 0 errors)

- **Quoted**: Elkanah 1:8, Hannah's vow 1:11, Eli and Hannah 1:14–18, Hannah 1:22, 1:26–28; Hannah's song 2:1–10 (opened
  after *supplicāvit:*, closed at *cornū attollet*); Eli 2:23–25; the man of God 2:27–36 in “…” with God's words in ‘…’
  (as the settled Josh 24:2), reopened *“‘Scītō* at the 2:31 paragraph (Acts 2:17, Judg 9:16); the night calls 3:4–6,
  Eli 3:9 (the words he gives Samuel in ‘…’), God 3:11–14, 3:16–18. *X: words, inquit* colons made commas (1:17, 1:18,
  3:10, 3:11, 3:18); the parentheses *(inquit virō suō)* 1:22 and *(inquit Jōva Deus …)* 2:30 made commas.
- **Reported, unmarked**: 1:20, 1:23, 2:15–16, 2:20, the plea inside 2:36, 3:8, 3:14.
- **Paragraphs** added 1:4, 1:21, 1:24 (1738's indent at 1:23b moved to the next verse start), 2:11, 2:18, 2:22, 2:31,
  3:10 (length); none per speaker.
- **Poetry**: Hannah's song 2:1–10 as verse, opening mid-verse after *Et Anna hīs verbīs supplicāvit:* (as Ruth 1:16),
  on the BSB's lines, q1/q2 only (as Judges 5); the Greek-sourced lines inside 2:8 set the same way. 2:30 prose.
- **Capitals**: *Isrāēlītic-* 2:28, 2:29; *Ēliānæ* 3:14; 1:13 a colon before the main clause a comma.
- **Forms**: *Jōvā* ablatives; *sēde* 1:9 (noun), *pōta* 1:15, *vīsa* 2:17, *superbē*, *cōnātūs* 2:3, *tribūs* 2:28,
  *domūs* 2:31, *coercuerit* 3:13 (hiatus).
- **Doubts**: 1:20 *a lova* (1738's *loua*): added to the draft fixes (§1.14). 2:15 *sumturum*: now a spelling row
  (*sūmptūrum*). 1:24 *dēliculum* (Gaffiot *dēliculus*) left.

## Proofreaders

Four proofreaders read every verse of the preview against the BSB, the Vulgate, the Hebrew and 1738, and compared the
preview's words with 1738's word by word: every difference is an approved spelling rule or a listed draft fix; no
omission or misreading found. Their rows (notes "proofread:") were merged over the readers'.

- **p1 (1–8)**: 3 forms: 1:20 *ā Jōvā*; **2:10 *Jōva*** (a names row had made the subject of *jūdicābit* an ablative);
  8:13 *pistrīcēs*. Pointed out 2:5 *eōusque* (decision 80: *eō usque* apart; draft fixes §5). Left: 6:17 *penderunt*,
  1:24 *dēliculum*, 8:15 *decimābit* beside 8:17 *decumābit* (both 1738's).
- **p2 (9–16)**: 2 layout (10:12, 15:15: the colon after the speaker a comma before *inquit*, as 60 of the book's 69
  such quotations), 5 forms (13:8 *exercitus*, 13:13 and 14:43 *fēcerīs*, 14:17 *Factāque*, 15:2 *īnfestāverint*:
  with it every *īnfēst-* in the book made *īnfest-*, the settled Num *īnfestārent*). Spelling gaps found (*adscēnsū*,
  *adscend-*, *petiēruntque*): rules added. Left: 10:5 *cytharā* beside 16:16 *cithar-*, 14:20 *seipsōs*, 9:27
  *paulīsper*. 10:25 *populō* kept.
- **p3 (17–24)**: 1 layout (23:22, the comma before the parenthesis dropped), 3 forms (23:11 *estne*, 24:16 *ne* the
  interrogative, 23:28 *dirēmptōria*). Spelling gaps (*intelligatque*, *adseclārum*, *pēnulæ*, *diremtoria*): rules
  added; 19:4 *Saūlō* (the draft fix's word had missed the names marking): row added. 22:11–21 left at 255 words.
  Left: 18:26 *quō*, 20:19 *ad summum ter*, 21:3 *aliquot quīnque pānēs*, 24:10 *fēcerat*.
- **p4 (25–31)**: 1 layout (27:7 a paragraph where Castellio's sentence starts; chapter 27 was 245 words in one), 3
  forms (30:10 *cis*, the preposition, which a names row had marked *Cīs* as Kish; 25:30 *fueris* future perfect; 25:31
  *memineris*, a judgment). Reader 8's stale *sūmserat* row dropped. 30:23 *nōs* kept.
