## A. Notes where the Greek text differs (the modern editions against the TR)

**Decision:** accept (Nathan, 2026-11-17: "Apply them")

1. **1:18** *mentis*: TR τῆς διανοίας, "of the understanding"; SBLGNT (and RP) τῆς καρδίας (NRSVue "the eyes of your heart"; the Vulgate *cordis*). The TR alone.
   **Apply:** `Eph.1.18` la: `Illūstrātīs vestræ mentis oculīs,` → `Illūstrātīs vestræ mentis\f + \fr 1:18 \ft Cōdicēs antīquiōrēs habent τῆς καρδίας, ‘cordis’.\f* oculīs,`
2. **3:9** *mystēriī commūniō*: TR ἡ κοινωνία, "fellowship"; SBLGNT (and RP) ἡ οἰκονομία (NRSVue "the plan of the mystery"). The TR alone.
   **Apply:** `Eph.3.9` la: `quæ sit mystēriī commūniō,` → `quæ sit mystēriī commūniō\f + \fr 3:9 \ft Cōdicēs antīquiōrēs habent ἡ οἰκονομία, ‘dispēnsātiō’.\f*,`
3. **5:21** *Deī metū*: TR ἐν φόβῳ Θεοῦ; SBLGNT (and RP, NA28) ἐν φόβῳ Χριστοῦ (NRSVue "out of reverence for Christ"). The TR alone.
   **Apply:** `Eph.5.21` la: `cum Deī metū.` → `cum Deī metū\f + \fr 5:21 \ft Cōdicēs antīquiōrēs habent ἐν φόβῳ Χριστοῦ, ‘cum Chrīstī metū’.\f*.`

## C. Brackets: words the modern editions leave out

**Decision:** accept (Nathan, 2026-11-17: "Apply them")

In each, the NRSVue lacks the words. Items 7 and 8 are words no Greek has.

4. **3:9** *per Jēsum Chrīstum*: TR διὰ Ἰησοῦ Χριστοῦ (NRSVue "God, who created all things").
   **Apply:** `Eph.3.9` la: `omnia per Jēsum Chrīstum condidit` → `omnia [per Jēsum Chrīstum] condidit`
5. **3:14** *Dominī nostrī Jēsū Chrīstī*: TR τοῦ Κυρίου ἡμῶν Ἰησοῦ Χριστοῦ (NRSVue footnote "Other ancient authorities add *of our Lord Jesus Christ*").
   **Apply:** `Eph.3.14` la: `ad Patrem Dominī nostrī Jēsū Chrīstī,` → `ad Patrem [Dominī nostrī Jēsū Chrīstī],`
6. **5:30** *ex ejus carne atque ossibus*: TR ἐκ τῆς σαρκὸς αὐτοῦ καὶ ἐκ τῶν ὀστέων αὐτοῦ (NRSVue footnote "Other ancient authorities add *of his flesh and of his bones*").
   **Apply:** `Eph.5.30` la: `sumus nōs, ex ejus carne atque ossibus.` → `sumus nōs[, ex ejus carne atque ossibus].`
7. **3:1** *lēgātiōne fungor*: in no Greek. The TR and SBLGNT both break off (ὑπὲρ ὑμῶν τῶν ἐθνῶν —; NRSVue "for the sake of you gentiles,"), and Castellio completes the sentence, likely from 6:20 πρεσβεύω.
   **Apply:** `Eph.3.1` la: `propter vōs aliēnigenās, lēgātiōne fungor:` → `propter vōs aliēnigenās[, lēgātiōne fungor]:`
8. **6:24** *Deī*: in no Greek (ἡ χάρις).
   **Apply:** `Eph.6.24` la: `Adsit Deī grātia` → `Adsit [Deī] grātia`

## Considered and left out

- 4:9 *prius*, 4:17 *cēterī*, 6:10 *meī frātrēs*: a word that changes nothing a reader needs.
- 5:10 *accepta Deō* for τῷ κυρίῳ: with the Vulgate; the referent is open.
- 6:10–11: a full stop that 1573 prints, for the next spot check.
