## How it was made

Seven verse readers (1–3, 4–7, 8–11, 12–14, 15–17, 18–20, 21–24) under one brief (`2sam1-24-phase2/BRIEF.md`), each
verse beside the KJV, the BSB (with its layout), the Hebrew and the printed text; a names reader across the book, making
the readers' names agree; two notes readers (1–12, 13–24: the candidates of `pipeline notes 2Sam`, the NRSVue, the LXX,
the Vulgate, 1738's footnotes and asterisks, and Castellio's 1554 text and annotations read on the page:
`notes-1554-a.md`, `-b.md`); then four proofreaders (1–6, 7–12, 13–18, 19–24; `PROOF.md`) who compared the preview with
1738 word by word and read every verse again: 17 forms rows, one paragraph, the 14:10 misreading and one spelling gap.
The ten readers were launched together at 10:42 (Nathan's exception, to use the quota before its 10:50 reset); all ten
stopped on the limit before writing anything and were resumed one at a time after it, as were the proofreaders. Their
notes: `2sam1-24-stages3-5-readers.md`; their files: `2sam1-24-phase2/`.
