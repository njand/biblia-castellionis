# *Cālēbus*: Caleb's two long vowels, by the Hebrew

Nathan, 2026-11-19: "Yes, Cālēbus" (asked: should Caleb follow the Hebrew everywhere?). The Hebrew כָּלֵב has qamets
and tsere, both long (WLC, e.g. Josh 15:13 וּלְכָלֵב, 21:12 לְכָלֵב); the Greek Χάλεβ has no long letter; Gaffiot has no
entry (`python -m pipeline latin macron Caleb`: Wiktionary only, unmarked). Numbers had made him short by Wiktionary's
bare *Caleb* (decision 52's order, the Greek and a dictionary before the Hebrew); decision 82 lets the Hebrew decide
where the Greek has no long letter to set against it. Every form in the settled text (10 words in Numbers and
Deuteronomy) respelled; Joshua's forms rows changed with them. The printed texts keep the print's *Calebus* (no marks).
