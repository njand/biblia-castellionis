## A. Notes where the Greek text differs (the modern editions against the TR)

**Decision:** accept (Nathan, 2026-11-17: "Apply them")

1. **2:2** *ut Deī Patrisque et Chrīstī mystērium percipiant*: TR τοῦ μυστηρίου τοῦ Θεοῦ καὶ Πατρὸς καὶ τοῦ Χριστοῦ; the modern editions τοῦ μυστηρίου τοῦ θεοῦ, Χριστοῦ (NRSVue "God's mystery, that is, Christ", footnote giving the TR's reading). Brackets would mislead here, so a note.
   **Apply:** `Col.2.2` la: `et Chrīstī mystērium percipiant,` → `et Chrīstī mystērium\f + \fr 2:2 \ft Cōdicēs antīquiōrēs habent τοῦ μυστηρίου τοῦ θεοῦ, Χριστοῦ, ‘mystēriī Deī, id est Chrīstī’.\f* percipiant,`
2. **3:15** *dīvīna pāx*: TR ἡ εἰρήνη τοῦ Θεοῦ; the modern editions τοῦ Χριστοῦ (NRSVue "the peace of Christ", footnote "of God"), as Rom 14:10.
   **Apply:** `Col.3.15` la: `Vigeatque dīvīna pāx in` → `Vigeatque dīvīna pāx\f + \fr 3:15 \ft Cōdicēs antīquiōrēs habent ἡ εἰρήνη τοῦ Χριστοῦ, ‘Chrīstī pāx’.\f* in`
3. **4:8** *ut vestrārum rērum statum cognōscat*: TR ἵνα γνῷ τὰ περὶ ὑμῶν, Tychicus learning their news; the modern editions ἵνα γνῶτε τὰ περὶ ἡμῶν (NRSVue "so that you may know how we are", footnote giving the TR's sense).
   **Apply:** `Col.4.8` la: `ut vestrārum rērum statum cognōscat,` → `ut vestrārum rērum statum cognōscat\f + \fr 4:8 \ft Cōdicēs antīquiōrēs habent ἵνα γνῶτε τὰ περὶ ἡμῶν, ‘ut nostrārum rērum statum cognōscātis’.\f*,`

## C. Brackets: words the modern editions leave out

**Decision:** accept (Nathan, 2026-11-17: "Apply them")

4. **1:2** *et Dominō Jēsū Chrīstō*: TR καὶ Κυρίου Ἰησοῦ Χριστοῦ. NRSVue footnote "Other ancient authorities add *and the Lord Jesus Christ*".
   **Apply:** `Col.1.2` la: `ā Deō Patre nostrō, et Dominō Jēsū Chrīstō.` → `ā Deō Patre nostrō[, et Dominō Jēsū Chrīstō].`
5. **1:14** *per ejus sanguinem*: TR διὰ τοῦ αἵματος αὐτοῦ. NRSVue footnote "Other ancient authorities add *through his blood*".
   **Apply:** `Col.1.14` la: `habēmus per ejus sanguinem, peccātōrum` → `habēmus [per ejus sanguinem,] peccātōrum`
6. **2:11** *peccātōrum*: TR τὸ σῶμα τῶν ἁμαρτιῶν τῆς σαρκός; the modern editions τὸ σῶμα τῆς σαρκός. NRSVue footnote "Other ancient authorities add *of the sins*".
   **Apply:** `Col.2.11` la: `hūmānōrum peccātōrum corpore` → `hūmānōrum [peccātōrum] corpore`
7. **4:18** *Deī* and *Āmēn*: *Deī* is in no Greek (ἡ χάρις); TR ἀμήν (NRSVue footnote "Other ancient authorities add *Amen*").
   **Apply:** `Col.4.18` la: `Adsit vōbīs Deī grātia: Āmēn.` → `Adsit vōbīs [Deī] grātia: [Āmēn.]`

## Considered and left out

- 2:18 *quæ nōn didicit* (TR ἃ μὴ ἑώρακεν; the modern editions without μή): the NRSVue footnote calls the Greek uncertain, so it is contested.
- 3:16, 3:22, 4:13: Lord for God and the like; 2:7 *in eā*: no change a reader needs.
- 1:7, 1:22, 4:12: SBLGNT and NA28 disagree, or NA28 keeps the TR's word.
- 2:22–23, 1:19: disputed construals; 4:15 *Nympham … ejus* reads either way.
