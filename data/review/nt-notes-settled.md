## A. The notes already in the text

**Decision:** accept (Nathan, 2026-11-17: "Yes, rewrite the settled notes and front matter's list of signs too")

1. **Matt 19:24, Mark 10:25, Luke 18:25** κάμηλος: the reading note, shortened to the Greek with its plain sense and
   Castellio's reading. *Doctī nostræ ætātis plērīque camēlum* (chosen 2026-11-12, `brackets-and-notes.md`) goes, since
   ‘camēlus’ now gives the sense.
   **Apply:** `Matt.19.24` la: `κάμηλος; Castelliō fūnem nauticum intellegit, doctī nostræ ætātis plērīque camēlum.` → `κάμηλος, ‘camēlus’; Castelliō fūnem nauticum intellegit.`
   **Apply:** `Mark.10.25` la: `κάμηλος; Castelliō fūnem nauticum intellegit, doctī nostræ ætātis plērīque camēlum.` → `κάμηλος, ‘camēlus’; Castelliō fūnem nauticum intellegit.`
   **Apply:** `Luke.18.25` la: `κάμηλος; Castelliō fūnem nauticum intellegit, doctī nostræ ætātis plērīque camēlum.` → `κάμηλος, ‘camēlus’; Castelliō fūnem nauticum intellegit.`
2. **Mark 1:2, 3:29, 5:1** text notes: *Græcē hodiē legitur* → *Cōdicēs antīquiōrēs habent*.
   **Apply:** `Mark.1.2` la: `Græcē hodiē legitur ἐν τῷ Ἠσαΐᾳ` → `Cōdicēs antīquiōrēs habent ἐν τῷ Ἠσαΐᾳ`
   **Apply:** `Mark.3.29` la: `Græcē hodiē legitur αἰωνίου` → `Cōdicēs antīquiōrēs habent αἰωνίου`
   **Apply:** `Mark.5.1` la: `Græcē hodiē legitur Γερασηνῶν` → `Cōdicēs antīquiōrēs habent Γερασηνῶν`
3. **Mark 1:10** εἶδεν: as Matt 3:16 (`matt-notes.md` item 14).
   **Apply:** `Mark.1.10` la: `Græcē εἶδεν sine nōmine; Castelliō Jōhannem intellegit, doctī nostræ ætātis Jēsum, quī ex aquā ascendit.` → `Græcē εἶδεν, ‘vīdit’, sine nōmine; Castelliō Jōhannem intellegit.`
4. **Jude 6, 1 John 4:4, 1 Pet 5:8** slips in Castellio's text: *Castelliōnis ēditiōnēs ‘…’ habent* → *Castelliōnis
   ēditiōnēs ‘…’*, as Matt 20:5.
   **Apply:** `Jude.1.6` la: `Castelliōnis ēditiōnēs ‘Deī’ habent.` → `Castelliōnis ēditiōnēs ‘Deī’.`
   **Apply:** `1John.4.4` la: `Castelliōnis ēditiōnēs ‘nōbīs’ habent.` → `Castelliōnis ēditiōnēs ‘nōbīs’.`
   **Apply:** `1Pet.5.8` la: `Castelliōnis ēditiōnēs ‘noster’ habent.` → `Castelliōnis ēditiōnēs ‘noster’.`

Already in the form, so unchanged: Mark 12:29 (*Græcē ὁ θεὸς ἡμῶν, ‘Deus noster’.*), 12:42 (an explanation of the
coins, not a reading) and 15:16 (*Græcē ἔσω τῆς αὐλῆς, ‘intrā ātrium’.*). The OT notes give Castellio's reasons from his
1554 annotations and keep their sentences.
