## A. Notes where the Greek text differs (the modern editions against the TR)

**Decision:** accept (Nathan, 2026-11-17: "Apply them")

1. **1:6** *abluit* and *rēgēs ac sacerdōtēs*: TR λούσαντι, "washed", and βασιλεῖς καὶ ἱερεῖς; the modern editions λύσαντι and βασιλείαν, ἱερεῖς (NRSVue "freed us from our sins … and made us a kingdom, priests", footnote "washed"). Castellio's 1:6 holds the Greek's 1:5b–6.
   **Apply:** `Rev.1.6` la: `Deō et Patrī suō fēcit,` → `Deō et Patrī suō fēcit\f + \fr 1:6 \ft Cōdicēs antīquiōrēs habent λύσαντι, ‘solvit’, et βασιλείαν, ἱερεῖς, ‘rēgnum, sacerdōtēs’.\f*,`
2. **5:10** *nōsque … rēgēs et sacerdōtēs rēgnātūrōs*: TR ἐποίησας ἡμᾶς … βασιλεῖς καὶ ἱερεῖς, καὶ βασιλεύσομεν; the modern editions ἐποίησας αὐτοὺς … βασιλείαν καὶ ἱερεῖς, with "they will reign" (NRSVue "you have made them a kingdom and priests … and they will reign on earth"); at 5:9 they lack ἡμᾶς (NRSVue "you ransomed for God saints from every tribe"). Missed by the candidates file, since RP reads αὐτούς too.
   **Apply:** `Rev.5.10` la: `rēgnātūrōs in terrā.”` → `rēgnātūrōs in terrā\f + \fr 5:10 \ft Cōdicēs antīquiōrēs habent ἐποίησας αὐτοὺς … βασιλείαν καὶ ἱερεῖς, ‘fēcistī eōs … rēgnum et sacerdōtēs’, et in 5:9 nōn habent ἡμᾶς.\f*.”`
3. **8:7** *quibus ad terram dējectīs, tertia pars arborum*: the modern editions (and RP) add καὶ τὸ τρίτον τῆς γῆς κατεκάη (NRSVue "and a third of the earth was burned up"). The TR alone.
   **Apply:** `Rev.8.7` la: `quibus ad terram dējectīs, tertia pars arborum` → `quibus ad terram dējectīs,\f + \fr 8:7 \ft Cōdicēs antīquiōrēs addunt καὶ τὸ τρίτον τῆς γῆς κατεκάη, ‘et tertia pars terræ combusta est’.\f* tertia pars arborum`
4. **8:13** *angelum*: TR ἀγγέλου, the TR alone; the modern editions and RP ἀετοῦ (NRSVue "I heard an eagle").
   **Apply:** `Rev.8.13` la: `audīvī quendam angelum volantem` → `audīvī quendam angelum\f + \fr 8:13 \ft Cōdicēs antīquiōrēs habent ἀετοῦ, ‘aquilam’.\f* volantem`
5. **9:19** *in ōre eōrum*: the modern editions (and RP) add καὶ ἐν ταῖς οὐραῖς αὐτῶν (NRSVue "in their mouths and in their tails"). The TR alone.
   **Apply:** `Rev.9.19` la: `in ōre eōrum erat: sīquidem` → `in ōre eōrum erat\f + \fr 9:19 \ft Cōdicēs antīquiōrēs addunt καὶ ἐν ταῖς οὐραῖς αὐτῶν, ‘et in caudīs eōrum’.\f*: sīquidem`
6. **11:12** *Audīvī*: the modern editions (and the later TR) ἤκουσαν, "they heard" (NRSVue "Then they heard", footnote "Other ancient authorities read *I*"). Castellio's Greek had ἤκουσα, as RP.
   **Apply:** `Rev.11.12` la: `Audīvīque vōcem magnam` → `Audīvīque\f + \fr 11:12 \ft Cōdicēs antīquiōrēs habent ἤκουσαν, ‘audīvērunt’.\f* vōcem magnam`
7. **13:1** *Ego in maris arēnā stāns*: TR (and RP) ἐστάθην, "I stood"; the modern editions ἐστάθη (12:18), the dragon (NRSVue 12:18 "Then the dragon took his stand on the sand of the seashore", footnote "Then I stood"). The KJV has no 12:18, so the note goes here.

8. **13:10** *Sī quis captīvōs abigit*: TR εἴ τις αἰχμαλωσίαν συνάγει, "he who leads into captivity", the TR alone; the modern editions εἴ τις εἰς αἰχμαλωσίαν (NRSVue "If you are to be taken captive, into captivity you go"). The second half is left: the NRSVue reads it with the TR.

## Considered and left out

- 1:8 *Dominus* (the modern editions κύριος ὁ θεός), 4:11 *Domine* ("our Lord and God"), 20:12 *ante Deum* ("before the throne"), 19:17, 21:7: a title, or the same scene; no change a reader needs.
- 6:17 *ejus īræ* (the modern editions αὐτῶν, NRSVue footnote "his"), 11:8 *noster*, 10:11 *ille*: a change of person or number only.
- 18:2 and 20:14: clauses the modern editions add that repeat what the verse already says.
- 2:20: either text has her teach and lead astray while the church tolerates her.
- 13:18: 666 in every text; 19:13 βεβαμμένον, 9:13 τεσσάρων, 18:3, 21:3 Θεὸς αὐτῶν: NA28 with the TR.
- 9:16 *bis mīlliēs centēna mīllia*: 200,000,000, the same as the modern δισμυριάδες μυριάδων.
- 22:1 *pūrum*, 16:5 *Domine*, 16:14 *terrārum et*, 16:17 *cælī*, 18:6 *vōs*, 22:20 *Etiam*: a word that changes nothing a reader needs.
- 12:12 *incolīs*: a bracket would leave *Væ terræ et maris* without its construction.
- 12:10, 13:8, 14:13, 16:14: where Castellio's construal is one the Greek allows.
- 14:4 *I sunt*, 14:15 *Iinmitte*, 18:21 *sacietur*: as `text/orig/REV.usfm` has 1573's text, for the next spot check (the edited text reads *Hī*, *Immitte*, *jaciētur*).
