# Spellings found in Judges: *bracchium*, *temptandōrum*, *conjugem*, *nēdum*

Proposed 2026-11-19, after Judges was settled (Nathan: "Are there any potential new spelling rules you need my feedback
on?"). The settled Judges was compared word by word with every other settled book and with Gaffiot; four spellings
no approved rule covers. Each item changes the settled text by its Apply lines; the rules go into
`data/rules/orthography.tsv` as approved, for future drafts. The printed texts (`text/orig/`) keep what the page has
(1738 *brachia*, *tentandorum*, *coniugem*, *ne dum*).

## 1. *bracchium*, not *brāchium* (12 places)

**Decision:** accept (Nathan, 2026-11-19: "Most sources say that \"bracchium\" is better than \"brāchium\".")

Gaffiot heads the word *brāchĭum*; the macronizer has *bracchium* (M, W) beside *brāchium* (W). The settled books were
split: *brāchi-* 9 times (Deuteronomy 8, Exodus 15:16, Acts 13:17) and *bracchi-* 3 times (Exod 6:6, Luke 1:51, John
12:38). Claude had recommended *brāchium*; Nathan chose *bracchium*, the spelling most sources prefer (his reading of them; the
sources were not checked here). Unmarked, as the settled *bracchium*
(the a long by position only). Judges' two (*brāchia*, *brāchiīs*) are 1738's *brachia*, *brachiis*, set back from
Wikisource's *bracchia* in stage 2 (`judg1-21-stage2.md` §2) on the expectation of a rule.

1. **Exod 15:16** *brāchiī* → *bracchiī*.
    **Apply:** `Exod.15.16` la: `brāchiī` → `bracchiī`

## 2. Judg 3:1 *tentandōrum* → *temptandōrum*

**Decision:** accept (Nathan, 2026-11-19: "You judged the other 3 clear-cut mistakes correctly")

Decision 37 (*temptō*: temptare 198 : tentare 61) lists *tentando*, *tentandos* and the rest, not this form.

1. **Judg 3:1** *tentandōrum* → *temptandōrum*.
    **Apply:** `Judg.3.1` la: `tentandōrum` → `temptandōrum`

## 3. Judg 13:11 *coniugem* → *conjugem*

**Decision:** accept (Nathan, 2026-11-19, as item 2)

The j rule missed this one word (Wikisource's spelling); Gaffiot *conjūx, conjŭgis*; the settled books *conjug-* 35 times.

1. **Judg 13:11** *coniugem* → *conjugem*.
    **Apply:** `Judg.13.11` la: `coniugem` → `conjugem`

## 4. Judg 14:17 *ne dum* → *nēdum*

**Decision:** accept (Nathan, 2026-11-19, as item 2)

Gaffiot *nēdum*, one word; the settled Gen 44:8, 1 Cor 6:3, Phlm 16 *nēdum*. 1738 and Wikisource print two words.

1. **Judg 14:17** *nē dum* → *nēdum*.
    **Apply:** `Judg.14.17` la: `nē dum` → `nēdum`
