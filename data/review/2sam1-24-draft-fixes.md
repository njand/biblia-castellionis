# 2 Samuel 1–24: 1738's slips and Castellio's marks taken out of the draft

*2026-11-21, phase 2, as `1sam1-31-draft-fixes.md`. The draft (`text/draft/2SA.usfm`) was built from the lines read on the
page, so 1738's printing slips and Castellio's *G. … H.* / *L. … H.* marks came through into the edition's starting
text. The printed text (`text/orig/2SA.usfm`) keeps both (decision 58); the edition reads the words Castellio meant and
drops the marks, keeping their words. Whether a marked passage gets a footnote is the notes pass's
(`2sam1-24-phase2/notes-a.md`, `notes-b.md`). Each slip was read on the page in phase 1 (`2sam1-24-transcription.md`).*

**Applied to the draft** under decision 30's standing rule.

## 1. Printing slips and a misreading (9)

**Decision:** accept (Claude, under the standing rule of decision 30, 2026-11-21)

1. **3:27** *Asaclis* → *Asaelis*: a *c* for *e* (seen by Claude on the page in phase 1); 1750 *Asaelis*; Asahel, as
   2:18–32 *Asael* (reader r1, names reader).
    **Apply:** `2Sam.3.27` la: `Asaclis` → `Asaelis`
2. **4:9** *adservisti* → *adseruisti*: 1738 prints *adseruisti*, the perfect of *adserō* ("set free": "who hast
   redeemed my life", Hebrew פָּדָה); the draft's u/v step made it *adservisti*, a form of no verb (reader r2's doubt).
    **Apply:** `2Sam.4.9` la: `adservisti` → `adseruisti`
3. **11:9** *Urjas* → *Urias*: 1738 *Vrjas* (a *j*, seen by Claude in phase 1); Uriah is *Urias* everywhere else.
    **Apply:** `2Sam.11.9` la: `Urjas` → `Urias`
4. **11:27** *in ma trimoniumque* → *in matrimoniumque*: a space in the word (reader r3).
    **Apply:** `2Sam.11.27` la: `in ma trimoniumque` → `in matrimoniumque`
5. **12:17** *senatcres* → *senatores*: a *c* for *o* (seen by Claude in phase 1).
    **Apply:** `2Sam.12.17` la: `senatcres` → `senatores`
6. **12:20** *adoranit* → *adoravit*: an *n* for *u* (seen by Claude in phase 1).
    **Apply:** `2Sam.12.20` la: `adoranit` → `adoravit`
7. **12:20** *adposito.epulatus* → *adposito, epulatus*: 1738's stop mid-sentence (seen by Claude in phase 1); reader r4
   made it a comma.
    **Apply:** `2Sam.12.20` la: `adposito.epulatus` → `adposito, epulatus`
8. **20:8** *pervenernnt* → *pervenerunt*: an *n* for *u* (seen by Claude in phase 1; reader r6).
    **Apply:** `2Sam.20.8` la: `pervenernnt` → `pervenerunt`

9. **14:10** *deserto* → *deferto*: a misreading in phase 1, not a slip of 1738's. The page prints *deferto* (leaf 371,
   column 1, line 58: the *f*'s full crossbar, as *faxo* in the line, against the *ſ* of *ſolio*, *ſi* above; seen by
   Claude on proofreader p3's crop); the hOCR *deferto*; the sense *dēfertō ad mē*, "bring him to me" (Hebrew
   וַהֲבֵאתוֹ אֵלַי, Vulgate *adduc eum ad me*). Read line corrected (row 1392); the printed text in
   `2sam-orig-deferto.md`.
    **Apply:** `2Sam.14.10` la: `deserto ad me` → `deferto ad me`

16:1 *David autem. paullulum* (1738's stray stop) is reader r5's layout row, as 1 Samuel's punctuation.
