# 1 Timothy: light notes and brackets

Built 2026-11-17 by the process in `nt-notes-process.md`, with the test and wording of `nt-notes-form.md`: "the modern
editions" means SBLGNT and NA28, against the TR.

**Sources read:** `1tim-notes-candidates.md` (`python -m pipeline notes 1Tim`); the whole letter, read verse by verse
against the TR, SBLGNT, BSB, WEB, the Vulgate and 1573 as printed, with every line of the SBLGNT apparatus (one
reader for 1–2 Timothy); the NRSVue (Bible Gateway, read 2026-11-17) for every verse below: it reads with the modern
text against Castellio, and none of its footnotes gives his sense as an alternative. No word of the edited text
differs from 1573 in more than spelling.

## A. Notes where the Greek text differs (the modern editions against the TR)

**Decision:** accept (Nathan, 2026-11-17: "Apply them")

1. **3:16** *Deus appāruit*: TR Θεὸς ἐφανερώθη; the modern editions ὅς (NRSVue "He was revealed in flesh", footnote "Gk *Who*; other ancient authorities read *God* or *Which*").
   **Apply:** `1Tim.3.16` la: `Deus appāruit in carne,` → `Deus\f + \fr 3:16 \ft Cōdicēs antīquiōrēs habent ὃς ἐφανερώθη, ‘quī appāruit’.\f* appāruit in carne,`

## B. Notes where Castellio's Latin says what the Greek does not

**Decision:** accept (Nathan, 2026-11-17: "Apply them")

2. **5:4** *doceat*: the Greek (TR and SBLGNT) μανθανέτωσαν, "let them learn" (NRSVue "they should first learn their religious duty"; the Vulgate *discat*). Castellio has the widow teach her household.
   **Apply:** `1Tim.5.4` la: `doceat in prīmīs` → `doceat\f + \fr 5:4 \ft Græcē μανθανέτωσαν, ‘discant’.\f* in prīmīs`

## C. Brackets: words the modern editions leave out

**Decision:** accept (Nathan, 2026-11-17: "Apply them")

3. **1:17** *sapientī*: TR μόνῳ σοφῷ Θεῷ. NRSVue "the only God".
   **Apply:** `1Tim.1.17` la: `sōlī sapientī Deō` → `sōlī [sapientī] Deō`
4. **2:7** *per Chrīstum*: TR ἐν Χριστῷ. NRSVue footnote "Other ancient authorities add *in Christ*".
   **Apply:** `1Tim.2.7` la: `dīcō per Chrīstum, nōn` → `dīcō [per Chrīstum], nōn`
5. **3:3** *nōn sordidus*: TR μὴ αἰσχροκερδῆ (NRSVue has "not a lover of money" for the following word).
   **Apply:** `1Tim.3.3` la: `nōn percussor, nōn sordidus, sed` → `nōn percussor, [nōn sordidus,] sed`
6. **4:12** *spīritū*: TR ἐν πνεύματι. NRSVue "in love, in faith, in purity".
   **Apply:** `1Tim.4.12` la: `cāritāte, spīritū, fidē` → `cāritāte, [spīritū,] fidē`
7. **5:16** *quis, aut, sī*: TR εἴ τις πιστὸς ἢ πιστή; the modern editions εἴ τις πιστή (NRSVue "If any believing woman", footnote giving the other readings).
   **Apply:** `1Tim.5.16` la: `Sī quis, aut, sī quæ fīdēns` → `Sī [quis, aut, sī] quæ fīdēns`
8. **6:5** *Ā tālibus abstinētō*: TR ἀφίστασο ἀπὸ τῶν τοιούτων. NRSVue footnote "Other ancient authorities add *Withdraw yourself from such people*".
   **Apply:** `1Tim.6.5` la: `habentium. Ā tālibus abstinētō.` → `habentium. [Ā tālibus abstinētō.]`
9. **6:17** *vīvente*: TR ἐν τῷ Θεῷ τῷ ζῶντι. NRSVue "but rather on God".
   **Apply:** `1Tim.6.17` la: `sed in vīvente Deō, quī` → `sed in [vīvente] Deō, quī`
10. **6:21** *Āmēn*: TR ἀμήν.
   **Apply:** `1Tim.6.21` la: `tibi grātia: Āmēn.` → `tibi grātia[: Āmēn].`

## Considered and left out

- 1:1, 1:2, 5:21 *Dominī*, *nostrō*; 5:4 *honestum est, et*: no change a reader needs.
- 1:4 οἰκοδομίαν, 6:19 ὄντως: shades.
- 2:8 *hominēs*: Castellio's word.
- 4:10: SBLGNT and NA28 disagree.
