## A. Notes where the Greek text differs (the modern editions against the TR)

**Decision:** accept (Nathan, 2026-11-17: "Apply them")

1. **12** *recipe*: TR σὺ δὲ αὐτόν … προσλαβοῦ, "receive him"; the modern editions have no verb (NRSVue "I am sending him … back to you", footnote "you; receive him"). Bracketing *recipe* would leave the Latin without a verb, so a note.
   **Apply:** `Phlm.1.12` la: `mihi intimum recipe:` → `mihi intimum recipe\f + \fr 1:12 \ft Cōdicēs antīquiōrēs nōn habent προσλαβοῦ, ‘recipe’: ὃν ἀνέπεμψά σοι αὐτόν, ‘quem tibi remīsī’.\f*:`
