- **17:2–3.** The mother's blessing, which is KJV 17:2b, opens Castellio's 17:3 (*cumque illa eī ā Jōvā bene
  precārētur, reddidit*). His sentence is built that way and 1738 numbers it the same, so this is minor and not a
  misplacement.
- I found no other verse holding another verse's Latin. 20:13–14 is already explained in the decision note.
