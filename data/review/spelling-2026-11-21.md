# *pulcher*, *dījūdicō*, *sīcine*: three spellings for the whole edition

*2026-11-21.* Raised in 2 Samuel's phase 2 (`2sam1-24-edition.md` point 8); Nathan's answers the same day: "Always
'pulchritūdō' and 'pulcher'. Fix the settled books. *dījūdic-* as in Gaffiot. And I think *Sīcine* (one c) is actually
better attested. Can you check that?"

**Decision:** accept (Nathan, 2026-11-21, the words above; *sīcine* checked by Claude as below)

## The evidence

- ***pulcher***: Gaffiot *pulcher*, *pulchrĭtūdō* (the macron tool); the settled text had *pulchr-* eleven times and
  *pulcr-* once (1 Sam 9:2, 1738's *pulcritudine*). *Sepulcrum* is another word and keeps its approved rule
  (*sepulchr → sepulcr*, decision 42).
- ***dījūdicō***: Gaffiot *dījūdĭcō* (the macron tool; Wiktionary *dīiūdicō*, *dījūdicō*). The settled *dijūdic-*
  (seven words) came from the pipeline's convention that leaves a vowel before a consonantal *j* unmarked (*cujus*,
  *majōrem*); *dī-* is long by nature, so it now joins the prefixes the convention already marks (*ē-, dē-, prō-,
  trā-* before *jaciō*: `pipeline/macronize.py` `LONG_BEFORE_J`, with a test).
- ***sīcine***, one *c*: Lewis & Short has the word as *sīcĭnĕ* (29 quotations). The corpus (`pipeline latin attest`):
  *sicine* 35 times, 13 in tier 1 (Plautus 11, Catullus 3, Terence 2, Cicero 2, Livy 2, Silius 4, Quintilian 2);
  *siccine* 53 times, none in tiers 1 or 1.5: two in Erasmus and the rest Neo-Latin (Castellio three times among them),
  with one in a late text of Augustine. Gaffiot alone has *sīccĭnĕ*; Wiktionary and the Macronizer *sīcine*. So the
  double *c* is a Renaissance spelling, Castellio's own (1573 prints *Siccine* at John 18:22 and Acts 7:1), and the
  classical one is single. The settled John 18:22 already reads *Sīcine*; Acts 7:1 *Siccīne* also had the long mark on
  the wrong syllable. The printed text (`text/orig/`) keeps *Siccine* as printed.

Rules approved in `data/rules/orthography.tsv` for the books to come: stem *pulcr → pulchr*, word *siccine → sicine*.
2 Samuel's preview takes all three (11:2 and 14:25 *pulchritūdin-*, 14:17 *dījūdicandā*, 16:9 *Sīcine*).
