# 1 Kings 1–22: 1738's slips and Castellio's marks taken out of the draft

*2026-11-22, phase 2, as `2sam1-24-draft-fixes.md`. The draft (`text/draft/1KI.usfm`) was built from the lines read on
the page, so 1738's printing slips and Castellio's *G. … H.* / *L. … H.* marks came through into the edition's starting
text. The printed text (`text/orig/1KI.usfm`) keeps both (decision 58); the edition reads the words Castellio meant and
drops the marks, keeping their words. Whether a marked passage gets a footnote is the notes pass's. Each slip was read
on the page in phase 1 (`1kgs1-22-transcription.md`, the readers' notes by row).*

**Applied to the draft** under decision 30's standing rule.

## 1. Printing slips (9)

**Decision:** accept (Claude, under the standing rule of decision 30, 2026-11-22)

1. **3:1** *intera* → *interea*: a dropped *e* (reader 03, at 3×; 1750 *interea*).
    **Apply:** `1Kgs.3.1` la: `dum intera domum` → `dum interea domum`
2. **6:8** *lanuas* → *Januas*: a lower-case *l* for the capital I (reader 06, at 5×; 1750's OCR the same).
    **Apply:** `1Kgs.6.8` la: `lanuas habebant` → `Januas habebant`
3. **6:27** *in templa* → *in templo*: *medio in templo* (reader 06: the *a* is clear; 1750 *in templo*).
    **Apply:** `1Kgs.6.27` la: `medio in templa mutuo` → `medio in templo mutuo`
4. **11:33** *Mclcomum* → *Melcomum*: a *c* for *e*, or a broken *e* (reader 08, at 4×); 1738 prints *Melcomum* at 11:5,
   and 1750 here.
    **Apply:** `1Kgs.11.33` la: `Mclcomum` → `Melcomum`
5. **11:38** *tib* → *tibi*: the *i* not printed (reader 08, at 4×; 1750 *tibi tribuam*).
    **Apply:** `1Kgs.11.38` la: `Israelitasque tib tribuam` → `Israelitasque tibi tribuam`
6. **17:1** *adparco* → *adpareo*: a *c* for *e* (reader 12, at 4×; 1750 *appareo*).
    **Apply:** `1Kgs.17.1` la: `cui ego adparco` → `cui ego adpareo`
7. **20:14** *promitit* → *promittit*: one *t* (reader 13, at 3×; 1750 *promittit*).
    **Apply:** `1Kgs.20.14` la: `fore promitit Jova` → `fore promittit Jova`
8. **22:52** *Jou mque* → *Jovamque*: the *a* did not print (reader 15, at 5×; 1750 *Iouamque*); the draft's u/v step
   left *Jou*.
    **Apply:** `1Kgs.22.52` la: `Jou mque offendit` → `Jovamque offendit`
9. **18:17** *est tune is es* has no stop after *est* (reader 12, at 4×; 1750 *est:*): left to the verse reader's
   punctuation, not a word to change (*tune* is *tū-ne*).

## 2. Castellio's marks dropped, their words kept (6 verses)

**Decision:** accept (Claude, under the standing rule of decision 30, 2026-11-22)

1. **1:8** *L. et Cerethaei et Phelethaei H.*
    **Apply:** `1Kgs.1.8` la: `et Reis L. et Cerethaei` → `et Reis et Cerethaei`
    **Apply:** `1Kgs.1.8` la: `Phelethaei H. heroesque` → `Phelethaei heroesque`
2. **2:12** *G. natus annos duodecim, H.*
    **Apply:** `1Kgs.2.12` la: `consedit, G. natus` → `consedit, natus`
    **Apply:** `1Kgs.2.12` la: `duodecim, H. estque` → `duodecim, estque`
3. **2:28** *ad G. L. Solomonem H.* (the Hebrew has Absalom)
    **Apply:** `1Kgs.2.28` la: `non ad G. L. Solomonem H. inclinasset` → `non ad Solomonem inclinasset`
4. **11:5** *L. et Chamosum Moabitarum H.*
    **Apply:** `1Kgs.11.5` la: `Sidoniorum, L. et` → `Sidoniorum, et`
    **Apply:** `1Kgs.11.5` la: `Moabitarum H. deum` → `Moabitarum deum`
5. **15:6** *L. Abiamum H.* (the Hebrew has Rehoboam; 1738's footnote *f) H. Roboamum.*)
    **Apply:** `1Kgs.15.6` la: `inter L. Abiamum H. et` → `inter Abiamum et`
6. **18:22** *G. L. lucanique vates quadringenti. H.*
    **Apply:** `1Kgs.18.22` la: `quinquaginta, G. L. lucanique vates quadringenti. H.` → `quinquaginta, lucanique vates quadringenti.`

## 3. Found by the verse readers (to apply after all nine, with their rows)

**Decision:** accept (Claude, under the standing rule of decision 30, 2026-11-22)

1. **3:2** *Qui bus* → *Quibus*: 1738 divides the word at the line end with no hyphen (reader 03 in phase 1: *Qui|bus*,
   1750 *Qui-*); the draft joined the lines with a space (verse reader r2).
    **Apply:** `1Kgs.3.2` la: `Qui bus quidem` → `Quibus quidem`
2. **4:33** *Disservit … disservit* → *Disseruit … disseruit*: 1738 prints *Disseruit*, the perfect of *disserō*; the
   draft's u/v step made *disservit* (verse reader r2).
    **Apply:** `1Kgs.4.33` la: `Disservit de arboribus` → `Disseruit de arboribus`
    **Apply:** `1Kgs.4.33` la: `parietinis: disservit et` → `parietinis: disseruit et`
3. **6:34** *valuas* → *valvas*: folding doors, *valvæ*; the draft's u/v step left the u (verse reader r3).
    **Apply:** `1Kgs.6.34` la: `valuas pineas` → `valvas pineas`
4. **6:38** *at que* → *atque*: 1738 divides the word at the line end with no hyphen (reader 06 in phase 1: *at|que*,
   1750 *at-*; verse reader r3).
    **Apply:** `1Kgs.6.38` la: `est, at que ita` → `est, atque ita`
5. **7:51** *confecratum*, *repofuit* → *consecratum*, *reposuit*: a phase-1 misreading of the long *ſ* (seen by
   Claude on the page: `1kgs-orig-consecratum.md`; verse reader r3).
    **Apply:** `1Kgs.7.51` la: `Solomo, confecratum a` → `Solomo, consecratum a`
    **Apply:** `1Kgs.7.51` la: `ibique repofuit.` → `ibique reposuit.`
6. **12:21** *beniamiticam* → *benjamiticam*: the consonantal i the draft's i/j step left (verse reader r5), as the settled
   *Benjamin-*; the capital is the layout's.
    **Apply:** `1Kgs.12.21` la: `beniamiticam` → `benjamiticam`
7. **14:10** *simus* → *fimus*: 1738 prints *ſimus* (long ſ, as *ſolet* in the line: seen by Claude on leaf 414, column 1,
   line 65), a printer's slip for *fimus*, "dung" (Hebrew הַגָּלָל, "as one sweeps away dung"); verse reader r6. The
   printed text keeps *simus*.
    **Apply:** `1Kgs.14.10` la: `quam simus prorsus` → `quam fimus prorsus`
8. **17:4, 17:6** *coruis* → *corvis*: ravens, *corvus*; the draft's u/v step left the u (verse reader r7).
    **Apply:** `1Kgs.17.4` la: `Ego coruis mandabo` → `Ego corvis mandabo`
    **Apply:** `1Kgs.17.6` la: `coruis` → `corvis`
9. **22:34** *lorieam* → *loricam*: a phase-1 misreading (the page prints *lori-|cam*, seen by Claude:
   `1kgs-orig-consecratum.md` item 2; verse reader r9).
    **Apply:** `1Kgs.22.34` la: `et lorieam vulneravit` → `et loricam vulneravit`

## 4. Names brought to one form (11)

**Decision:** accept (Claude, under the standing rule of decision 30, 2026-11-22; the names reader's "Spellings to unify",
`1kgs1-22-phase2/names.md`). The printed text keeps 1738's spellings.

1. **11:26, 21:22** *Nabathi* → *Nabati*: one form within the book (12:2, 12:15, 15:1, 16:3, 16:26, 16:31, 22:52 *Nabati*).
    **Apply:** `1Kgs.11.26` la: `Jeroboamus Nabathi filius` → `Jeroboamus Nabati filius`
    **Apply:** `1Kgs.21.22` la: `Jeroboami Nabathi filii` → `Jeroboami Nabati filii`
2. **21:22** *Achiae* → *Ahiae*: Baasha's father Ahijah, *Ahiae* at 15:27 and 15:33.
    **Apply:** `1Kgs.21.22` la: `Baasae Achiae filii` → `Baasae Ahiae filii`
3. **16:1** *Ananis* → *Hananis*: one form within the book (16:7 *Hananis*), H for ḥet (חֲנָנִי) as *Ahiae*.
    **Apply:** `1Kgs.16.1` la: `Ananis filium` → `Hananis filium`
4. **17:10** *Sarephtam* → *Sarephtham*: one form within the book (17:8 *Sarephtham*).
    **Apply:** `1Kgs.17.10` la: `Sarephtam ire` → `Sarephtham ire`
5. **22:48** *Asiongaber* → *Asiongabre*: 9:26 and the settled Deut 2:8 *Asiōngābre*.
    **Apply:** `1Kgs.22.48` la: `apud Asiongaber naufragio` → `apud Asiongabre naufragio`
6. **2:26** *Anathota* → *Anathotha*: the settled Josh 21:18 *Anāthōtha* (neuter plural; here the accusative of goal).
    **Apply:** `1Kgs.2.26` la: `ut Anathota in` → `ut Anathotha in`
7. **4:9** *Bethsamem* → *Bethsemem*: the settled *Bēthsemem* (1 Sam 6:9 and four more).
    **Apply:** `1Kgs.4.9` la: `et Bethsamem,` → `et Bethsemem,`
8. **11:5, 11:7, 11:33** *Chamosum*, *Chamoso* → *Chamossum*, *Chamosso*: the settled *Chamōssī* (Num 21:29), *Chamōssus*
   (Judg 11:24).
    **Apply:** `1Kgs.11.5` la: `et Chamosum Moabitarum` → `et Chamossum Moabitarum`
    **Apply:** `1Kgs.11.7` la: `etiam Chamoso Moabitarum` → `etiam Chamosso Moabitarum`
    **Apply:** `1Kgs.11.33` la: `Sidoniorum, Chamosum Moabitarum` → `Sidoniorum, Chamossum Moabitarum`
