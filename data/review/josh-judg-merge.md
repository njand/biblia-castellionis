# Joshua and Judges brought into line after the two were settled in parallel (2026-11-19)

Judges (settled on another workstation, origin/main c562f04) and Joshua (this one) were settled the same day, each
under the decisions its own session knew. On merging, each book takes the other's approved decisions for the
settled text:

- **Joshua** takes decision 80 (*usque* with a short u, *eō usque* apart; Nathan, 2026-11-18: "Accept all four",
  `data/review/usque.md`, applied to every settled book of that day): 19 *ūsque* and one *eōusque*. The Judges
  session's other spellings (*bracchium*, *temptandōrum*, *conjugem*, *nēdum*) do not occur in Joshua.
- **Judges** takes decision 81 (*ad-* unassimilated in *adquīrō, adsequor, adsignō, adstō, adsurgō*; Nathan: "We may
  need to update already approved books based on these rules", `ad-unassimilated.md`): 18:22 *assecūtī* and 4:20 *astāret*; decision
  82's *Gaza* short (Nathan: "Apply all seven", the settled *Gāzam* included,
  `gaza-short.md`); and decision 83's *Cālēbus* (Nathan: "Yes, Cālēbus", to "Should Caleb follow the Hebrew as
  Cālēbus everywhere?", `caleb-long.md`).

The names decision 82 settled for Joshua that also occur in Judges (Zorah *Saraa*, Kedesh *Cedes*, Taanach *Thanach*)
are proposed separately, not applied here (`judg-names-after-josh.md`). `text/orig/` is not touched.
