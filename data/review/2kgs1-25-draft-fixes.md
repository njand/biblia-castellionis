# 2 Kings 1–25: 1738's slips and Castellio's mark taken out of the draft

*2026-11-23, phase 2, as `1kgs1-22-draft-fixes.md`. The draft (`text/draft/2KI.usfm`) was built from the lines read on
the page, so 1738's printing slips and Castellio's *G. L. … H.* mark came through into the edition's starting text. The
printed text (`text/orig/2KI.usfm`) keeps both (decision 58); the edition reads the words Castellio meant and drops the
mark, keeping its words. Whether the marked passage gets a footnote is the notes pass's. Each slip was read on the page
in phase 1 (`2kgs1-25-transcription.md`, § Wording doubts; the readers' notes by row).*

**Applied to the draft** under decision 30's standing rule.

## 1. Printing slips (6) and one accent

**Decision:** accept (Claude, under the standing rule of decision 30, 2026-11-23)

1. **1:1** *Moabibitæ* → *Moabitæ*: the syllable printed twice across the line end (*Moabi-/bitæ*, looked at in
   phase 1); 1738 has *Moabitarum* at 3:4, 3:7.
    **Apply:** `2Kgs.1.1` la: `Achabo Moabibitae ab` → `Achabo Moabitae ab`
2. **13:14** *eouites* → *equites*: the *q* printed without its tail (reader 06; 2:12 *currus et equites*, the same
   cry).
    **Apply:** `2Kgs.13.14` la: `currus et eovites.` → `currus et equites.`
3. **13:15** *præcepi.,* → *præcepit,*: the *t* not printed, a stop in its place (reader 06).
    **Apply:** `2Kgs.13.15` la: `ei praecepi., ut` → `ei praecepit, ut`
4. **19:5** *Ezechiani*: **withdrawn** (2026-11-23, on reader r7). First taken for a misprint of *Ezechiam* and
   applied; but *Ezechīānī*, "Hezekiah's men", is the subject of *retulerunt* (the Hebrew's "the servants of King
   Hezekiah"), as Castellio's *Ambriānī* (1 Kings), *Abnēriānī*, *Adarezeriānī* (2 Samuel); with *Ezechiam* the
   sentence has no subject. 1738 prints *Ezechiani* here (leaf 452) and *Ezechiam* at 18:37. The draft restored:
    **Apply:** `2Kgs.19.5` la: `postquam Ezechiam Esajae` → `postquam Ezechiani Esajae`
5. **25:17** *eubitus* → *cubitus*: a misprint (row 3021; the vote had *cubitus*); *duodeviginti cubitus in
   altitudinem*.
    **Apply:** `2Kgs.25.17` la: `duodeviginti eubitus in` → `duodeviginti cubitus in`
6. **8:1** *indîci* → *indici*: 1738's circumflex, which the printed text keeps (decision 67); the edition marks
   quantities with macrons (`propose`), and the draft carries no other accent.
    **Apply:** `2Kgs.8.1` la: `Nam indîci a Jova` → `Nam indici a Jova`
7. **Left as 1738 prints them**: 9:16 *decubabat* (1750 the same): not a slip but *dēcubō, -āre*, which Gaffiot has (the
   macron tool knows the verb, not the inflected form; reader r4); 4:13 *quær eret*, which the draft already joins.

## 2. Castellio's mark dropped, its words kept (1 verse)

**Decision:** accept (Claude, under the standing rule of decision 30, 2026-11-23)

1. **5:26** *G. L. ut emas H.* (Elisha to Gehazi: the Greek and the Latin add "that you might buy")
    **Apply:** `2Kgs.5.26` la: `vestes, G. L. ut emas H. oleas` → `vestes, ut emas oleas`

## 3. Found by the verse readers (to apply after all nine, with their rows)

**Decision:** accept (Claude, under the standing rule of decision 30, 2026-11-23; checked against the printed text)

1. **2:19** *quemaddum* → *quemadmodum*: 1738's slip, *quemad-/dum* across a line end without the *mo* (reader r1;
   the printed text has *quemaddum*).
    **Apply:** `2Kgs.2.19` la: `commodum, quemaddum videret` → `commodum, quemadmodum videret`
2. **3:9** *circujvissent* → *circuivissent*: the draft's own i/j error; 1738 prints *circuiuissent* (reader r1).
    **Apply:** `2Kgs.3.9` la: `iter circujvissent:` → `iter circuivissent:`
3. **3:16** *alueo* → *alveo*: the draft's u/v step left the *u* (reader r1).
    **Apply:** `2Kgs.3.16` la: `in hoc alueo` → `in hoc alveo`
4. **7:2, 7:17, 7:19** *triumuir* → *triumvir*: the draft's u/v step left the *u* (reader r3; 1738 *triumuir*).
    **Apply:** `2Kgs.7.2` la: `Tum triumuir, cujus` → `Tum triumvir, cujus`
    **Apply:** `2Kgs.7.17` la: `Triumuir autem ille` → `Triumvir autem ille`
    **Apply:** `2Kgs.7.19` la: `respondit triumuir ille` → `respondit triumvir ille`
   The same in later chapters (Claude, the draft searched for *triumuir*):
    **Apply:** `2Kgs.9.25` la: `inquit triumuiro Badacari` → `inquit triumviro Badacari`
    **Apply:** `2Kgs.10.25` la: `et triumuiris imperat` → `et triumviris imperat`
    **Apply:** `2Kgs.10.25` la: `et triumuiri.` → `et triumviri.`
    **Apply:** `2Kgs.15.25` la: `ejus triumuir, per` → `ejus triumvir, per`
5. **10:23** *Ionadabo* → *Jonadabo*: the draft's i/j step left the initial *I* (reader r4; 10:15 *Jonadabum*).
    **Apply:** `2Kgs.10.23` la: `cum Ionadabo Rechabi` → `cum Jonadabo Rechabi`
6. **11:8** *introjverit* → *introiverit*: the draft's i/j error; 1738 prints *introiuerit* (reader r5; the draft
   searched for *-jv-*: only this and 3:9).
    **Apply:** `2Kgs.11.8` la: `septum introjverit, moriatur` → `septum introiverit, moriatur`
7. **17:24, 17:31, 18:34, 19:13** *Sepharuaim-* → *Sepharvaim-*: the draft's u/v step left the *u* (reader r6; the
   draft searched for *-uaim-*).
    **Apply:** `2Kgs.17.24` la: `et Sepharuaimo coloniam` → `et Sepharvaimo coloniam`
    **Apply:** `2Kgs.17.31` la: `Sepharuaimenses Adramelecho` → `Sepharvaimenses Adramelecho`
    **Apply:** `2Kgs.17.31` la: `diis sepharuaimensibus filios` → `diis sepharvaimensibus filios`
    **Apply:** `2Kgs.18.34` la: `dii sepharuaimenses, henenses` → `dii sepharvaimenses, henenses`
    **Apply:** `2Kgs.19.13` la: `urbis Sepharuaimi, Henae` → `urbis Sepharvaimi, Henae`
8. **23:11** *Pharuarim* → *Pharvarim*: 1738's *u* for the consonant, as *Sepharuaim* (Hebrew *parwārîm*; the
   Vulgate has *Pharurim*, so a judgment; reader r9).
    **Apply:** `2Kgs.23.11` la: `apud Pharuarim,` → `apud Pharvarim,`

## 4. Names brought to one form (3; the names reader, `2kgs1-25-phase2/names.md` §1)

**Decision:** accept (Claude, under the standing rule of decision 30, as 1 Kings' §4, 2026-11-23)

1. **8:26** *Amris* → *Ambris*: Omri, the settled *Ambris, Ambrim, Ambrī* (1 Kgs 16:16–28).
    **Apply:** `2Kgs.8.26` la: `Athaliam, Amris Israelitarum` → `Athaliam, Ambris Israelitarum`
2. **14:11, 14:13** *Bethsamem* → *Bethsemem*: Beth-shemesh, the settled *Bēthsemem* (1 Sam 6:9; 1 Kgs 4:9).
    **Apply:** `2Kgs.14.11` la: `apud Bethsamem, quae` → `apud Bethsemem, quae`
    **Apply:** `2Kgs.14.13` la: `cepit apud Bethsamem Joas` → `cepit apud Bethsemem Joas`
3. **23:13** *Chamoso* → *Chamosso*: Chemosh, the settled *Chamōssō* (1 Kgs 11:7), *Chamōssī* (Num 21:29).
    **Apply:** `2Kgs.23.13` la: `et Chamoso Moabitarum` → `et Chamosso Moabitarum`

Left for Nathan (`names.md` items 4–5 and its question 3): 25:23 *Netophatita* → *Nethophatita*; 25:6, 25:20 *Reblatham*
→ *Reblam* (Castellio's transliteration of the Hebrew's "toward Riblah"); 20:12 *Baladam* → *Baladan*.
