# 2 Samuel 1–24, phase 2: the readers' and proofreaders' notes

Seven verse readers (1–3, 4–7, 8–11, 12–14, 15–17, 18–20, 21–24), a names reader and two notes readers (1–12, 13–24),
2026-11-21, under one brief (`2sam1-24-phase2/BRIEF.md`, after 1 Samuel's); then four proofreaders (1–6, 7–12, 13–18,
19–24; `PROOF.md`). Their own reports are `2sam1-24-phase2/r1.md` … `r7.md`, `names.md`, `notes-a.md`, `notes-b.md`,
`notes-1554-a.md`, `-b.md`, `p1.md` … `p4.md`; their rows `rN-*.tsv`, `names-forms.tsv`, `pN-*.tsv`, merged by Claude
into `data/decisions/2Sam.layout.tsv` and `2Sam.forms.tsv` (the names reader's rows over the verse readers', the
proofreaders' over both). Internal notes, condensed by Claude from their reports; where a call was changed after them,
it says so.

- **Quoted**: the Amalekite 1:6–10 in “…” with his and Saul's words in ‘…’; David 1:14–16, 2:5–7; Abner with Asahel and
  Joab 2:20–27; Abner 3:8–10; David 3:28–29. *(inquit …)* parentheses at 1:15, 2:21 made commas. 3:12–25 reported.
- **Paragraphs** 1:11 (length), 2:18, 2:24, 3:12, 3:14, 3:28 (1738's indents); the draft's 3:31 (mid-sentence) moved to

- **Doubts**: 3:27 *Asaclis*, 1:21 *arua*, *peremtum*, *diremti*: draft fixes and rules.

## Proofreaders

Four proofreaders read every verse of the preview against the BSB, the Vulgate, the Hebrew and 1738, and compared the
preview's words with 1738's word by word (the approved rules applied to 1738's side, u/v and i/j folded): every
difference is an approved rule or a listed draft fix, except one misreading.

- **p1 (1–6)**: 6 forms: 2:5 *sepelīverītis* (perfect subjunctive, over r1), 3:19 *eādem*, 3:22 *compositā*, 5:24
  *gressūs*, 3:26 *Bōrsirā*, 6:3 *Ozā*.
- **p2 (7–12)**: 2 forms: 11:4#2 *Ea* (the second copy nominative), 12:10 *absutūrum*. Raised *pulcritūdō*.
- **p3 (13–18)**: 5 forms: 13:5 *paret*, 13:16 *illā*, 16:9 *Sīccine*, 17:1 *lēctīs*, 18:3 *fuerimus*. **Found 14:10
  *deferto*** misread as *deserto* in phase 1 (seen by Claude: `2sam-orig-deferto.md`, draft fixes §1.9). Spelling gap
  *petiitque*: rule added. Raised *dijūdic-*.
- **p4 (19–24)**: 4 forms: 21:17 *victumque*, 23:10 *cecīdit* (from *caedō*), 23:22 *vēnit*, 24:13 *īnfēstārī*; 1 layout:
  24:11 a paragraph at 1738's indent (r7's 24:10 note on it is now out of date).
