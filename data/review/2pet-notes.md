## A. Notes where the Greek text differs (the modern editions against the TR)

**Decision:** accept (Nathan, 2026-11-17: "Apply them")

1. **3:10** *cōnflagrābunt*: TR κατακαήσεται, "will be burned up"; SBLGNT εὑρεθήσεται, NA28 οὐχ εὑρεθήσεται (a conjecture), both against the TR (NRSVue "will be disclosed", footnote "will not be found or will be burned up"). The note gives the manuscripts' reading.
   **Apply:** `2Pet.3.10` la: `et quæ sunt in eā rēs, cōnflagrābunt.` → `et quæ sunt in eā rēs, cōnflagrābunt\f + \fr 3:10 \ft Cōdicēs antīquiōrēs habent εὑρεθήσεται, ‘invenientur’.\f*.`
2. **2:2** *perniciem*: TR ταῖς ἀπωλείαις, the TR alone; the modern editions and RP ταῖς ἀσελγείαις (NRSVue "many will follow their debaucheries").
   **Apply:** `2Pet.2.2` la: `Eōrumque perniciem multī subsequentur` → `Eōrumque perniciem\f + \fr 2:2 \ft Cōdicēs antīquiōrēs habent ταῖς ἀσελγείαις, ‘libīdinēs’.\f* multī subsequentur`

## C. Brackets: words the modern editions leave out
