# Capitals for the adjectives and adverbs of peoples and places: fourteen lower-case words

*2026-11-20. Asked by Nathan ("Should we change Genesis 39:1 to use the upper case form of Ægyptius?", then "Yes,
propose them"), after 1 Samuel 30:11 *homō quīdam Ægyptius* was set with a capital.*

**The rule as the edition keeps it.** An adjective of a people or place takes a capital, like the noun: the
Deuteronomy layout rows give the reason (`Deut.6.12` *servitūtis Ægyptiæ*, "capital for the place adjective (as the
settled Exod 20:2)"; `Deut.28.60` *Ægyptiās*, "the adjective of the people"). In the settled text (`text/*.usfm`,
counted 2026-11-20): about 120 forms of *Ægypti-* with a capital (Exodus, Deuteronomy, 1 Samuel, Acts; Genesis 16:1
*Hagārem Ægyptiam*, Exod 10:12 *terram Ægyptiam*), every *Isrāēlītic-*, *Palæstīn-*, *Mōabīt-*, *Hebræ-*, *Rōmān-*, and
*Chananæ-* 132 times with a capital against one lower case. The lower-case forms are these eleven alone, in Genesis
37–50 and Exodus 31–35, with Revelation's three adverbs and adjective (below).

**Where they came from.** None was a decision: no layout row, review item or note chose the lower case (searched
2026-11-20). They kept the base text's case when Genesis 28–50 (2026-11-08) and Exodus 31–40 (2026-11-15) were
settled. 1738 itself is inconsistent (`text/orig/`): *terram Chananæam* (twice) beside *terram chananæam* (Gen 37:1),
*terram ægyptiam*, *tribu iudaica*. The printed text keeps 1738's case (decision 58); only the edition changes.

**Adverbs too** (Nathan, 2026-11-20: "Do you really propose that we handle adjectives and adverbs differently?"): no.
The dictionaries capitalize the adverbs as the adjectives (Gaffiot *Græcē, Lătīnē, Hĕbrăĭcē*; Lewis & Short *Graece,
Latine*), and so does the settled text: *Hebraicē* 33 times (John 5:2, 19:13, 19:17 … and the notes), *Græcē* 36,
*Latīnē* 8. Revelation's three lower-case words (9:11 *hebraicē … græcō sermōne*, 16:16 *hebraicē*) are the
exceptions, so they join the list (items 12–14). The forms rows `Exod.31.2` and `Exod.35.30` *jūdaicā* stay as they
are (their key ignores case; the macrons are unchanged).

**Decision:** accept all (Nathan, 2026-11-20: "Ok, go ahead and capitalize all these")

1. **Gen 37:1** *quī terram chananæam* → *Chananæam*, as *terram Chananæam* at Gen 11:31, Exod 13:5, 13:11, Lev 14:34.
    **Apply:** `Gen.37.1` la: `terram chananæam,` → `terram Chananæam,`
2. **Gen 39:1** *homō ægyptius* → *Ægyptius*, as 1 Sam 30:11 *homō quīdam Ægyptius* and Acts 21:38 *es Ægyptius*.
    **Apply:** `Gen.39.1` la: `homō ægyptius,` → `homō Ægyptius,`
3. **Gen 45:18** *ægyptia bona* → *Ægyptia*.
    **Apply:** `Gen.45.18` la: `illīs ægyptia` → `illīs Ægyptia`
4. **Gen 45:19** *ægyptiōrum vehiculōrum* → *Ægyptiōrum*.
    **Apply:** `Gen.45.19` la: `illīs ægyptiōrum` → `illīs Ægyptiōrum`
5. **Gen 45:23** *ægyptia bona* → *Ægyptia*.
    **Apply:** `Gen.45.23` la: `decem, ægyptia` → `decem, Ægyptia`
6. **Gen 47:6** *ægyptia terra* → *Ægyptia*, as Exod 10:12 *terram Ægyptiam*.
    **Apply:** `Gen.47.6` la: `tibi ægyptia` → `tibi Ægyptia`
7. **Gen 47:11** *terræ ægyptiæ* → *Ægyptiæ*.
    **Apply:** `Gen.47.11` la: `terræ ægyptiæ` → `terræ Ægyptiæ`
8. **Gen 50:7** *omnēsque ægyptiī senātōrēs* → *Ægyptiī*.
    **Apply:** `Gen.50.7` la: `omnēsque ægyptiī` → `omnēsque Ægyptiī`
9. **Exod 31:2** *ex tribū jūdaicā* → *Jūdaicā*, as *Jūdaic-* at Josh 15:1, 18:14, Judg 17:7, Acts 12:11, 26:3, Titus 1:14.
    **Apply:** `Exod.31.2` la: `tribū jūdaicā,` → `tribū Jūdaicā,`
10. **Exod 32:7** *ex ægyptiā terrā* → *Ægyptiā*.
    **Apply:** `Exod.32.7` la: `ex ægyptiā` → `ex Ægyptiā`
11. **Exod 35:30** *ex tribū jūdaicā* → *Jūdaicā*, as 31:2.
    **Apply:** `Exod.35.30` la: `tribū jūdaicā` → `tribū Jūdaicā`
12. **Rev 9:11** *hebraicē Abbadōn* → *Hebraicē*, as John 5:2, 19:13, 19:17.
    **Apply:** `Rev.9.11` la: `est hebraicē Abbadōn,` → `est Hebraicē Abbadōn,`
13. **Rev 9:11** *græcō autem sermōne* → *Græcō*, as the adjective's other capitals.
    **Apply:** `Rev.9.11` la: `græcō autem sermōne` → `Græcō autem sermōne`
14. **Rev 16:16** *quī hebraicē vocātur* → *Hebraicē*.
    **Apply:** `Rev.16.16` la: `quī hebraicē vocātur` → `quī Hebraicē vocātur`
