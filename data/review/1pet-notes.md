# 1 Peter: light notes and brackets

Built 2026-11-17 by the process in `nt-notes-process.md`, with the test and wording of `nt-notes-form.md`: "the modern
editions" means SBLGNT and NA28, against the TR.

**Sources read:** `1pet-notes-candidates.md` (`python -m pipeline notes 1Pet`); the letter, read verse by verse
against the TR, SBLGNT, BSB, WEB, the Vulgate and 1573 as printed, with every line of the SBLGNT apparatus (one reader for James and 1–2 Peter);
the NRSVue (Bible Gateway, read 2026-11-17) for every verse below: it reads with the modern text against Castellio,
and none of its footnotes gives his sense as an alternative, except where an item says so. No word of the edited
text differs from 1573 in more than spelling.

## A. Notes where the Greek text differs (the modern editions against the TR)

**Decision:** accept (Nathan, 2026-11-17: "Apply them")

1. **3:15** *Dominum Deum*: TR Κύριον δὲ τὸν Θεόν; the modern editions κύριον δὲ τὸν Χριστόν (NRSVue "sanctify Christ as Lord").
   **Apply:** `1Pet.3.15` la: `sed Dominum Deum animīs` → `sed Dominum Deum\f + \fr 3:15 \ft Cōdicēs antīquiōrēs habent κύριον δὲ τὸν Χριστόν, ‘Dominum autem Chrīstum’.\f* animīs`

## B. Notes where Castellio's Latin says what the Greek does not

**Decision:** accept (Nathan, 2026-11-17: "Apply them")

2. **3:18** *prō nōbīs*: the Greek (TR and SBLGNT) ἅπαξ περὶ ἁμαρτιῶν ἔπαθε, "suffered for sins once for all" (NRSVue; the Vulgate *pro peccatis*). *pro nobis* in 1573 and 1758: Castellio's own.
   **Apply:** `1Pet.3.18` la: `semel prō nōbīs passus est` → `semel prō nōbīs\f + \fr 3:18 \ft Græcē περὶ ἁμαρτιῶν, ‘prō peccātīs’.\f* passus est`
3. **5:2** *ovīle Chrīstī*: the Greek (TR and SBLGNT) τὸ ποίμνιον τοῦ Θεοῦ, "the flock of God" (NRSVue; the Vulgate *gregem Dei*). *ouile Christi* in 1573.
   **Apply:** `1Pet.5.2` la: `pāscite istud ovīle Chrīstī,` → `pāscite istud ovīle Chrīstī\f + \fr 5:2 \ft Græcē τὸ ποίμνιον τοῦ θεοῦ, ‘ovīle Deī’.\f*,`

## C. Brackets: words the modern editions leave out

**Decision:** accept (Nathan, 2026-11-17: "Apply them")

4. **4:14** *Per illōs quidem vituperātur, sed per vōs collaudātur*: TR κατὰ μὲν αὐτοὺς βλασφημεῖται, κατὰ δὲ ὑμᾶς δοξάζεται (NRSVue footnote "Other ancient authorities add *On their part he is blasphemed, but on your part he is glorified*").
   **Apply:** `1Pet.4.14` la: `vōs īnsidet. Per illōs quidem vituperātur, sed per vōs collaudātur.` → `vōs īnsidet. [Per illōs quidem vituperātur, sed per vōs collaudātur.]`
5. **1:22** *per Spīritum*: TR διὰ πνεύματος (NRSVue footnote "Other ancient authorities add *through the Spirit*").
   **Apply:** `1Pet.1.22` la: `vestrōs animōs, per Spīritum ad frāternum amōrem` → `vestrōs animōs, [per Spīritum] ad frāternum amōrem`
6. **1:23** *in sempiternum*: TR εἰς τὸν αἰῶνα (NRSVue "the living and enduring word of God").
   **Apply:** `1Pet.1.23` la: `et manentem in sempiternum.` → `et manentem [in sempiternum].`
7. **3:16** *ceu maleficīs*: TR ὡς κακοποιῶν (NRSVue "when you are maligned", footnote "when they malign you as evildoers").
   **Apply:** `1Pet.3.16` la: `dē vōbīs, ceu maleficīs, maledīcant` → `dē vōbīs, [ceu maleficīs,] maledīcant`
8. **4:1** *prō vōbīs*: TR ὑπὲρ ἡμῶν (NRSVue "Christ suffered in the flesh", footnote "add *for us* or *for you*").
   **Apply:** `1Pet.4.1` la: `Igitur Chrīstō prō vōbīs carne supplicium passō` → `Igitur Chrīstō [prō vōbīs] carne supplicium passō`
9. **5:11** *glōria et*: TR ἡ δόξα καὶ τὸ κράτος (NRSVue "To him be the power", footnote "glory and power").
   **Apply:** `1Pet.5.11` la: `Eī glōria et imperium` → `Eī [glōria et] imperium`
10. **5:14** *Jēsū: Āmēn*: TR ἐν Χριστῷ Ἰησοῦ. Ἀμήν (NRSVue "who are in Christ", footnote "add *Jesus* or *Amen* or *Jesus. Amen*").
   **Apply:** `1Pet.5.14` la: `quotquot estis in Chrīstō Jēsū: Āmēn.` → `quotquot estis in Chrīstō [Jēsū: Āmēn].`
11. **5:5** *obsequendō*: TR ἀλλήλοις ὑποτασσόμενοι; the modern editions ἀλλήλοις τὴν ταπεινοφροσύνην ἐγκομβώσασθε (NRSVue "clothe yourselves with humility in your dealings with one another").
   **Apply:** `1Pet.5.5` la: `et omnēs invicem obsequendō, modestiā ōrnāminī` → `et omnēs invicem [obsequendō,] modestiā ōrnāminī`
12. **5:6** *cūrātiōnis*: in no Greek (TR and SBLGNT ἐν καιρῷ; NRSVue "in due time"); the Vulgate *in tempore visitationis*. 1573 prints *curationis*.
   **Apply:** `1Pet.5.6` la: `ut vōs tempore cūrātiōnis ēvehat` → `ut vōs tempore [cūrātiōnis] ēvehat`

## Considered and left out

- 5:2 κατὰ θεόν (the modern editions add "as God would have you do it"), 3:8, 5:12: no change a reader needs.
- 1:17 *sevērissimē* for ἀπροσωπολήμπτως ("impartially"): his paraphrase of judging each by his deed.
- 5:10 *Jēsum*, 3:20 *semel*: a word that changes nothing a reader needs.
- 1:22 καθαρᾶς, 4:16 μέρει: NA28 with the TR.
- 1:4, 2:21, 2:24, 3:21, 5:8, 5:10: a change of person only.
- 5:6: 1573 as transcribed reads *(, Vt VOS tempore curationis euehat)*, for the next spot check.
