# Euangelium: εὐ- as a diphthong, not ēv- (proposal)

Proposed 2026-11-12 (Claude), at Nathan's request. Nothing is applied until the Decision line is filled in. The change
is spelling only: every *ēvangel-* in the settled text becomes *euangel-*, with the diphthong unmarked, as the edition
leaves *æ* and *eu* unmarked.

## Why

- **The edition already writes the same Greek εὐ- before a vowel as a diphthong once:** Phil 4:2, settled *Euodiam*
  (Εὐοδία), where Wikisource has *Evodiam*. Every *ēvangel-* is the other way, so the edition is inconsistent now.
- **Gaffiot's headwords write these words with u:** *ĕuangĕlĭum*, *ĕuangĕlizō*, *Ĕuŏdĭa*, *Ĕuandĕr* (beside *Ēvander*),
  and leave the diphthong eu unmarked elsewhere: *Eurōpa*, *eunūchus*, *Euphrātēs* (`python -m pipeline latin macron`).
- **Lewis & Short** (as Nathan reads it; the lookup's shortened entry does not show the remark) calls *Evander* a less
  correct spelling of *Euander*. Wiktionary, as Nathan reports, treats the eu as a diphthong.

Against: Lewis & Short's own headword is *ēvangĕlĭum*; Nathan's 2019 text has *ēvangelium*; and the edition's j/v
rule writes Greek -οι- before a vowel with a consonant (*Troja*). Settling on *Euangelium* takes the diphthong view,
as *Euodiam* already does.

## 3. The code

With the text, `pipeline/publish.py` changes too:

- the collection title in `COLLECTIONS`: *Ēvangelia* → *Euangelia*;
- the colophon credit: *Ēvangelia*, *Ēvangelium*, *Ēvangeliīs*, *Ēvangeliō* → *Euangel-*;
- the download file names (Nathan, 2026-11-12): the stem `evangelia` → `euangelia`, so `castellio-evangelia.pdf`,
  `.epub`, `-sine-macronibus.epub`, `.txt`, `.tsv` and `.json` become `castellio-euangelia…`. Links to the old
  names stop working. The site's download list is made from the files the build writes, `RELEASE.md` names only the
  whole Bible's `castellio.*` files, and `.github/workflows/release.yml` uploads by wildcard (`build/epub/*.epub`,
  `build/pdf/castellio*.pdf`, `build/txt/*`), so none of them needs changing.

The test that names the stem (`pipeline/tests/test_publish.py`, `test_collection`) and the two mentions of
`castellio-evangelia` in `HUMAN.md` change with it. `pipeline/books.py` keeps Wikisource's page names
(*Evangelium auctore Marco*), which are addresses.

Sections 2 and 3 follow the decision under section 1.
