## B. Notes where Castellio's Latin says what the Greek does not

**Decision:** accept (Nathan, 2026-11-17: "Apply them")

2. **4** *quod līberōs tuōs ex vēritāte vīvere comperī*: the Greek (TR and SBLGNT) εὕρηκα ἐκ τῶν τέκνων σου περιπατοῦντας, "to find some of your children walking in the truth" (NRSVue; the Vulgate *de filiis tuis*). Castellio says all her children; 1573, 1715, 1726 and 1750 the same.
   **Apply:** `2John.1.4` la: `quod līberōs tuōs ex` → `quod līberōs tuōs\f + \fr 1:4 \ft Græcē ἐκ τῶν τέκνων σου, ‘quōsdam ex līberīs tuīs’.\f* ex`

## C. Brackets: words the modern editions leave out

**Decision:** accept (Nathan, 2026-11-17: "Apply them")

3. **3** *Dominō*: TR παρὰ Κυρίου Ἰησοῦ Χριστοῦ (NRSVue footnote "Other ancient authorities add *the Lord*").
   **Apply:** `2John.1.3` la: `ā Dominō Jēsū Chrīstō` → `ā [Dominō] Jēsū Chrīstō`
4. **9** *Chrīstī* (the second): TR ἐν τῇ διδαχῇ τοῦ Χριστοῦ (NRSVue footnote "Other ancient authorities add *of Christ*").
   **Apply:** `2John.1.9` la: `in doctrīnā Chrīstī, is` → `in doctrīnā [Chrīstī], is`
5. **13** *Āmēn*: TR ἀμήν (NRSVue footnote "Other ancient authorities add *Amen*").
   **Apply:** `2John.1.13` la: `ēlēctæ: Āmēn.` → `ēlēctæ[: Āmēn].`

## Considered and left out

- 8 *perdāmus … accipiāmus* (the modern editions "you"): a change of person only.
- 7, 12: the same sense; 5, 6, 11: word order.
