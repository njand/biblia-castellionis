The names readers' tables are `josh1-24-names.md`. Kept as written (internal notes); where a call was changed after them, `josh1-24-stages3-5.md` says so. Reader 4's rows for 10:34–43 were re-keyed after the versification there was corrected (10:34 *expugnātā* → 10:35, 10:40 *Idemque* → 10:39, 10:41 `p` → 10:40, 10:42 *terrā* → 10:41, 10:43 *Jōvā* → 10:42).

### Quotation structure

- **1:2–9, God to Joshua**, direct (*age tū, trājice*, *ego … dōnō*, *estō*): opens at 1:2 *“Quandoquidem* after the
  narrator's *in hunc modum:* (1:1), closes at 1:9 *adfutūrus.”* No inner level.
- **1:10–11, Joshua to the officers**: reported (*mandāvit, ut …*; *trājectūrōs esse … invāsūrōs*), unmarked; the BSB's
  ‘Prepare your provisions’ has no Latin counterpart in direct form.
- **1:13–15, Joshua to the eastern tribes**, direct (*Recordāminī*): 1:12's full stop becomes a colon, opens 1:13,
  closes 1:15 *ad orientem.”*. Moses' command inside it is reported (*scīlicet, ut … trājicerētis … redīrētis*), so no
  ‘…’.
- **1:16–18, the tribes' answer**: reported throughout (*respondent … esse factūrōs*, *esse obtemperātūrōs*,
  *moritūrum*, *tantum … esset*), unmarked against the BSB's quotation.
- **2:1–7**: Joshua's order (*mandāvit, ut explōrātum īrent*), the report to the king (2:2 acc. and inf.), the king's
  order (2:3 *quī … jubērent, ut …*) and Rahab's answer (2:4–5 *respondet … vēnisse … sē nescīre*) all reported,
  unmarked.
- **2:9–13, Rahab**, direct (*sciō ego*, *jūrāte mihi*, *date*): opens 2:9 *“Sciō* after *loquitur in hunc modum:*
  (2:8), closes 2:13 *dēfendātis.”*.
- **2:14, the spies' answer**, direct (*offerimus*, *referēmus*): *respondent: “Vītam … referēmus.”*; Castellio's
  parenthesis stays inside.
- **2:16, Rahab's advice**: reported (*monuitque, ut …*), unmarked.
- **2:17–20, the spies' conditions**, direct (*nōs … līberābimur*, *tū … adligābis*): opens 2:17 *“Nōs*, closes 2:20
  *līberī.”*. 2:21 her assent is narrated (*condiciōnem accēpit*), not quoted.
- **2:24, the spies' report**: reported (*ostendunt … subjicī*), unmarked.
- **3:2–6**: the officers' command (*præcipiunt, ut …*, 3:4 *Neque enim … solitōs esse* acc. and inf.), Joshua's to the
  people (3:5 *indīxit: quoniam … esset*) and to the priests (3:6 *mandāvit, ut …*) all reported.
- **3:7–8, God to Joshua**, direct (*ego tē … facere incipiam*, *tū præcipe*): opens 3:7 *“Ego* after *dīxerat:*,
  closes 3:8 *cōnsistant.”*; the charge to the priests inside is reported (*ut … cōnsistant*).
- **3:9–13, Joshua to Israel**, direct (*adeste*, *audīte*, *sciētis*, *adhibēte*): opens 3:9 *“Adeste*, closes 3:13
  *cumulum.”*; no narrator between (the BSB's *He continued* at 3:10 has no Latin), so one quotation.

### Problems found and fixed

- **2:20 *hoc* → *hōc*** (forms). *nōs erimus ab hōc, quō tū nōs obstrīnxistī, sacrāmentō līberī*: the ablative with
  *sacrāmentō* after *ab*; the proposal had the nom./acc. *hoc*.
- **5:5 *modō* → *modo*** (forms). *quī modo arma ferre possent*: the adverb (those, that is, who could bear arms), as
  the readers' own 1:17 and 2:14 and the settled *quī modo vīcēsimum*; the proposal had the ablative of *modus*.
- **6:3–4, a stray stop** (layout). 1738 ends 6:3 at *facite.* and prints 6:4 as *Septem sacerdotibus septem tubas
  buccinas arcae praeferentibus.*: an ablative absolute standing alone without a verb. Joined to 6:3 with a comma

  with their trumpets before the ark is how the six days' rounds are made (Hebrew 6:4). The speech's marks (6:2 to 6:5)
  are unchanged.

### Calls confirmed though they looked odd

- **5:13–14** *Cui ille:* closing 5:13, then “Immō ego dux exercitūs Jōvæ nunc veniō,” inquit. — the colon and the

- **5:14** *domine* lower case (Joshua to the unknown man, Hebrew אֲדֹנִי), as the readers decided.
- **6:18** *nē, sī quid inde cēperitis, … cōnflētis* — left as the future perfect *cēperitis*. The protasis inside the
  *nē*-clause could be read as a perfect subjunctive by attraction (*cēperītis*); the settled text keeps the future
  perfect in the like case (*ut sī ad extrēmōs cælī fīnēs fueritis*), so the proposal stands. Alternative: *cēperītis*.
- **6:19** *aēnea*, **6:24** *æreīsque*: as the readers' rows and the settled *aēneum*, *æreīs*.
- **5:12** *Manna* capital: 1738's, and the settled text has *Manna* 5 to *manna* 3.
- **5:10** *pascha* lower case: the settled majority (13 to 3).
- **1:11** the semicolon before *iīs ā Jōvā Deō suō datam*, **2:1** *Quī, cum eō vēnissent: dīvertērunt*, **2:15** the

  2:24, 3:3–5, 4:1–3, 4:21–24, 5:9, 6:6–7, 6:10, 6:22.
- Paragraphs: none over 250 words; no break falls inside a sentence.
- Versification: every verse matches the KJV's (5:2–3 and 4:15–17 as the draft has them).

### Could not fix (outside my files or outside my chapters)

## Proofreader 4: Joshua 18–24

Every verse of 18:1–24:33 read whole, as Latin, beside the KJV and the BSB (the WEB for paragraphs, the Hebrew where a
form needed it), with every flag in `python check.py p4 18-24` gone through. Two rows (1 layout, 1 forms); the check
runs clean (216 verses, no ERROR).

### Problems found and fixed

- **22:21, paragraph.** 22:9–29 stood as one paragraph of 462 words (the narrative of the altar, the envoys' speech
  22:16–20 and the eastern tribes' answer 22:22–29). Split at 22:21, where the BSB and WEB both break, a new speaker
  begins and the envoys' speech has just closed (’” at 22:20): 22:9–20 is 228 words, 22:21–29 is 234 (decision 52).
  The readers' capitals in the row are kept.
- **23:16 *adōrāverītis*, not *adōrāveritis*.** The *sī* clause belongs to the indirect statement of 23:15 (*vōs hæc …
  attendite …; vidēlicet … sīc futūrum esse, ut īdem … importet …, sī … adōrāverītis: utque … pereātis*), so its verb
  is subjunctive, the perfect standing for direct speech's future perfect. The settled text has the same call
  (Deut 6:25 *ac vōs jūstitiam habitūrōs, sī cūrāverītis*). 23:12's *cohæseritis, miscueritis, habueritis* stay future
  perfect, because there the *sī* clause goes with the imperative *scītōte* and not with anything reported.

### Calls confirmed though they looked odd

- 18:8, 21:2, 22:8: the BSB quotes Joshua's charge, the Levites' request and Joshua's blessing, but Castellio reports
  each one (*ut … reverterentur: sēsē … jactūrum*; *Jōvam … præcēpisse, ut*; *monuit, ut … partīrentur*). Unmarked, rightly.
- 22:16–20: the congregation's message in single marks inside the envoys' speech, closed ’” at 22:20, is right. The
  question mark at 22:20 (*Annōn … cīvitātem?*) is right.
- 22:24–28: what the descendants might say is reported (*negent … esse*, *nihil eōs habēre*), so it has no inner marks.
- 23:2–16 and 24:2–15: the long speeches are split and reopened with “ (and “‘ at 24:8, inside God's words) without
  closing the paragraph before, as in Deut 27/31. 24:13 closes only God's words (’), and Joshua's own words go on at 24:14.
- 24:21 *At populus: “Immō Jōvæ serviēmus,” inquiunt.*: the colon frame plus *inquiunt* after the speech is Castellio's
  own doubled frame, kept. *populus … inquiunt* (a collective with a plural verb) is his too.
- 24:22: Joshua's question and *Et illī: “Testāmur.”* stay in one paragraph, since both fall in one verse.
- 24:23: Joshua speaks with no frame, in a paragraph of its own. The Hebrew has no *said he* either.
- 24:6 *mare rubrum* lower case, as the settled text (16×). 24:31 *senum* (of *senex*), not *sēnum*. 21:21 *asȳlum*
  is right. 22:20 *dēlīquisset* is right. 24:32 *quessitis* as the settled Gen 33:19. 24:32 *cesserat* is right (*cēdō, cessī*).
