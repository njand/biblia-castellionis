# Ruth 1–4, phase 1: the readers' notes

The two readers' notes as written (2026-11-19), for `ruth1-4-transcription.md`. Row numbers are those of
`data/witnesses/read-1738/Ruth1-4.voted.tsv` (and of the `row` column of `Ruth1-4.corrections.tsv`); verse numbers
are 1738's. Where Claude settled a point afterwards, the summary says so: the note letters (`m)`, `n)`, `a)`, `b)`)
and the asterisk at row 253 were taken out of the text rows (the read files carry no note marks; the notes are the
`%` rows); row 251a is written as line 103 (the hOCR's number for *impofuit.*); row 318/1/91 *ita,* is the
catchword, made a `%` row.
