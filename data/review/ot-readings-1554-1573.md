# Thirteen slips in the settled Genesis and Exodus, found by reading 1738 and confirmed in 1554 and 1573

Proposed 2026-11-14. Reading on 1738's leaves the 3,022 words its OCR missed (decision 68) found fifteen places
where 1738's word is a different word from the settled text's. Each was then read on the page images of Castellio's
own revision of **1554** (MDZ bsb10196330) and the **1573** Basel edition (bsb10141277), as Exod 18:5 and 20:24 were
(decision 64). **In thirteen of them all three printings agree against the settled text** (with Exod 22:9, found later: thirteen to change, as Gen 11:25 is a number, kept classical): the settled text took
the word from Wikisource (or your 2019 transcription, which Wikisource follows), and the slip is theirs (at Gen
11:25 they differ among themselves in spelling only). In two (Exod 6:14, 29:12) 1738 stands alone, and the settled
text is right.

The printed text (`text/orig/draft/`) already has 1738's words. These Apply lines are for the reading edition.
**Your decision.**

## The readings

1. **Gen 11:25** *annōs centum ūndēvīgintī* stays: 1554 *centum nouẽdecim* (image 18), 1573 *centũ nouendecim* (31),
   1738 *centum nouemdecim* print Castellio's *novemdecim*, but the edited text uses the classical number forms
   (Nathan, 2026-11-14: "always use the more classical number forms, i.e. undeviginti, not novemdecim"; the
   printed text keeps the print's). No Apply line.
2. **Gen 15:19** *Ceanezæōs* → 1554 *Cenezæos* (19), 1573 *Cenezæos* (33), 1738 *Cenezæos*.
   **Apply:** `Gen.15.19` la: `Ceanezæōs` → `Cenezæōs`
3. **Gen 16:4** *Hic cum Hagāre* → 1554 *Ille cum Hagare* (19), 1573 *Ille* (33), 1738 *Ille*.
   **Apply:** `Gen.16.4` la: `Hic cum Hagāre` → `Ille cum Hagāre`
4. **Gen 26:2** *ostenderō, cōnsīditō* → 1554 *consideto* (25), 1573 *consideto* (40), 1738 *consideto*: the future
   imperative of *cōnsideō* "settle, sit down" (Gaffiot *cōnsĭdĕō, sĭdēre*), not *cōnsīdō*. Marked *cōnsidētō*:
   the *ō* of *con-* long before *s*, the *i* short, the *ē* of the second conjugation.
   **Apply:** `Gen.26.2` la: `ostenderō, cōnsīditō` → `ostenderō, cōnsidētō`
5. **Gen 41:19** *nihil umquam fœdus* → 1554 *nihil unquam fœdius* (35), 1573 *fœdius* (51), 1738 *fœdius* (œ seen
   on zoomed crops): the comparative, "nothing uglier"; *fœdus* makes no sense there.
   **Apply:** `Gen.41.19` la: `nihil umquam fœdus` → `nihil umquam fœdius`
6. **Exod 3:5** *in quō stās* → 1554 *in quo tu astas* (42), 1573 *in quo tu astas* (60), 1738 *in quo tu adstas*.
   *astās* as 1554 and 1573 (and decision 37's assimilated *ad-*); Gaffiot *astō*, the *a* short.
   **Apply:** `Exod.3.5` la: `in quō stās` → `in quō tū astās`
7. **Exod 3:6** *suam abdidisset* → 1554 *suã abscondisset* (42), 1573 *suam abscondisset* (60), 1738
   *abscondisset*.
   **Apply:** `Exod.3.6` la: `suam abdidisset` → `suam abscondisset`
8. **Exod 5:16** *paleam ministrātī* → 1554 *paleã ministrari* (44), 1573 *paleam ministrari* (62), 1738
   *ministrari*: the infinitive.
   **Apply:** `Exod.5.16` la: `paleam ministrātī` → `paleam ministrārī`
9. **Exod 8:19** *ille occultāvit animō* → 1554 *ille occalluit animo* (45), 1573 *occaluit* (63), 1738
   *occalluit*: *occallēscō* "grow callous" (Gaffiot), Pharaoh's heart hardening; *occultāvit* is a slip.
   **Apply:** `Exod.8.19` la: `ille occultāvit animō` → `ille occalluit animō`
10. **Exod 12:22** *īnfīgite* → 1554 *inficite superliminaria* (47), 1573 *inficite* (66), 1738 *inficite*: *īnficiō*
    "dip, stain" (Gaffiot *īnfĭcĭō*), the lintel stained with the blood; not *īnfīgō* "fix".
    **Apply:** `Exod.12.22` la: `īnfīgite` → `īnficite`
11. **Exod 15:14** *cohorrēscunt* → 1554 *cohorrescant* (49), 1573 *cohorrescant* (68), 1738 *cohorrescant*: the
    subjunctive, beside *invādat* in the same verse.
    **Apply:** `Exod.15.14` la: `cohorrēscunt` → `cohorrēscant`
13. **Exod 21:6** *aurem fībulā perforātō* → 1554 *aurem subula perforato* (image 52), 1573 *aurem subula
    perforato* (72), 1738 *subula*: the awl (Gaffiot *sūbŭla*; Vulgate *subula*, Hebrew מַרְצֵעַ), not *fībula* "clasp".
    **Apply:** `Exod.21.6` la: `aurem fībulā perforātō` → `aurem sūbulā perforātō`
14. **Exod 29:18** *odōre dēlīniat* → 1554 *suaui odore delinit* (57), 1573 *suaui odore delinit* (82), 1738
    *delinit*: the indicative (*eumque … dēlīnit*, as *fit* before it); Gaffiot *dēlīnĭō*.
    **Apply:** `Exod.29.18` la: `odōre dēlīniat` → `odōre dēlīnit`
16. **Exod 22:9** *quæ cujus dīcātur* → 1554 *quæ cuia dicatur* (image 53, seen), 1573 *cuia* (72, seen), 1738
    *cuia* (leaf 136), 1750 OCR *cuia*: the adjective *cūjus, -a, -um* "whose" (Gaffiot *cŭjus, a, um*), feminine with
    *rēs*: "which is said to be whose". Found by the check of the 1738 route on Exodus 21–23
    (`data/review/exod21-23-ocrcheck.md`).
    **Apply:** `Exod.22.9` la: `quæ cujus dīcātur` → `quæ cuja dīcātur`
15. **Exod 29:12** *invergitō* stays: 1554 *inuergito* (57) and 1573 *in-|uergito* (82) as the settled text; 1738's
    *inspergito* is its own (kept in the printed text only). No Apply line.
12. **Exod 6:14** *Rūbēninæ* stays: 1554 and 1573 print *Rubeninæ*; 1738's *rubeniæ* is its misprint, kept in the
    printed text only. No Apply line.

**Decision:** accept all (Nathan, 2026-11-15: "Accept all of the other proposals I didn't comment on")
