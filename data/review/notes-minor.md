# Notes without plot or theological significance: a proposal to drop them

Proposed 2026-11-19 (Claude), on Nathan's standard: "Minor notes (e.g. rare names in lists) that don't really have any
plot or theological significance can be dropped. Also check previous notes to see if any should go based on this
standard." Every footnote in the settled text (`text/*.usfm`: 201 notes, 47 in the Old Testament, 154 in the New) was
read against it. A note stays where the departure changes what happens in the story, what a law requires, or what a
passage says about God, Christ or salvation, and where it glosses a word no dictionary has (the coinages *Azȳmālia*,
*Monētum*, Gen 33:19 *quessitis*). It goes where it concerns only a name, a place, a number, a date, a material, a plant,
a coin or a furnishing detail. Joshua's 13:3 is dropped from `josh-notes.md` on the same standard.

**41 notes proposed for removal** (18 Old Testament, 23 New), each with an Apply line that takes out the note and
leaves the word before it. The text itself is unchanged; the evidence for each note stays in its review file. `review
check` gives every line `ok`.

**Kept, though near the line** (listed so they can be added if Nathan disagrees):
- Exod 40:20 *vectēs āræ* for the ark: Nathan asked for this note (decision 73).
- Judg 15:19 the jawbone's tooth for the hollow at Lehi: what the miracle was.
- Gen 29:27 seven years for Leah's bridal week: the plot of Jacob's service.
- Exod 23:6 *inimīcī tuī* for "your poor": what the law protects.
- Luke 14:5 *asinus* where older manuscripts have "son": the force of the saying.
- 3 John 5 "brothers, and strangers too": the point of the praise.
- Rev 8:7 the third of the earth burned: one of the trumpet plagues, a part of the vision's scheme.
- The other New Testament notes: each touches Christ, God, the Spirit, salvation or the sense of a saying (e.g. John 1:18,
  1 Tim 3:16, Matt 24:36, Luke 2:14, Matt 19:24 *rudentem*), or is one of the six Nathan named (decision 53).

**Elsewhere:** `brief.md` (the light notes) and `ot-notes-process.md` §2 gain the standard, so later books are read by it.
