# 2 Timothy: light notes and brackets

Built 2026-11-17 by the process in `nt-notes-process.md`, with the test and wording of `nt-notes-form.md`: "the modern
editions" means SBLGNT and NA28, against the TR.

**Sources read:** `2tim-notes-candidates.md` (`python -m pipeline notes 2Tim`); the whole letter, read verse by verse
against the TR, SBLGNT, BSB, WEB, the Vulgate and 1573 as printed, with every line of the SBLGNT apparatus (one
reader for 1–2 Timothy); the NRSVue (Bible Gateway, read 2026-11-17) for every verse below: it reads with the modern
text against Castellio, and none of its footnotes gives his sense as an alternative. No word of the edited text
differs from 1573 in more than spelling.

## A. Notes where the Greek text differs (the modern editions against the TR)

**Decision:** accept (Nathan, 2026-11-17: "Apply them")

1. **4:14** *reddat eī Dominus*: TR ἀποδῴη, "may the Lord repay"; the modern editions ἀποδώσει (NRSVue "the Lord will pay him back"): a statement, not a wish.
   **Apply:** `2Tim.4.14` la: `digna ejus factīs præmia.` → `digna ejus factīs præmia\f + \fr 4:14 \ft Cōdicēs antīquiōrēs habent ἀποδώσει, ‘reddet’.\f*.`

## B. Notes where Castellio's Latin says what the Greek does not

**Decision:** accept (Nathan, 2026-11-17: "Apply them")

2. **2:6** *Labōrandum agricolæ est prius, quam frūctum percipiat*: the Greek (TR and SBLGNT) τὸν κοπιῶντα γεωργὸν δεῖ πρῶτον τῶν καρπῶν μεταλαμβάνειν, "the farmer who does the work ought to have the first share of the crops" (NRSVue). Castellio takes πρῶτον with the work: work first, fruit after.
   **Apply:** `2Tim.2.6` la: `quam frūctum percipiat.` → `quam frūctum percipiat\f + \fr 2:6 \ft Græcē τὸν κοπιῶντα γεωργὸν δεῖ πρῶτον τῶν καρπῶν μεταλαμβάνειν, ‘labōrantem agricolam oportet prīmum frūctuum participem esse’.\f*.`

## C. Brackets: words the modern editions leave out

**Decision:** accept (Nathan, 2026-11-17: "Apply them")

3. **1:11** *externārum gentium*: TR ἐθνῶν. NRSVue footnote "Other ancient authorities add *of the gentiles*".
   **Apply:** `2Tim.1.11` la: `ego externārum gentium præcō` → `ego [externārum gentium] præcō`
4. **3:4** *cæcī*: in no Greek. Castellio marked it himself (Wikisource *L. caeci et G.*; 1573 *Cæci*).
   **Apply:** `2Tim.3.4` la: `īnflātī, cæcī et voluptātis` → `īnflātī, [cæcī] et voluptātis`
5. **4:22** *Jēsūs Chrīstus* and *Āmēn*: TR ὁ Κύριος Ἰησοῦς Χριστός and ἀμήν. NRSVue "The Lord be with your spirit", footnote "add *Amen*".
   **Apply:** `2Tim.4.22` la: `Dominus Jēsūs Chrīstus adsit` → `Dominus [Jēsūs Chrīstus] adsit`
   **Apply:** `2Tim.4.22` la: `vōbīs grātia: Āmēn.` → `vōbīs grātia[: Āmēn].`

## Considered and left out

- 2:19 *Chrīstī nōmen* (the modern editions κυρίου): the same person.
- 2:3, 2:7, 4:1: shades.
- 2:14: SBLGNT and NA28 disagree.
- 2:21 *erō ūtile*: the approved *hero* → *ero* rule makes it read "I shall be useful"; for the spelling review.
