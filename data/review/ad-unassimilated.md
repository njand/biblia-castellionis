# *ad-* kept unassimilated in *adquīrō, adsequor, adsignō, adstō, adsurgō*

Nathan, 2026-11-19, on the Joshua spelling proposals (`josh1-24-rules-research.md`): "I disagree with adquiro →
acquiro, adsequor → assequor, adsigno → assigno, adsto → asto, adsurgo → assurgo based on corpus evidence. There
seems to be strong evidence to keep the d unassimilated. We may need to update already approved books based on these
rules. Remember to account for inflected forms, including participles." This narrows decision 37 (*ad-* assimilated):
these five verbs keep *ad-* in every form; the other *ad-* compounds stay as decision 37 has them. The printed texts
(`text/orig/`) keep Castellio's spelling (decision 58), *ad-* or assimilated as printed.

- **The rules** (`data/rules/orthography.tsv`): the 40 approved word rows that assimilated these verbs (*adsecutus →
  assecutus, adsignavit → assignavit, adstans → astans, adsto → asto, adsurgebat → assurgebat, adquisitus →
  acquisitus* …) set to `rejected`, with Nathan's words in their notes. Added, approved: **stem rules** that respell
  any assimilated form back, whatever its ending: *assequ- → adsequ-, assecu- → adsecu-* (*adsequor, adsecūtus*),
  *assign- → adsign-*, *assurg- → adsurg-, assurrex- → adsurrex-* (*adsurgō, adsurrēxī*), *acquir- → adquir-,
  acquisi- → adquisi-* (*adquīrō, adquīsītus*), *astab-, astan-, astat-, astit- → adst-* (*adstābat, adstāns,
  adstantibus, adstātūrus, adstitit*); and **word rows** for the forms of *adstō* that a stem would confuse with *ast*
  (but) and *Astarōth*: *astō, astās, astāre, astāret, astārent, astārem, astem, astēs, astet, astent, astāmus*.
  Checked: every word in the settled text and in Wikisource that begins with one of the stems is a form of these five
  verbs (not *acquiēscō, assideō, assūmō, asser, Assur, Astarōth*).
- **The settled text**, 111 words in 108 verses (below), respelled with their marks kept (the prefix *ad-* has no long
  vowel; *astāns → adstāns*, *assecūtus → adsecūtus*, *acquīsītus → adquīsītus*).

**Decision:** accept (Nathan, 2026-11-19: "We may need to update already approved books based on these rules")
