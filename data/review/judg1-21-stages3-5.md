## How the verses were made

Seven readers (1–3, 4–5, 6–8, 9–11, 12–16, 17–19, 20–21) under one brief (Deuteronomy's rules), each verse beside the
KJV, BSB and Hebrew; a names reader across the book; then four proofreaders (1–5, 6–9, 10–16, 17–21) read every verse
of the preview again (88 layout and 12 forms rows, each noted "proofread:"). Their notes:
`judg1-21-stages3-5-readers.md`. The `homograph` and `unknown` flags still shown are words the readers confirmed as
proposed (the unknowns are names the names reader found with no long vowel: *Gabaam*, *Maspham*, *Zebul* …).

- **Quotation marks**: Castellio reports most of what the Hebrew quotes, and reported speech is unmarked (decisions 4,
  25): all of chapters 10, 12, 17; most of 18 and 20–21; God's words at 2:20–22, 6:25–26, 7:2–10. Quoted where the Latin
  gives the words: the angel at Bochim (2:1–3), Ehud (3:19), Deborah and Barak (4:6–9), Jael (4:18, 4:22), the Song of
  Deborah (5:2–31, speech inside it in ‘…’), the angel and Gideon (6:12–23), Joash (6:31–32), the fleece (6:36–39), the
  dream (7:13–14), Gideon's cry (7:18, 7:20), Jotham (9:8–20), Gaal and Zebul (9:28–38), Jephthah's message (11:15–27,
  opened at 15 as the BSB, where Wikisource had opened it at 23), his vow and daughter (11:31–37), Manoah and the angel
  (13), Samson's riddle and its answers (14:12–18), 15:7, 15:16–18, Delilah (16), Micah and the Danites (18:24–25), the
  Levite, the old man and the householder (19), the Levite's account (20:4–7), the elders (21:20–22). *Inquit* set off
  by commas, the quotation closed and reopened (decision 65). 113 “ and 111 ”: two reopen at a new paragraph (9:16,
  11:23). Wikisource's straight quotes all replaced.
- **Poetry**: the Song of Deborah (5:2–31) on the BSB's lines; Samson's riddle and answers (14:14, 14:18), the jawbone
  song (15:16). Not Jotham's fable (point 5).
- **Paragraphs**: the BSB's (the draft lacked many of them in chapters 17–21: carried over by the readers), moved to the
  sentence start; added at new speeches and episodes; paragraphs over about 250 words split where BSB and WEB break
  (decision 52: 1:22, 3:20, 4:4, 4:12, 6:19, 8:4, 8:13, 9:16, 9:42, 11:4, 11:14, 11:23, 13:2 …).
- **Capitals**: *deus* lower case for Baal, Dagon, Chemosh (6:31, 9:27, 9:46, 11:24, 16:23); adjectives of peoples
  capitalized; the mid-sentence capitals Wikisource's line breaks had left (*Eīque*, *Simulque*, *Quī*) lowered.
- **Punctuation**: 1738's where Wikisource had broken or joined its sentences (17:2–3, 18:17–19, 19:9–11, 19:22–23,
  19:28–30, 20:4–5, 20:21–22, 20:38–39 …; point 6).
- **Forms**: chiefly the ablatives the macronizer leaves short (*terrā*, *eā*, *nūllā*, ablative absolutes) and **the
  ablative *Jōvā*** after *ā/ab*, *cōram*, in ablative absolutes (about 25 places); *vēnit*, *fūgit*, *occīdō* (kill)
  against *occidō*; *domūs*, *tribūs*, *sōlis*; perfect subjunctives (16:15 *dederīs*, *indicārīs*); the words no
  source marks (*tēmētumve*, *clītellātōrum*, *dīversātum*, *stuprandās*, *misertūrōs*, *abjectissimōs*,
  *laudābilissima*, *īnfermentātōs* …), each with its reason; 18:16 *introeunt* (a hiatus, not *œ*).
