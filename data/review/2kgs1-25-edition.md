two notes readers (1–12, 13–25; `NOTES.md`: the candidates of `pipeline notes 2Kgs`, the NRSVue, the LXX, the Vulgate,

then four proofreaders (1–6, 7–12, 13–18, 19–25; `PROOF.md`) who compared the preview with 1738 word by word and read
every verse again: 15 forms rows, five layout rows, and the spelling gaps of point 8. All one at a time, with the quota
checked before each. Their files and `STATUS.md`: `2kgs1-25-phase2/`.
