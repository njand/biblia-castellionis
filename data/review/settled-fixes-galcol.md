# Slips in the settled chapters, found while reading Galatians–Colossians

Proposed 2026-11-11. The readers of Galatians, Ephesians, Philippians and Colossians compared their words with your
settled forms (`data/gold/`) and found these markings there that the grammar of the verse rules out; each was checked
in its verse by me. Nothing is applied: these are settled chapters, so they wait for your Decision line.

**Decision:** accept all (Nathan, 2026-11-11: "Apply all as recommended")

1. **1 Cor 7:21** *sī liber fierī potes* → *līber*: "free" (ἐλεύθερος), the adjective *līber*; *liber* is "book"
   (your Luke 4:17 *liber*, rightly). Gal 3:28, Eph 6:8 and Col 3:11 write *līber* in this batch.
   **Apply:** `1Cor.7.21` la: `sī liber fierī` → `sī līber fierī`
2. **1 Cor 7:22** *quī liber vocātus est* → *līber* (the free man called, ἐλεύθερος; opposite *servus*).
   **Apply:** `1Cor.7.22` la: `quī liber vocātus` → `quī līber vocātus`
3. **1 Cor 7:37** *sed liber et suī jūris est* → *līber* (free, with *suī jūris*).
   **Apply:** `1Cor.7.37` la: `sed liber et` → `sed līber et`
4. **1 Cor 9:1** *Scīlicet nōn sum liber?* → *līber* (οὐκ εἰμὶ ἐλεύθερος;).
   **Apply:** `1Cor.9.1` la: `nōn sum liber?` → `nōn sum līber?`
5. **1 Cor 9:19** *Cum enim ab omnibus liber essem* → *līber* (ἐλεύθερος ὢν ἐκ πάντων).
   **Apply:** `1Cor.9.19` la: `omnibus liber essem` → `omnibus līber essem`
6. **Matt 13:46** *eumque compāret* → *comparet*: "and buy it" (ἠγόρασεν), the subjunctive of *comparō* (to get,
   procure), short a; *compāret* is from *compāreō* (to appear). Eph 4:28 *bona comparet* in this batch.
   **Apply:** `Matt.13.46` la: `eumque compāret` → `eumque comparet`
7. **Luke 7:5** *idemque nōbīs collēgium cōnstrūxit* → *īdemque*: masculine, the centurion (αὐτὸς ᾠκοδόμησεν); the
   neuter *idem* has a short i. Your settled text writes masculine *īdem* elsewhere (e.g. 2 Cor 8:16 *quī īdem*).
   **Apply:** `Luke.7.5` la: `idemque nōbīs` → `īdemque nōbīs`
8. **Acts 21:8** *Philippī Evangelistæ* → *Ēvangelistæ*: as your *ēvangelium* and Eph 4:11 *ēvangelistās* in this
   batch (Greek εὐ-, Gaffiot *ēvangelista*). The capital is kept (a title, "Philip the Evangelist").
   **Apply:** `Acts.21.8` la: `Philippī Evangelistæ` → `Philippī Ēvangelistæ`
9. **Luke 1:47** *dē Deō servātōre meō* → *Servātōre*: decision 20 capitalizes *Servātor* for God (your settled
   *Servātōrem* 3× capital; Phil 3:20 and Eph 5:23 in this batch); NRSV "God my Savior". An apposition to *Deō*, not
   an adjective.
   **Apply:** `Luke.1.47` la: `dē Deō servātōre meō` → `dē Deō Servātōre meō`
10. **Rom 10:6** *Nē dīxeris* → *dīxerīs*: the perfect subjunctive of prohibition; your settled perfect
    subjunctives are *-erīs* throughout (Matt 5:36 *jūrāverīs*, 11:25 *cēlāverīs*, 22:12 *intrāverīs*, Luke 1:45
    *crēdiderīs*, John 17:24 *amāverīs*, Acts 1:24 *dēstināverīs*); Col 2:21 *tetigerīs* in this batch.
    **Apply:** `Rom.10.6` la: `“Nē dīxeris` → `“Nē dīxerīs`
