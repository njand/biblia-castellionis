# *eundem*, *eandem*, *quendam*, *quandam*: n before d (proposal)

Proposed 2026-11-12 (Claude), from the sweep on the New Testament checklist (`data/review/nt-publication-checklist.md`).
Nothing is applied until the Decision line is filled in.

The settled text writes these with *m*: *quemdam* 22, *quamdam* 7, *eamdem* 7, *eumdem* 3, and *eundem* 4 times as well,
so it is not even consistent. The classical spelling is *n*. In the corpus's classical tier (T1), *eundem* has 30 hits
and *eumdem* 0, *eandem* 45 and *eamdem* 0, *quendam* 18 and *quemdam* 0, *quandam* 26 and *quamdam* 0. The *m* forms
are medieval and later (William of Tyre, Albert of Aix). Gaffiot 2016 declines *īdem* and *quīdam* with *eundem*,
*eandem*, *quendam*, *quandam*, listing *eumdem* only as a variant. No rule covers these words yet.
`orthography.tsv` would get word rules for all four.

Left as they are: *quamdiū*, *aliquamdiū*, *jamdiū* (Gaffiot heads them so), *circumdō*, *septemdecim*.

**Recommendation: accept all.**
