## C. Brackets: words the modern editions leave out

**Decision:** accept (Nathan, 2026-11-17: "Apply them")

1. **1:4** *misericordiam*: TR χάρις, ἔλεος, εἰρήνη. NRSVue footnote "Other ancient authorities read *Grace, mercy,*".
   **Apply:** `Titus.1.4` la: `grātiam, misericordiam, pācem` → `grātiam, [misericordiam,] pācem`
2. **3:15** *Āmēn*: TR ἀμήν. NRSVue footnote "Other ancient authorities add *Amen*".
   **Apply:** `Titus.3.15` la: `omnibus grātia: Āmēn.` → `omnibus grātia[: Āmēn].`
