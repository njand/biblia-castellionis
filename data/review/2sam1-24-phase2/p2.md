# 2 Samuel 7–12: proofreader p2 (2026-11-21)

Every verse of 7–12 read in `text/draft/2SA.preview.usfm` (built 12:28, after the merged decision files) against the
BSB with its layout, the Hebrew, the Vulgate and 1738 (`text/orig/2SA.usfm`), as p1 did for 1–6.

**Words.** The preview's words were compared with 1738's by script (case, macrons, æ/ae, i/j and u/v folded; the
approved word, stem and phrase rules applied to 1738's side). Every remaining difference is a listed draft fix
(`2sam1-24-draft-fixes.md`): the marks dropped at 8:7 (*G. … H.*), 8:8 (*G. L. … L. … G. … H.*) and 8:13 (*L. … H.*);
11:9 *Urjas → Urias*, 11:27 *ma trimoniumque*, 12:17 *senatcres*, 12:20 *adoranit* and *adposito.epulatus*. The
script missed four approved rules because 1738 prints u for v, and I checked them by hand: 7:8 *adsciui → ascīvī* and 11:27 *adsciuit → ascīvit*
(`adscivi`, `adscivit`), 12:15 *coniux → conjūnx* (`conjux`), 12:24 *adpellauit → appellāvit* (`adpellavit`). The
others are rule-made too (12:22 *misertus → miseritus*, 11:21 *oppetiit → oppetīvit*, *quum → cum*, *caussa → causa*,
*intelligens → intellegēns*, *redemt- → redēmpt-*, *contemto → contemptō*, *epistola → epistula*). No omission or
misreading found.

**Spelling gap (no rule, left for Claude/Nathan):** 11:2 *pulcritūdine* (1738 *pulcritudine*; again 14:25
*pulcritūdinis*) against the settled Gen 6:2 *pulchritūdine* (Gaffiot heads *pulchritūdō*). The settled 1 Sam 9:2 also keeps
*pulcritūdine*, so 2 Samuel follows 1 Samuel as it stands. The edition spells the word both ways. A word or stem rule
(`pulcrit → pulchrit`) would settle it, and 1 Sam 9:2 would then need a proposal.

**Macrons: 2 forms rows** (`p2-forms.tsv`):
- 11:4#2 *Ea*: *Ea postquam … lūstrāta est, domum suam rediit*, nominative (Bathsheba, Hebrew וְהִיא); r3's *eā* row
  marked both copies, right only for *cum eā rem habuit*.
- 12:10 *absutūrum*: the future participle was unmarked (the macron tool knows no form); *-tūrus* as Gaffiot's
  *āfŭtūrus* and the settled *adfutūrum*.

Every other word was checked in context: *eā/ea* (7:4, 8:7, 11:27, 12:24 ablative; 12:8, 12:21, 12:28 nominative),
*eō*, *quō* (11:7), *quā* (10:5, 11:15), *hīc* 12:5 (adverb); perfects (*vēnit* 9:6, 10:14, 12:4; *pervēnērunt* 10:2;
*pervēnit* 10:17; *cecidērunt* 11:17); perfect subjunctive *nōverīs* 7:20 (as p1's *dīxerīs*); 4th-declension endings
(*exercitūs* 8:16, 10:16, 10:18 genitive; *exercitū* 10:7; *exercitus* 11:7; *statū* 11:7; *pōtū* 11:13; *vīctū* 9:10;
*lūctū* 11:27; *cōnspectū* 7:15, 12:11); *subāctīs*, *redāctæ* (Lachmann); *cedrinā* 7:2 and *circumsīdēns* 11:16 as the
readers decided (Gaffiot *cĕdrĭnus*, *circumsīdō*); *conjūnx* 12:15 (settled 7×); *sub dīō* 11:11 (settled 7×). The
`macron-check` on 7–12 flags only settled conventions (*Jōv-, Jōāb-, Saūl-, Sība, usque, hūcusque, æris/æreum*,
names).

**Layout: no rows.** Quotation marks are balanced: 7:5–16 (God to Nathan) with the oracle in ‘…’ at 7:5–7, 7:8–11a,
7:12–16, the formula and the third-person instruction of 7:11b outside it; David's prayer 7:18–29, reopened at the
paragraph 7:22; 9:6, 9:7, 9:8, 9:9–10, 9:11b; 10:11–12; 11:11; 11:21b; 11:23–24; 12:1b–4; 12:5–6; 12:7–12 with God's
words in ‘…’, closed and reopened at the *inquit* of 12:7 and 12:11; 12:13–14; 12:22–23. Reported speech is unmarked:
7:2–3, 9:1–4, 9:11a, 11:10, 11:15, 11:19–21a, 11:25, 12:19, 12:21. 1738's question marks after indirect questions
(9:2, 9:4, 11:10, 11:20–21, 12:19, 12:21) are kept, as at 2:1 and in 1 Samuel. *X: words, inquit* has a comma at
9:7, 9:8, 12:7, 12:13. Every punctuation and capital difference from 1738 is a reader's row with its reason (7:8 colon, 7:29
stray stop, 10:16 full stop, 12:20 comma; capitals at speech starts, after complete questions, and on adjectives
of peoples). Paragraphs: 7:1, 7:4, 7:17, 7:22, 8:1, 8:9, 8:15, 9:1, 9:6, 10:1, 10:6, 10:15, 11:1, 11:6, 11:14, 11:26,
12:1, 12:13, 12:15, 12:24, 12:26. Each opens a Castellio sentence at a 1738 indent or a BSB break, and the longest
paragraph has 240 words (12:1–12). The rows with no layout line (8:15, 11:26, 12:24, 12:26) are the draft's own, at
BSB headings and sentence starts.

**Left as they stand.** 12:31 *omnibus oppidīs Mōabītārum* (Hebrew Ammonites): Castellio's reading (1554, 1726
likewise), for the notes pass (`notes-a.md` item 8). 7:11 *(inquiēs)* is kept in parentheses: it does not split a
quotation. The BSB sets 8:16–18 as a list (*li1*). The preview has it as prose, in Castellio's sentences, and no reader asked for a list.
