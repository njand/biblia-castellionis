# 2 Samuel 19–24: proofreader p4 (2026-11-21)

Every verse of 19–24 (206 verses; the song of ch. 22 and the last words 23:1–7 as poetry) read in
`text/draft/2SA.preview.usfm` (built 12:28, after the merged decision files) against the BSB with its layout, the Hebrew,
the Vulgate and 1738 (`text/orig/2SA.usfm`), as p1–p3 did for 1–18.

**Words.** The preview's words were compared with 1738's by p3's script (case, macrons, æ/ae, i/j and u/v folded; the
approved word, stem and phrase rules applied to 1738's side, the rule keys folded for u/v and i/j). Only three
differences remain, each a listed draft fix (`2sam1-24-draft-fixes.md`): 20:8 *pervenernnt → pervenerunt*, 23:13
*Odollam → Adullam*, 23:34 *Achithophelis → Achitophelis*. 24:12 *unumquoduis → unumquodvis* is folded away by u/v. No
Castellio marks (*G. L. H.*) stand in 1738's 19–24. The rest are approved rules (*quum → cum*, *crucifix- → crucī fīx-*
21:9, 21:13, *tamdiu → tam diū* 23:10, *caussa*, *adplic-*, *adparens*, *adgressus*, *adfici*, *adscenderet*, *adseris*,
*obedi- → obœdi-*, *sobolis/soboli → subolis/subolī*, *petierunt → petīvērunt*, *simulac → simul ac* …). I found no
omission or misreading, and no spelling gap that needs a new rule.

**Macrons: 4 forms rows** (`p4-forms.tsv`):
- 21:17 *victumque*: *vincō*'s participle (overcame), unmarked as the settled *victus, victōs, victīsque*. The preview's
  *vīctumque* is *vīctus* (food).
- 23:10 *cecīdit*: *caedō* (*tam diū eōs cecīdit*, he struck them; Hebrew וַיַּךְ), as the settled *cecīdī*, *cecīderit*.
- 23:22 *vēnit*: a perfect, beside *pervēnit* in the same sentence (and 23:19).
- 24:13 *īnfēstārī*: Gaffiot *īnfēstō*, matching the preview's *īnfēstōs* at 22:41.

`macron-check` on 19–24 flags only settled conventions (*Jōv-, Jōāb-, Sadōc-, Saūl-, Sība, Seba, Dane, usque, quæsō*)
