# r4: 2 Samuel 12–14 (42 layout, 79 forms; 0 errors)

Verse reader, 2026-11-21, under `BRIEF.md`: every verse of 2 Samuel 12–14 beside the KJV, the BSB (its layout column),
the WLC, the LXX for the names, and 1738's printed text (`text/orig/2SA.usfm`). Rows in `r4-layout.tsv` and
`r4-forms.tsv`. Every layout row's words checked against `text/draft/2SA.usfm` with `verses.words_of` (all match, case
apart; the punctuation changes, from `verses.punctuation_changes`, are listed below); every forms row checked to hit its
word in the preview's verse, and each one changes the preview. Castellio's *G. L. … H.* (13:21, 14:30), *L. … H.* (13:37)
and *G. … H.* (14:27) left in the draft as they stand (no layout row touches those verses).

- **Quoted**: Nathan's parable 12:1b–4 (after *hīs verbīs:*); David's sentence 12:5–6 (after *sīc dīcit:*); Nathan
  12:7–14 with God's words 12:7b–12 in ‘…’ (the parentheses *(inquit Jōva Deus Isrāēlītārum)* 12:7 and *(inquit Jōva)*
  12:11 made commas, the inner quotation closed and reopened, Nathan's reopened “‘ at *Ego*, as the settled 1 Sam
  2:30–31); David 12:13 and Nathan's answer 12:13b–14; David to his servants 12:22–23; the direct turn of Jonadab's advice
  13:5 (*“Quod sī ad tē …”*, the first sentence reported); Amnon 13:11; Tamar 13:12–13; Absalom 13:20 (closed before
  *Ita mānsit*); the king 13:25; the woman of Tekoa and the king 14:4–20 (her thought *‘Hem, ā rēge contendam …

  the king to Joab 14:21; Joab 14:22. 14:19's parenthesis *(inquit respondēns)* made commas.

  commas added before vocatives 13:12 *Nōlī, frāter*, 14:19 *dīcis, domine rēx*.
- **Reported, unmarked** (the BSB quotes): 12:19, 12:21, Joab's message 12:27–28, 13:4, 13:5a, 13:6, 13:7, 13:9,
  13:15–17, 13:24, 13:26–27, Absalom's orders 13:28, the report 13:30, Jonadab 13:32–33 and 13:35, Joab's instructions
