KJV, the BSB (its marks and layout columns), the Hebrew and 1738's printed text (`text/orig/2SA.usfm`). Rows in
`r5-layout.tsv` and `r5-forms.tsv`. Every layout row's words checked against `text/draft/2SA.usfm` (all 44 match, case
and punctuation apart); every forms row checked to hit its word in the preview's verse. Castellio's *L. … H.* (15:18),
*G. L. … H.* (15:20) and *G. … H.* (17:3) kept in the rows' text as the draft has them, for the merge. Quotation marks
