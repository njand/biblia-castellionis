# r2: 2 Samuel 4–7 (33 layout, 56 forms; 0 errors)

Verse reader, 2026-11-21, under `BRIEF.md`: every verse of 2 Samuel 4–7 beside the KJV, the BSB (its layout column), the
WLC, the LXX where it bears on a name, and 1738's printed text (`text/orig/2SA.usfm`). Rows in `r2-layout.tsv` and
`r2-forms.tsv`. Every layout row's words checked against `text/draft/2SA.usfm` with `verses.words_of` (all match; the
only punctuation changes are listed below); every forms row's key checked to hit its word in the verse (7:27 *causa*
hits the respelled *caussa*). No G./L./H. marks in these chapters.

- **Quoted**: the brothers 4:8; David's oath and sentence 4:9–11; the saying 5:8 (*dictum illud: “Cæcus et claudus
  domum nē intrent.”*, as 1 Sam 10:12); David and God 5:19 (*Cui Jōva, “Adgredere,” inquit, “nam …”*); David 5:20;
  Michal and David 6:20–22; God to Nathan 7:5–16 in “…”, the oracle in ‘…’ after *Sīc dīcit Jōva:* (the formula outside
  the inner quotation, as the settled 1 Sam 2:27, 15:2): ‘…’ 7:5–7, closed before 7:8's *Quamobrem sīc dīcit Jōva
  armipotēns (dīcēs meō Dāvīdī):*, reopened at *‘Ego tē* (1738 prints *Ego* capital), closed at 7:11 *præposuī.’*, the
  third-person sentence *Igitur tibi (inquiēs) … nūntiat Jōva, sē domum esse īnstrūctūrum* left in the outer quotation,
  reopened at 7:12 *‘Cum plēnā ætāte* (first person again), both closed at 7:16 *sempiternum.’”*; David's prayer
  7:18–29, reopened *“Ergō* at the 7:22 paragraph.
- **Punctuation**: *X: words, inquit* colons made commas (5:19 *Cui Jōva,*, *inquit,*; 6:21 *Cui Dāvīd,*); **6:21 the
  parenthesis *(quī mē … imperātōrem esse)* made commas**, since it would open the resumed quotation; 7:8 a colon added
  after *(dīcēs meō Dāvīdī)*; 7:29 1738's stray full stop *propāgō. tuō* dropped.
- **Reported, unmarked** (the BSB quotes): 4:10's messenger, 5:1–2 (the tribes and God's promise), 5:6 the Jebusites'
  taunt, 5:8a David's proclamation (an unfinished period), 5:23–24 God's second answer, 6:9 (no speech in Castellio),
  6:12's report, 7:2 David's proposal, 7:3 Nathan's answer, 7:11b.
- **Paragraphs**: added 4:4 (1738), 4:5 (BSB; the murder resumed after the aside), 5:11 (1738; the draft's 5:12 moved
  here, `-` at 5:12, which continues 5:11's sentence after a colon), 6:6 (BSB; `-` at 6:5, which continues 6:3–4's
  ablative absolutes), 6:20 (1738; `-` at 6:16, the main clause of the sentence begun at 6:15), 7:4 (7:1–16 was 269
  words), 7:17 (`-` at 7:18, the main clause of 7:17's sentence), 7:22 (7:17–29 was 285). Kept the draft's 4:9, 5:6,
  5:17, 6:12.
- **Capitals**: *Isrāēlīticī* 5:2, 7:8, *Isrāēlīticā* 6:5, *Isrāēlīticō* 6:21, *Isrāēlīticum* 7:24, *Jūdaicīs* 6:2
