# 2 Samuel 13–18: proofreader p3 (2026-11-21)

Every verse of 13–18 (15:9–11 one verse; 192 verses) read in `text/draft/2SA.preview.usfm` (built 12:28, after the
merged decision files) against the BSB with its layout, the Hebrew, the Vulgate and 1738 (`text/orig/2SA.usfm`), as
p1 and p2 did for 1–12.

**Words.** The preview's words were compared with 1738's by script (case, macrons, æ/ae, i/j and u/v folded; the
approved word, stem and phrase rules applied to 1738's side, with the rules' own keys folded for u/v and i/j as well,
so the rules reach words 1738 prints with u for v). Every remaining difference is a listed draft fix
(`2sam1-24-draft-fixes.md`): the marks dropped at 13:21 (*G. L. … H.*), 13:37 (*L. … H.*), 14:27 (*G. … H.*), 14:30
(*G. L. … H.*), 15:18 (*L. … H.*), 15:20 (*G. L. … H.*), 17:3 (*G. … H.*), and 17:27 *Berzelæus → Berzellæus*.
Three rule-made changes were reached only through 1738's u for v, checked by hand: 15:2 *audiuerat → audierat*,
15:36 *audiueritis → audieritis*, 16:21 *audiuissent → audīssent*. The rest are the approved rules (*quum → cum*,
*quemdam/quamdam → quendam/quandam*, *petiit → petīvit*, *sumt-/absumsit/sumsisse → sūmpt-/absūmpsit/sūmpsisse*,
*interemtum*, *adpon-, adloqu-, adlocut-, adfic-, adfer-, adpell-, adpend-, adpropinqu-, adgress-, adscend-, adlatur-*,
*intelligo/-ens*, *caussa*, *quotidie*, *quatuor*, *sexcenti*, *præteriissent*, *paullulum/paullo*, *adolescens*,
*cœnaculum*, *næ → nē*, *eis → iis*, *nec ne → necne*).

**A misreading of 1738 (not a rule; for Claude/Nathan).** 14:10 *dēsertō ad mē*: the read lines (`2Sam1-24.tsv`
row 1392, leaf 371 col. 1 line 58) and so `text/orig/2SA.usfm` and the draft have *deserto*, but the page prints
**deferto**: on a 4× crop of leaf 371 the letter has the f's full crossbar, as *faxo* in the same line, not the ſ's
left nub of *ſolio* and *ſi* in the line above. The hOCR itself reads *deferto*, and 1726's OCR *deferco*. The sense
needs it: *dēfertō ad mē*, future imperative of *dēferō*, "bring (report) him to me" (Hebrew וַהֲבֵאתוֹ אֵלַי,
Vulgate *adduc eum ad me*); *dēsertō* means nothing here. It needs a correction of the read line, a proposal against
`text/orig/2SA.usfm` (approved, so not changed here) and a draft fix → *dēfertō* (macron tool: *dēfertō* [MW]).

**Spelling gap (no rule):** 13:24 *petiitque* (1738 *petiitque*): the approved word rule *petiit → petivit* does not
reach the form with *-que*, so the preview prints *petiitque* beside *petīvit* in 13:6, 13:8, 13:26. A word rule
*petiitque → petivitque* would settle it (no settled verse has either form). Also p2's *pulcritūdinis* 14:25.
