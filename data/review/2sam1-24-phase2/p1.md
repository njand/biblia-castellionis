# 2 Samuel 1–6: proofreader p1 (2026-11-21)

Every verse of 1–6 read in `text/draft/2SA.preview.usfm` (built 12:28, after the merged decision files) against the
BSB with its layout, the Hebrew, the Vulgate and 1738 (`text/orig/2SA.usfm`).

**Words.** The preview's words were compared with 1738's by script (case, macrons, æ/ae, i/j and u/v folded; the
approved word, stem and phrase rules of `data/rules/orthography.tsv` applied to 1738's side). Every remaining
difference is a listed draft fix (`2sam1-24-draft-fixes.md`): the marks dropped at 1:19 (*G. L. … H.*) and 1:26
(*L. … H.*), and 3:27 *Asaclis → Asaelis*. The rule-made changes (*quum → cum, brachio → bracchio, adfero → affero,
peremtum → peremptum, diremti → dirempti, oppetiit → oppetivit, adsenso- → assenso-, petiit → petivit, quaesivisset
→ quaesisset, coenam → cenam, adprob-, adlocuti, sumsit → sumpsit, adseruisti → asseruisti, adgred-, adpellavit,
adscendere, simulac → simul ac, audivisset → audisset, adloquitur*) are all approved rules. No omission or misreading
found; no spelling gap needing a new rule.

**Macrons: 6 forms rows** (`p1-forms.tsv`):
- 2:5 *sepelīverītis*: a perfect subjunctive after causal *quī*. The settled text gives the perfect subjunctive
  *-erītis* (1 Thess 1:6–9, Acts 5:8). This overrides r1's *sepelīveritis*: r1 cited 1 Sam 8:18 *dēlēgeritis*, but
  that is a future perfect.
- 3:19 *eādem* (*eādem dē rē*); 3:22 *compositā* (*compositā pāce*, ablative absolute); 5:24 *gressūs* (genitive
  with *sonitum*): the preview had the nominative forms.
- 3:26 *Bōrsirā* (after *ā*, as 5:25 *ā Gabā*) and 6:3 *Ozā* (ablative absolute with *Ahiōne … agentibus*): the
  case ending only, with the names reader's stem marks kept.

Every other word was checked in context: the *hīc/hic*, *eō*, *quā* forms; perfects against presents (*vēnit*
3:20 and 5:13, *advēnit* 1:2, *fūgērunt*, *cōnfōdērunt*, *perfōdērunt*); 4th-declension endings (*currūs* 1:6,
*exercitūs* 2:8, *tribūs* 5:1, *passūs* 6:13); *dīxerīs* 1:16; *jejūnāre* 1:12 (Gaffiot *jĕjūnō*); *cujās* 1:13
(Gaffiot *cŭjās, ātis*); *ēphōdō* 6:14 (settled 1 Sam 2:18). `macron-check` on chapters 1–6 flags only settled
conventions (*Saūl-, Jōv-, Jōāb-, Abnēr, usque*).

**Layout: no rows.** Quotation marks are balanced in every speech: 1:3–10 with the nested ‘…’ at 1:7–9, 1:14–16,
1:19–27, 2:5–7, 2:20–27, 3:8–10, 3:28–29, 3:33–34, 4:8, 4:9–11, 5:8, 5:19–20, 6:20–22. Reported speech is unmarked:
1:13, 2:1, 3:12–13, 3:17–18, 3:24–25, 3:38–39, 5:1–2, 5:6, 5:23–24. Where 1738 has *X: words, inquit*, the
speaker's colon is now a comma: 1:6, 1:15, 2:20–21, 5:19, 6:21. Every other punctuation difference from 1738 is a
reader's row with its reason. The capitals at verse starts follow the sentences. Each paragraph opens where a
Castellio sentence starts (1738's indents plus 1:11, 3:6, 3:30, 4:5, 4:9, 5:6, 6:6, 6:12, all at BSB breaks), and
none runs over 191 words. The poetry of 1:19–27 and 3:33–34 is on the BSB's lines, and the *\m* after 3:34 is right.

**Left as they stand.** 6:20 *Ō quanta fuit … gravitās?*: 1738's question mark on an exclamation, kept as printed.
2:1 *in quodnam proficīscerētur?*: an indirect question with 1738's question mark, as at 1:3 and in 1 Samuel. 1:20
*Ascalōniīs*: capitalized by the readers, though 1738 prints *ascaloniis*; this follows the book's practice.

Rows: 0 layout, 6 forms.
