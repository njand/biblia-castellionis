# r3: 2 Samuel 8–11 (21 layout, 76 forms; 0 errors)

Verse reader, 2026-11-21, under `BRIEF.md`: every verse of 2 Samuel 8–11 beside the KJV, the BSB (its layout column),
the WLC, the LXX for the names, and 1738's printed text (`text/orig/2SA.usfm`). Rows in `r3-layout.tsv` and
`r3-forms.tsv`. Every layout row's words checked against `text/draft/2SA.usfm` with `verses.words_of` (all match, case
apart; the punctuation changes are listed below); every forms row checked to hit its word in the preview's verse, and
each one changes the preview. Castellio's *G. … H.*, *G. L. … L. … G. … H.* (8:7–8) and *L. … H.* (8:13) left in the
draft as they stand (no layout row touches those verses).

- **Quoted**: David and Mephibosheth 9:6 (*“Mephibosethe.” Cui ille: “Ecce mē tuum.”*), 9:7, 9:8; David to Ziba 9:9–10
  (closed at *convīctor erit.”*, the note on Ziba's household outside); the king 9:11b (*“Ergō cibum capiet …,” inquit
  ille, “sīcut …”*); Joab to Abishai 10:11–12 after *cum hujusmodī præceptīs:*; Uriah 11:11; the answer Joab gives the
  messenger 11:21 (*respondēret: “Etiam tuus Ūrīās Hettæus oppetīvit.”*); the messenger 11:23–24 after *atque ita
  retulit:*. No ‘…’ needed.
- **Punctuation**: *X: words, inquit* colons made commas (9:7 *Et Dāvīd,*, 9:8 *Atque ille supplex,*); **9:11 the
  parenthesis *(inquit ille)* made commas** (decision 65); colons kept where no *inquit* follows (9:6, 9:9, 11:11);
  **10:16 a full stop added** at *Adarezeriānī.* (1738 prints none; the preview had then lowered 10:17 *quod*).
- **Reported, unmarked** (the BSB quotes): 9:1, 9:2–4 (with 1738's question marks after the indirect questions, *an esset
  Siba?*, *ubinam esset?*), 9:11a Ziba's answer, 10:2 David's purpose, 10:3 the princes, 10:5, 11:5, 11:10, 11:12, the
  letter 11:15, the king's questions 11:20–21 (Castellio's accusatives and subjunctives, lower case inside the period),

- **Paragraphs**: added 8:9 (8:1–14 was 276 words; the BSB breaks and *Postquam* begins a sentence), 9:6, 10:6, 10:15,
  11:6 (1738's indents; 11:1–13 was 278 words); **removed 10:9** (`-`), the BSB's heading inside Castellio's sentence
  *Jōabus (cum …) intellegēns … ōrdināvit*. Kept the draft's 8:15 (the officers), 11:14, 11:26. 8:16–18 left as prose
  (Castellio's elliptical sentences), not the BSB's list lines.
- **Capitals**: adjectives of peoples and from names, 1738 lower case: *Damascēnōrum* 8:5 (as 8:6), *Bethrohōbānōrum*,
