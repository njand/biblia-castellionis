chapters 1–12). Every 1554 annotation on 2 Samuel 13–24 was read on the page and sorted as (a) a deliberate departure
from the Hebrew as read today, (b) a word no dictionary has, (c) commentary or a defence of Latin a reader can follow (no
note), or (d) a place where the annotation shows the settled text differs from what he meant. Decisions 53 and 56;
brief.md, "brief Latin footnotes"; the bar of `ot-notes-process.md` §2–3. The notes proposed are in `notes-b.md`.

Notes on the readings:
- **14:13**: the lemma does not match his own 1554 text, which reads *Cur igitur tu iſtam diuini populi periculo rationem
  iniuiſti* (canvas 178, column 332, the 1200-px page), as 1738 and the preview (*cūr igitur tū istam dīvīnī populī
  perīculō ratiōnem inīvistī*). The annotation was written against an earlier wording (*cõtra diuinum populum*), as A
  found at 7:23 and 1 Samuel's readers at 14:36. The sense (a plan against God's people) is the same. For
  `docs/editions.md`.
- **15:7**: the two Hebrew words are read on the zoom `z826-2s15.jpg`; their last letter looks to me like an aleph where
  ארבע / ארבעים would have an ayin (type or my reading; not settled). The sense is plain: "four" for the Hebrew's "forty".
  The NRSVue "four years" (note "Gk Syr: Heb *forty*") and the BSB "four years" (note "Syriac and some LXX manuscripts;
  Hebrew forty") do the same. No note. Brenton's LXX has forty (τεσσαράκοντα, `r5.md`), so Castellio's "Greek" is not
  his authority here: he cites Josephus.
- **18:3**: he reads עַתָּה "now" for אַתָּה "you" (the Hebrew "for now there are ten thousand like us"): *nam tū prō decem
  mīllibus nostrum es*, "you are worth ten thousand of us", **as the NRSVue** ("Gk Vg Symmachus: Heb *for now there are
  ten thousand such as we*") **and the BSB** ("Two Hebrew manuscripts, some LXX manuscripts, and Vulgate"). No note.
- **21:6**: he reads בְּהַר יְהוָה "on the mountain of the LORD" for the Hebrew's בְּחִיר יְהוָה "the chosen of the LORD"
  (*apud Gabāam Saūlī in Jōvæ monte*). The NRSVue does the same ("on the mountain of the LORD", note "Cn Compare: Heb *at
  Gibeah of Saul, the chosen of the Lord*"); the BSB keeps the Hebrew ("at Gibeah of Saul, the chosen of the LORD"). Not
  the consensus of `ot-notes-process.md` §3 for either kind: no note.
- **23:3**: commentary (his messianic reading of David's last words), no departure. The NRSVue and the BSB read 23:5 as a
  question expecting yes ("Is not my house …?"): his affirmative *ut aiat* is the same sense.
- **23:8**: see `notes-b.md` §E: the name (*Jasobaamus*, Chronicles' Jashobeam) and the spear (Chronicles' "he wielded his
  spear") from Chronicles, the 800 and "chief of the three" from Samuel; "Adino the Eznite" left out as part of the
  corrupt place. The NRSVue: "Gk Vg: Meaning of Heb uncertain" (twice); the BSB "Josheb-basshebeth the Tahchemonite …
  He wielded his spear against eight hundred men" (note: some LXX manuscripts, 1 Chr 11:11). Disputed, and the sense he
  gives is the modern Bibles'. No note.

**None proposed.** Every lemma but 14:13's matches the preview's words (spelling aside).

**Two are not 1554's** (no letter at the word, no gloss in the margin, on the 1200-px pages):

- **24:1** *a) quum Deus Abrahamo prolem promisisset innumerabilem, Dauid eam numerare voluit, quasi qui Dei promissum
  refelleret. Vid. 1. Par. 27.*: 1554 *RVrſus autẽ irato Iſraelitis Ioua, eò impul-|ſus eſt rex Dauid, ut iuberet
  Iſraelitas Iu-|dæoſq; numerari* (185, 346), no margin but the side-head *Iubet Da-|uid populũ | numerari.* Not found in
  1573 by the search (*promisisset* hits on canvases 153, 204, 411, 682, 858, none in 2 Samuel; *refelleret* none).

Every gloss is on the sense. 18:18's reconciles Absalom's "no son" with 14:27's three sons (the NRSVue and the BSB leave
the tension as it stands); 19:27's and 21:17's explain an image; 19:43's *decunx*; 24:1's the motive of the census.
None meets the bar (`notes-b.md` §D).

- 1738's six footnotes in 2 Samuel 13–24: four are 1554's margins; **15:7** (*Iosephus 4 habet …*) is a digest of the

  rationem*): written against another wording, as 7:23 and 1 Sam 14:36.
- Castellio's 15:7 *quatuor* rests on Josephus and the ease of reading ארבע for ארבעים (his words), not on the Greek; his
  18:3 and 21:6 readings are emendations he states (עתה, בהר), the first with "the Greek and Latin translator".
- 23:8: Castellio says he translated the place partly from 1 Chronicles 11, the Hebrew of Samuel being corrupt, and kept
  800 with Josephus (*Iſſæmum Achemæi filiũ*).
- **1573 prints the 23:3 annotation in the book** (canvas 224, search API: *3 Rector religiofus.) In Hebraeo est, Rector
  metus Dei …*, with the side-note *Religiofus*), as it did 12:26's (A) and 1 Samuel's.
- Castellio's *G.*, *G. L.*, *L.* … *H.* marks in 13:21, 13:37, 14:27, 14:30, 15:18, 15:20, 17:3 are in 1554's text;
  14:27's has the margin reference *3.Reg.15* (Abijam's mother, 1 Kgs 15:2).
