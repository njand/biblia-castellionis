# Seven slips in the settled New Testament, found by reading 1573's accents and confirmed in 1556

Proposed 2026-11-14. Reading 1573's accents on the page (decision 67, route 1) showed seven places where 1573
prints a different word from the settled text. Each was read on the page images of **1573** (MDZ bsb10141277, the
New Testament base, decision 24) and of **1556** (e-rara 10281656, Castellio's last lifetime edition, the
reference). In six, the two printings agree against the settled text; in one (John 14:16) they differ, and the
base is followed. The approved printed text (`text/orig/`) has the settled word in all seven too, since its words
are the settled text's: once these are decided, its next draft will follow.

Crops: `scratchpad/nt7/` of this session (1573 canvas and 1556 page ids below). **Your decision.**

1. **Luke 20:21** *Atque hīc sīc eum interrogārunt* → 1573 *Atq; hi sic eum* (canvas 808), 1556 *Atq; hi sic eum*
   (page 10282361): the pronoun, "these" (the spies of 20:20), not the adverb *hīc*.
   **Apply:** `Luke.20.21` la: `Atque hīc sīc eum` → `Atque hī sīc eum`
2. **John 9:27** *quod rūrsum vultis audīre?* → 1573 *quid rursum* (822), 1556 *quid rursum* (10282372): "why do
   you want to hear it again?".
   **Apply:** `John.9.27` la: `nec audīstis: quod rūrsum` → `nec audīstis: quid rūrsum`
3. **John 14:16** *Ego vērō meum Patrem exōrābō* → 1573 *egò verè* (826), 1556 *ego uerò* (10282375): the
   printings differ; the settled *vērō* is 1556's. Recommended: 1573's *vērē* "truly", as the base (decision 24;
   1556's readings are recorded as its variants). Your call.
   **Apply:** `John.14.16` la: `Ego vērō meum` → `Ego vērē meum`
4. **John 16:30** *Hinc tē ā Deō profectō crēdimus* → 1573 *profectũ* (828, the nasal bar, as its *Nũc* in the same
   verse), 1556 *profectum* (10282377): "we believe you came forth from God", the participle with *tē*; *profectō*
   "surely" is a slip.
   **Apply:** `John.16.30` la: `ā Deō profectō crēdimus` → `ā Deō profectum crēdimus`
5. **Acts 11:16** *illud Dominī dictum, quod dīxerat* → 1573 *quo dixerat* (841), 1556 *quo dixerat* (10282388):
   "the saying by which he had said".
   **Apply:** `Acts.11.16` la: `dictum, quod dīxerat` → `dictum, quō dīxerat`
6. **1 Cor 1:16** *Cēterum nesciō, an ūllum baptīzāverim* → 1573 *Cętero-|rum nescio an vllum* (867), 1556
   *Cęte-|rorū nescio an baptizauerim* (10282409): both *Cēterōrum* "of the rest"; *ūllum* is in 1573 (the base)
   only, and stays.
   **Apply:** `1Cor.1.16` la: `Cēterum nesciō` → `Cēterōrum nesciō`
7. **Acts 14:23** *Ac postquam cum suffrāgiīs iīs seniōrēs … creārunt* → 1573 *Ac postquá eis seniores* (844),
   1556 *Ac postquam eis seniores* (10282390): neither has *cum suffragiis* ("by vote"), which the settled text has
   from Wikisource (the early line, `data/calibration/scans.md`). (1556 also reads *cui crediderant* for 1573's *in
   quem crediderant*; the settled text has 1573's.)
   **Apply:** `Acts.14.23` la: `postquam cum suffrāgiīs iīs seniōrēs` → `postquam iīs seniōrēs`

**Decision:** accept all except 3 (Nathan, 2026-11-15: John 14:16 keeps 1556's *vērō*, 1573's *verè* taken as a misprint like its *tondat*; the printed text keeps *verè*)
