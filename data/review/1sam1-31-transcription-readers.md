# 1 Samuel 1–31, phase 1: the readers' notes

The eighteen readers' notes as written (2026-11-20), two leaves each, for `1sam1-31-transcription.md`. Row numbers
are those of `data/witnesses/read-1738/1Sam1-31.voted.tsv` (and of the `row` column of `1Sam1-31.corrections.tsv`);
verse numbers are 1738's. Where Claude settled a point afterwards, the summary says so. Lines the readers added with a
suffixed number (`51a`) were renumbered from 900 up on their leaf when merged (`read_lines` takes whole numbers); the
reading sheets' numbering was one off from the brief's formula (sheet k starts at row 14k+3), which the readers
noticed and which changed nothing, each crop being labelled with its row.
