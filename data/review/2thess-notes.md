**Decision:** accept (Nathan, 2026-11-17: "Apply them")

## C. Brackets: words the modern editions leave out

**Decision:** accept (Nathan, 2026-11-17: "Apply them")

   **Apply:** `2Thess.2.4` la: `templō tamquam Deus sedeat` → `templō [tamquam Deus] sedeat`

- 1:9, 3:14: contested construals.
