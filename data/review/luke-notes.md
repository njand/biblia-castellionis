# Luke: light notes, brackets and corrections

Built 2026-11-17 by the process in `nt-notes-process.md`, as for Mark and Matthew.

**Sources read:**
- `luke-notes-candidates.md` (`python -m pipeline notes Luke`), with its new section for the TR alone.
- The whole of Luke, read verse by verse against the TR, SBLGNT, BSB and WEB by four readers (chapters 1–6, 7–12, 13–18 and 19–24). Each of their findings was checked here against the files.
- The NRSVue (Bible Gateway), text and footnotes, for every verse below.
- The NET Bible's notes for 6:16 and 7:35.
- 1573's page images for 14:21, 15:12 and 19:47, and its OCR for the rest; for Castellio's own readings, the OCR of 1556 and the later printings (`nt-notes-form.md` §2).

Rewritten the same day to the test and wording of `nt-notes-form.md`: "the modern editions" means SBLGNT and NA28, and the notes use its formulas. In every item the NRSVue reads with the modern text against Castellio.

## A. Notes where the Greek text differs

**Decision:** accept (Nathan, 2026-11-17: "Apply them")

1. **2:14** *ergā hominēs benevolentia*: TR ἐν ἀνθρώποις εὐδοκία, "goodwill toward men"; the modern editions εὐδοκίας, "among those whom he favors" (NRSVue, with a footnote giving the TR's reading).
   **Apply:** `Luke.2.14` la: `hominēs benevolentia.”` → `hominēs benevolentia\f + \fr 2:14 \ft Cōdicēs antīquiōrēs habent ἐν ἀνθρώποις εὐδοκίας, ‘inter hominēs quibus favet’.\f*.”`
2. **4:44** *Galilææ*: TR τῆς Γαλιλαίας; the modern editions τῆς Ἰουδαίας. NRSVue "Judea", with a footnote giving "Galilee".
   **Apply:** `Luke.4.44` la: `per Galilææ collēgia` → `per Galilææ\f + \fr 4:44 \ft Cōdicēs antīquiōrēs habent τῆς Ἰουδαίας, ‘Jūdææ’.\f* collēgia`
3. **6:48** *quippe in petrā fundātam*: TR τεθεμελίωτο γὰρ ἐπὶ τὴν πέτραν; the modern editions διὰ τὸ καλῶς οἰκοδομῆσθαι αὐτήν, "because it had been well built" (NRSVue, with a footnote giving the TR's reading).
   **Apply:** `Luke.6.48` la: `quippe in petrā fundātam.` → `quippe in petrā fundātam\f + \fr 6:48 \ft Cōdicēs antīquiōrēs habent διὰ τὸ καλῶς οἰκοδομῆσθαι αὐτήν, ‘quod bene ædificāta erat’.\f*.`
4. **8:26, 8:37** *Gadarēnōrum*: TR Γαδαρηνῶν; the modern editions Γερασηνῶν (as Mark 5:1, accepted). NRSVue "Gerasenes" at both, with a footnote at each. One note, at the first.
   **Apply:** `Luke.8.26` la: `agrum Gadarēnōrum, quī` → `agrum Gadarēnōrum\f + \fr 8:26 \ft Cōdicēs antīquiōrēs habent Γερασηνῶν, ‘Gerasēnōrum’, ut et v. 37.\f*, quī`
5. **9:10** *in dēsertum locum, urbis vocātæ Bēthsaida*: TR εἰς τόπον ἔρημον πόλεως καλουμένης Βηθσαϊδά; the modern editions εἰς πόλιν καλουμένην Βηθσαϊδά, "into a city called Bethsaida" (NRSVue).
   **Apply:** `Luke.9.10` la: `vocātæ Bēthsaida.` → `vocātæ Bēthsaida\f + \fr 9:10 \ft Cōdicēs antīquiōrēs habent εἰς πόλιν καλουμένην Βηθσαϊδά, ‘in urbem vocātam Bēthsaida’.\f*.`
6. **9:35** *cārissimus*: TR ὁ ἀγαπητός; the modern editions ὁ ἐκλελεγμένος, "my Chosen" (NRSVue, with a footnote giving "my Beloved").
   **Apply:** `Luke.9.35` la: `Fīlius meus cārissimus;` → `Fīlius meus cārissimus\f + \fr 9:35 \ft Cōdicēs antīquiōrēs habent ὁ ἐκλελεγμένος, ‘ēlēctus’.\f*;`
7. **9:50** *contrā nōs … ā nōbīs*: TR καθ᾿ ἡμῶν … ὑπὲρ ἡμῶν; the modern editions ὑμῶν … ὑμῶν, "against you … for you" (NRSVue). This is a change of person, as at 1 John 4:4.
   **Apply:** `Luke.9.50` la: `ā nōbīs est.”` → `ā nōbīs est\f + \fr 9:50 \ft Cōdicēs antīquiōrēs habent καθʼ ὑμῶν … ὑπὲρ ὑμῶν, ‘contrā vōs … prō vōbīs’.\f*.”`
8. **10:15** *quæ ad cælum ūsque ēvecta es*: TR ἡ ἕως τοῦ οὐρανοῦ ὑψωθεῖσα; the modern editions μὴ ἕως οὐρανοῦ ὑψωθήσῃ;, "will you be exalted to heaven? No…" (NRSVue). This is the same note as at Matt 11:23.
   **Apply:** `Luke.10.15` la: `ūsque ēvecta es,` → `ūsque ēvecta es\f + \fr 10:15 \ft Cōdicēs antīquiōrēs habent μὴ ἕως οὐρανοῦ ὑψωθήσῃ; ‘num ad cælum ūsque efferēris?’\f*,`
9. **14:5** *asinus*: TR ὄνος, "donkey"; SBLGNT υἱός, "son". NRSVue "a child or an ox", with a footnote giving "a donkey".
   - RP has υἱός too, so the apparatus has no unit here. The readers found it, and the new TR-alone section now lists it.
   **Apply:** `Luke.14.5` la: `ejus asinus aut` → `ejus asinus\f + \fr 14:5 \ft Cōdicēs antīquiōrēs habent υἱός, ‘fīlius’.\f* aut`
10. **16:9** *ut cum dēfūnctī fueritis*: TR ἐκλίπητε, "when you fail" (die); the modern editions ἐκλίπῃ, "when it (the wealth) is gone" (NRSVue).
    - The candidates file dropped this as "another form of the same word". The ending changes the subject, so it is put here by hand.
    **Apply:** `Luke.16.9` la: `cum dēfūnctī fueritis,` → `cum dēfūnctī fueritis\f + \fr 16:9 \ft Cōdicēs antīquiōrēs habent ἐκλίπῃ, ‘cum dēfēcerint opēs’.\f*,`
11. **23:15** *ad eum enim mīsī vōs*: TR ἀνέπεμψα γὰρ ὑμᾶς πρὸς αὐτόν, "I sent you to him"; the modern editions ἀνέπεμψεν γὰρ αὐτὸν πρὸς ἡμᾶς, "he sent him back to us" (NRSVue).
    **Apply:** `Luke.23.15` la: `mīsī vōs,` → `mīsī vōs\f + \fr 23:15 \ft Cōdicēs antīquiōrēs habent ἀνέπεμψεν γὰρ αὐτὸν πρὸς ἡμᾶς, ‘remīsit enim eum ad nōs’.\f*,`
12. **24:17** *ambulantēs, idque trīstēs?*: TR καί ἐστε σκυθρωποί, part of the question; the modern editions καὶ ἐστάθησαν σκυθρωποί, "They stood still, looking sad" (NRSVue, with a footnote giving the TR's reading).
    **Apply:** `Luke.24.17` la: `idque trīstēs?”` → `idque trīstēs\f + \fr 24:17 \ft Cōdicēs antīquiōrēs habent καὶ ἐστάθησαν σκυθρωποί, ‘et stetērunt trīstēs’.\f*?”`

## B. Notes where Castellio's Latin says what the Greek does not

**Decision:** accept (Nathan, 2026-11-17: "Apply them")

13. **6:16** *Jūdam Jācōbī frātrem*: Greek (TR and SBLGNT) Ἰούδαν Ἰακώβου, "Judas of James". NRSVue "Judas son of James", with no footnote; the NET has no note on it. Castellio reads "brother", as the KJV does, after Jude 1. *Iudam Iacobi fratrem* in 1573 (OCR), 1681, 1715, 1726, 1747, 1750 and 1758. The note gives the Greek alone, which names no relation.
    **Apply:** `Luke.6.16` la: `Jācōbī frātrem,` → `Jācōbī frātrem\f + \fr 6:16 \ft Græcē Ἰούδαν Ἰακώβου, ‘Jūdam Jācōbī’, sine ‘frātrem’.\f*,`
14. **7:35** *Ita suīs omnibus aliēna est sapientia*: the same reading as Matt 11:19 (Matthew's file, item 15). NRSVue "wisdom is vindicated by all her children"; the NET (tn) gives "shown to be right" as the alternative. *aliena est sapientia* in 1573 (OCR) and every later printing whose OCR gives the verse (1715, 1726, 1747, 1750, 1758).
    **Apply:** `Luke.7.35` la: `aliēna est sapientia.”` → `aliēna est sapientia\f + \fr 7:35 \ft Græcē ἐδικαιώθη ἡ σοφία ἀπὸ τῶν τέκνων αὐτῆς πάντων, ‘jūstificāta est sapientia ab omnibus fīliīs suīs’.\f*.”`
15. **15:12** *dīvīsit eī*: Greek (TR and SBLGNT) διεῖλεν αὐτοῖς, "between them" (NRSVue), and the Vulgate *illis*. Castellio gives it to the younger son only, which 15:31 ("all that is mine is yours") does not bear out.
    - 1573 prints *diuiſit ei facultates* (canvas 804, read on the page), and so do 1726 and 1758. 1556's OCR doesn't give the verse.
    - 1681, 1715, 1747 and 1750 print *eis*: later editors brought it into line with the Greek. Whether *ei* is Castellio's choice or an early misprint can't be told without 1556's page; the note gives the Greek either way.
    **Apply:** `Luke.15.12` la: `dīvīsit eī facultātēs,` → `dīvīsit eī\f + \fr 15:12 \ft Græcē διεῖλεν αὐτοῖς, ‘dīvīsit iīs’.\f* facultātēs,`
16. **19:47** *Pharisæī*: Greek (TR and SBLGNT) οἱ ἀρχιερεῖς, "the chief priests" (NRSVue). Elsewhere Castellio renders the word as *pontificēs* (20:1, 20:19, 22:2). 1573 prints *Pharisæi verò & Scribæ* (canvas 807, read on the page), and 1747, 1750 and 1758 the same.
    **Apply:** `Luke.19.47` la: `Pharisæī vērō et Scrībæ` → `Pharisæī\f + \fr 19:47 \ft Græcē οἱ ἀρχιερεῖς, ‘pontificēs’.\f* vērō et Scrībæ`

## D. Corrections to the edited text

**Decision:** accept (Nathan, 2026-11-17: "Apply them")

44. **3:7** *summōvit* → *summonuit*: Greek ὑπέδειξεν, "warned".
    - The witnesses: 1573 prints *ſummonuit* (canvas 792; its OCR too). 1681 and 1747 have *summonuit*, 1750 *submonuit*, and 1556's OCR *ommonuit*. Only 1758 and Wikisource have *summovit*.
    - The settled Matt 3:7, the same saying, reads *summonuit*, and `data/rules/orthography.tsv` (row 408, approved: *submoneo* → *summoneo*) cites it.
    - `data/decisions/Luke.orig.tsv` calls 1573's *summonuit* "a misprint for summouit". It is not one, and that row would go on acceptance; `text/orig/LUK.usfm` already has *summonuit*.
    **Apply:** `Luke.3.7` la: `quis vōs summōvit futūram` → `quis vōs summonuit futūram`
45. **14:21** *fīcōs* → *vīcōs*: Greek ῥύμας, "lanes" (NRSVue "the streets and lanes"); *fīcōs* means "figs".
    - 1573 prints *ficos* (canvas 803, read on the page), a misprint; Wikisource copies it.
    - 1556 prints *uicos* (OCR), and 1715, 1747, 1750 and 1758 *vicos* (`data/calibration/Luke14-24-collation.tsv`).
    - Gaffiot: *vīcus*. `text/orig` keeps *ficos* (decision 58), as for Mark 5:3.
    **Apply:** `Luke.14.21` la: `atque fīcōs:` → `atque vīcōs:`
46. **11:36** *lucernā* → *lucerna*: the lamp is the subject of *collūstret*, "as if a lamp lit you with its brightness". 1573's OCR has *fulgore lucerna collustret*.
    **Apply:** `Luke.11.36` la: `fulgōre lucernā collūstret` → `fulgōre lucerna collūstret`
47. **12:53** *ā fīlia* → *ā fīliā* (ablative after *ā*; 1573 *à filia*).
    **Apply:** `Luke.12.53` la: `māter ā fīlia,` → `māter ā fīliā,`

## Considered and left out

**From the apparatus and the TR-alone list:**
- 7:28 *vātēs*: the modern editions drop προφήτης and τοῦ βαπτιστοῦ; John is named either way.
- 3:32 *Salmōnis*, and 3:33 *Arāmī*, which the modern test now admits (SBLGNT and NA28 read τοῦ Ἀδμὶν τοῦ Ἀρνί): the NRSVue footnote at 3:33 says the manuscripts "vary widely", so the reading is contested.
- 6:10 *tam sāna, quam erat altera*: left out, as at Mark 3:5.
- 13:15 the singular *persōnāte*; 13:31 *diē* for "hour"; 17:2 *molā*: a single word each.
- 23:45 *sōl īnfuscātus est*: "the sun's light failed" is close, and the NRSVue's own footnote gives "eclipsed".
- 24:53 *collaudantēs … grātiās agentēs*, where the modern editions leave out αἰνοῦντες καί.
- The single-word entries.

**The passages Westcott–Hort double-brackets** (22:19b–20, 24:3, 24:6, 24:12, 24:36, 24:40, 24:51–52): NA28 and SBLGNT keep them, so they are not marked. 22:43–44 and 23:34a stay unmarked, as decided on 2026-11-12.

**From the reading of each chapter:**
- 12:25 *statūram … cubitō*: NRSVue gives "add a cubit to your stature" in its footnote.
- 12:49 *quid volō?*: the Greek is ambiguous.
- 22:31 *cōnātus est*: NRSVue gives "has obtained permission" in its footnote.
- 17:6 *caprifīcō* for "mulberry", and 19:4 *caprifīcum*: the name of a tree.
- 23:39 *maledīcēbant* after *ūnus*: 1573 prints it so.
- Also 1:1, 2:49, 7:29, 7:47, 9:51, 11:41, 12:1.

**A question for later, not part of this file:** the edition divides 24:48–49 differently from the KJV and the NRSVue. 24:48 ends *et ego sum vōbīs Patris meī prōmissum missūrus*, which the KJV begins 24:49 with ("And, behold, I send the promise of my Father"). 1573's verse numbers here have not been checked.
