# Slips in the settled chapters, found while reading Acts 1–28

Proposed 2026-11-09. The Acts readers compared their words with your settled forms (`data/gold/`) and found four
markings there that the grammar or Gaffiot rules out. Each was checked in its verse. Nothing is applied: these are
settled chapters, so they wait for your Decision line. (Gen 24:8 *religiōnē*, which they also met, is already item 4
of `settled-fixes-exod.md`.)

**Decision:** accept all (Nathan, 2026-11-09)

1. **Gen 6:16** *tertiāque contignātiōnē distinguitō* → *contignātiōne*: ablative singular of a third-declension
   noun (*contignātiō, -ōnis*), short final e. Acts 20:9 has *dē tertiā contignātiōne*.
   **Apply:** `Gen.6.16` la: `tertiāque contignātiōnē distinguitō` → `tertiāque contignātiōne distinguitō`
2. **Gen 37:4** *ut eum amīce adpellāre* → *amīcē*: the adverb (Gaffiot *amīcē*), as Acts 15:33 *amīcē dīmissī*.
   **Apply:** `Gen.37.4` la: `eum amīce adpellāre` → `eum amīcē adpellāre`
3. **Luke 2:13** *Et repentē exstitit* → *repente*: Gaffiot *rĕpentĕ* (short final e); your only *repentē*. Acts
   2:2, 16:26 and 22:6 have *repente*.
   **Apply:** `Luke.2.13` la: `Et repentē exstitit` → `Et repente exstitit`
4. **Matt 4:24** *tōtam Sȳriam pervāsit* → *Syriam*: Gaffiot *Sўrĭa* (Συρία, short υ), as your Luke 2:2 *Syriæ* and
   Acts 15:23, 15:41, 18:18, 20:3, 21:3.
   **Apply:** `Matt.4.24` la: `tōtam Sȳriam pervāsit` → `tōtam Syriam pervāsit`
