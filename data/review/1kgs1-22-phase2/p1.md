# 1 Kings 1–5: proofreader p1 (2026-11-22)

I read every verse of 1–5 in `text/draft/1KI.preview.usfm` (built 11:34 from the merged decision files) against the
BSB with its marks, the Hebrew, the Vulgate and 1738 (`text/orig/1KI.usfm`).

**Words.** A script compared the preview's words with 1738's, folding case, macrons, æ/ae, i/j and u/v, and checked
each difference against the approved rules of `data/rules/orthography.tsv`. Every difference is explained by an
approved rule or a listed draft fix (`1kgs1-22-draft-fixes.md`):
- **Draft fixes:** the marks dropped at 1:8, 2:12 and 2:28; 2:26 *Anathotha*; 3:1 *interea*; 3:2 *Quibus*;
  4:9 *Bethsemem*; and 4:33 *Disseruit … disseruit*, which now matches 1738.
- **Rule changes:** *quum → cum, pulcr- → pulchr-, audivisti → audisti, eis → iis, sumta → sumpta, adfers → affers,
  maledixit → male dixit, adsedit → assedit, adgressus → aggressus, adgredere, adloquitur, adparuit, adfecisti,
  adparet, adfirmat, audiverunt, audiverant, audivisset*.

I found no omission or misreading. Every punctuation difference from 1738 has a reader's layout row, or is one of the
dropped marks.

**Spelling gaps: four words need a rule** (decision 37: *ad-* assimilated, except compounds of *sum* and the five
verbs of `ad-unassimilated.md`). The preview keeps 1738's *ad-* in these four, though related forms are already
assimilated:
- 2:29 ***adgrediātur***. The approved rules give *aggrediantur, aggredi, aggreditur* …, and the preview has
  *aggredere* 2:31 and *aggressus* 2:25, 2:34, 2:46. Proposed: *adgrediatur → aggrediatur*.
- 3:1 ***adfīnitātem***. The settled text has *affīnitātem* (2×), and *adfinis → affinis* is approved. Proposed:
  *adfinitatem → affinitatem*.
- 3:24 ***Adlātōque***. The approved rules include *adlatam → allatam* and *adlaturus → allaturus*, and the settled
  text has only *allāt-*. Proposed: *adlatoque → allatoque*.
- 1:33 ***Adsūmite***. The settled text has only *assūm-* (*assūmptō* 13×, *assūmētur*, *assūmpsit*). Proposed:
  *adsumite → assumite*.

One doubt for Nathan:
- 1:2 ***adsectātōrēs***. Gaffiot and Wiktionary give *assectātor*. *Adsector* belongs to *adsequor*, which
  Nathan kept unassimilated, and the approved stem rule *assecl- → adsecul-* also keeps *ad-*. Nathan should decide
  whether *adsectātor* goes with those words or with decision 37.

*adfuit* 1:37 and *adsint* 5:6 are compounds of *sum*, so they stay. *adsurgit* 2:19 follows the approved *adsurg-*
rule.

**Macrons: 6 forms rows** (`p1-forms.tsv`):
- 1:8, 1:49, 2:19 ***Adōniā***: ablative after *ab* and *dē*. The preview had *Adōnia*. The first declension's
  ablative is long even with a short nominative *-as*, as the settled *dē Ēlīā* (Rom 11:2) and *ā Gabā*.
- 2:42 ***responderīs***: a perfect subjunctive after *cum*, which r1 also read it as. The settled text marks
  perfect subjunctives *-erīs* (*fēcerīs*, *interfēcerīs*, *dīxerīs* 2 Sam 1:16) and future perfects *-eris*. This
  row overrides r1's short *-eris*.
- 5:4 ***īnfestum***: the e is short, as the settled 1 Sam 26:19 *īnfestum*. The preview had *īnfēstum*.
- 5:6 ***dīxeris***: a future perfect beside *solvam* (Hebrew תֹּאמֵר). The preview had the subjunctive's
  *dīxerīs*.

I checked every other word in context:
- **Ablatives:** *eā, tubā, magnā cōpiā, sūmptā … ampullā, dextrā, Gethā, mediā nocte, lūce mātūtīnā,
  præfectūrā, ā Tipsā*.
- **Perfects against presents:** *vēnit* 1:28 and 2:13 against *venit* 2:30 (historic present); *sēdit* 1:46;
  *cōnsēdit, resēdit, assēdit* 2:12–19; *interēmit* 2:25; *fōvit* 1:4; *fīnierant* 1:41.
- ***hīc/hic*:** 1:31, 1:45, 3:26.
- **Other forms:** *dissilīret* 1:40 (Gaffiot *-īre*); *parietinīs* 4:33 (Gaffiot *parietĭnæ*; only the ending
  long); *sitīs* 4:13; *subāctīs* 5:3; *quiētē* 2:6 (the adverb, settled 7×); *petiistī* 3:11 (the settled
  *petiistis* Acts 3:14, so no rule is needed); *æreīs* 4:13 (settled); *significāveris* 5:9 (future perfect).

`macron-check` on 1–5 flags only settled conventions: *Jōva, Jōāb-, Sadōc-, Āzariās, usque, corōs*.

**Layout: no rows.**
- **Quotation marks:** balanced throughout (checked by script), with no nested ‘…’.
- **Reported speech:** unmarked at 1:2, 1:13, 1:52, 2:26, 2:36–38, 3:5 and 5:5.
- ***inquit* set off:** 1:31, 1:42–43, 2:17–23, 2:31, 2:44, 3:22–27.
- **Paragraphs:** each opens at a Castellio sentence start. The largest are 1:41–53 and 3:3b–14 (240 and 244 words),
  so none needs a split.
- **Other:** the 3:1 chapter break mid-sentence (*Cōnfirmātō rēgnō, / Solomō …*) and the paragraph inside 3:3 at
  *Cumque* are right as r2 set them. The officials and prefects of chapter 4 are prose, as 2 Sam 23.

**Left as they stand:**
- 1:16 *quid habēret?*: 1738's question mark on an indirect question, as in 2 Samuel.
- 3:26 *Quæsō domine*: no comma, as printed.
- 5:7–8–10: three short paragraphs (25, 49 and 51 words). They follow 1738's indent at 5:8 and the BSB's break at
  5:10. r2's doubt, merging them into one paragraph of about 125 words, is a fair alternative for Nathan.

No printer's slips were found beyond the draft fixes.

Rows: 0 layout, 6 forms.
