# r5: 1 Kings 10–12 (verse reader, 2026-11-22)

105 verses (10:1–29, 11:1–43, 12:1–33) read beside the draft, the preview, the KJV, the BSB (layout), the Hebrew and
1738's printed text (`text/orig/1KI.usfm`). Rows: **29 layout** (`r5-layout.tsv`), **75 forms** (`r5-forms.tsv`). Every
layout row was built by script from the draft's own verse, with only quotation marks, capitals, `\p` and joined line
breaks added, and each row's words checked against the draft's. I compared the draft's words with the printed text
verse by verse (u/v, i/j and æ/ae folded). The only differences are the three fixes already applied (11:5 marks,
11:33 *Melcomum*, 11:38 *tibi*).

- **Quoted**: the queen of Sheba's words 10:6–9 (after *rēgem sīc alloquitur:*), with a capital at *Utique*. Jova's
  threat 11:11–13 (after *in hunc modum:*), *Quoniam*. Ahijah's words 11:31–39 (*“Cape partēs decem; nam ita tibi
  significat Jōva …: ‘Ego sum …’”*), with Jova's message inside them in ‘…’, closing together at 11:39 (’”).
  Rehoboam's answer 12:14 (*“Meus pater …”*). Jeroboam's thought 12:26–27 (*sīc sēcum cōgitāre cœpit: “Jam rēgnum …”*),
  quoted as 1 Sam 16:6. **Reported and left unmarked** where the BSB quotes: 11:2 (the prohibition), 11:21–22 (Hadad
  and Pharaoh), 12:4–11 (the people, the elders, the young men), 12:5, 12:16 (the people's cry, *nihil jam sibi jūris
  in Dāvīde … superesse*, so the BSB's four lines of verse are not set as poetry: 2 Sam 20:1 was direct speech), 12:24
  (Shemaiah), 12:28 (Jeroboam to the people).
- **Paragraphs**: the draft's kept at 10:1, 10:14, 11:1, 11:9, 11:14, 11:23, 11:26, 11:41, 12:1, 12:16, 12:20 and 12:25.
  I added two at 1738's indents, 10:21 and 12:31 (the BSB breaks at both), and split three for length (decision 52),

  becomes 51 + 241) and 12:12 (12:1–15, 274, becomes 208 + 66). 1738 has no indent at 10:14, 11:9, 11:23, 11:41 or
  12:20 (the BSB's headings, kept).
- **Mid-verse line breaks** removed: 11:2, 11:24, 11:25, 11:27, 11:29, 12:5, 12:14, 12:16, 12:18, 12:24, 12:32. The
  BSB's breaks inside 11:27, 12:16 and 12:24 were not taken, because 1738 has no indent there.
- **Capitals** (adjectives of peoples and names, as the settled text): 10:22 *Cilica*, *Hīrāmicā*; 11:1 *Mōabītidas,
  Ammonītidas, Idūmæās, Sīdōniās, Hettæās*; 11:14 *Idūmæī*; 11:17 *Idūmæīs*; 11:24 *Damascēnum*; 12:20 *Dāvīdicam*;

  *lēctōrum* 12:21. Names from the settled text (*Hīrāmī, Ophīre, Sophēnōs, Eliādæ, Mellōnem, Jōsēphēōrum,
  Adōrāmum*) or from the Hebrew vowel (*Astōrethum, Chamōsum, Melcōmum, Razōn(em), Nabāthī/Nabātī, Sarēda, Sīlōniō/-um*).
  Left unmarked, with no source and no long Hebrew vowel to go on: *Taphnis/Taphne*, *Genubathum*, *Ahias*, *Semejæ*,
  *Pharanem* (as settled) and *Sesacum* (as settled).
- **Misprints and slips**: 11:38 *tib* and 11:33 *Mclcomum* are corrected in the draft. **12:21 *beniamiticam***: the
  draft keeps 1738's i for consonantal j (every other word has j). It should be *benjamiticam*, to go into §3 of the
  draft fixes. My layout row keeps the draft's spelling (*Beniamiticam*), and the form row gives *Benjamīticam*. No u/v
  slips, no word split in two and no ſ read as f in 10–12.

## Doubts

- 10:1 *vēnit*: perfect, with the Hebrew, though *adit* and *prōpōnit* (10:2) are historic presents. *venit* is
  possible.
- 10:16 *insumtis*: printed so, and kept. The settled text has *īnsūmpt-*, but no approved rule covers it. Claude
  might add a rule.
- 11:26 *Nabathi* and 12:2, 12:15 *Nabati*: Castellio's own inconsistency, each kept as printed.
- 12:6 *respondendum?* and 12:9 *aliquid?*: 1738 ends these indirect questions with question marks. Left as printed.
- Forms no source knows, built from their stems: *accubātiōnem*, *extorsūrus*, *sacellānōs*, *decimōquīntō*,
  *decimāquīntā*.
- 10:27 *aurī* (the Hebrew has silver) is left for the notes, as asked.
