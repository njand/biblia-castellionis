## Doubts

- **2:26 *Anathota*** (1738 *vt Anathota in prædium suum*): spelled without *h* against the settled *Anāthōtha*
  (Josh 21:18); and the case: read as an ablative of place (*Anāthōtā*, at Anathoth, to his estate), though an
  accusative of goal would be *Anāthōtam*. For the names reader: whether to spell *Anāthōtha* here.
- **1:17 *tu mihi tuæ*** (1738 as printed): *tuæ* is the dative of your handmaid (Hebrew לַאֲמָתֶךָ), Castellio's
  ellipsis; not a slip, but terse.
- **2:46**: 2:46b *Cōnfirmātō rēgnō,* (comma kept) runs into 3:1; for the reader of ch. 3.
- No printer's slips found in 1–2 beyond those already fixed (1:8's and 2:12's, 2:28's marks taken out). The draft's
  spellings *adsurgit … assēdit* (2:19 preview), *male dīxit* (2:8 preview, the draft's *maledixit*) come from the
  approved rules, not from this reading.
