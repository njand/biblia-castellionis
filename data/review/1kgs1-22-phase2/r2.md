## Printer's slips and draft slips (words not changed)

- **3:2 *Qui bus*** (1738 prints *Qui bus*, two words; the draft and the preview carry it, the preview as *Quī būs*):
  the word *Quibus* split by the printer. To join as *Quibus* (draft-fixes style), with the macron form *Quibus*.
- **4:33 *Disservit … disservit*** in the draft: 1738 prints *Disseruit … disseruit* (perfect of *disserō*); the
  draft's u/v conversion made it *Disservit*. Should be *Disseruit … disseruit* (no macrons: *disseruit*).
- 3:1 *intera* already fixed (draft-fixes 1). No other slips seen in 3–5.

## Doubts

- **5:8 and 5:10 paragraphs**: 5:8 follows 1738's indent (Hiram's letter set apart), which leaves 5:10–12's narrative
  needing its own paragraph (the BSB's break). If a letter after a colon should stay with its introduction, drop both
  and keep 5:7–12 whole (150 words).
- **3:22 *inquit;***: 1738's semicolon marks the contrast *meus … tuus*; made a comma by decision 65's form; a
  semicolon after the closing mark (*inquit; “tuus …”*) would keep it, if preferred.
- **3:27 *Tum rēx: “Dētur …,” inquit***: the colon kept beside the set-off *inquit* (both speech markers, as 1738).
