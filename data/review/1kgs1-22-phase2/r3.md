# r3: 1 Kings 6–7 (verse reader, 2026-11-22)

text (`text/orig/1KI.usfm`, the read lines `data/witnesses/read-1738/1Kgs1-22.tsv`). Rows: **23 layout**
(`r3-layout.tsv`), **52 forms** (`r3-forms.tsv`). Every layout row's words and every form checked against the draft

## Printer's slips and draft slips (words not changed)

- **6:38 *at que***: 1738 prints *at* at the end of leaf 397, col. 1, l. 99 (after the margin's *#38*) and *que*
  at the start of the next line, with no hyphen read; the draft and the printed text carry *at que*. It is *atque*
  (the versification note itself quotes *atque ita*).
- **7:51 *confecratum*, *repofuit***: the read lines give *confe-* / *cratum* and *repo-* / *fuit* (leaf 399, col. 1,
  ll. 99–103); the words are *consecratum*, *reposuit*, and 1738 very likely prints long ſ there, read as *f* (the
  rule is s for ſ). Not checked on the page image (none cached here): leaf 399 should be looked at, and
  `text/orig/1KI.usfm` corrected by proposal if so; the draft needs the same.
- **6:34 *valuas***: the u/v step's slip, as r2's *disservit*: 1738 *valuas* is *valvās* (folding doors; Gaffiot
  *valvæ*); the draft and the preview (*valuās*) carry *valuas*.
- **7:34 *humeri***: the draft's *humeri* beside 7:30's *humeros* (the preview *umerōs*, by the approved rule
  *humeros → umeros*); the settled text has only *umer-* (*umerīs* 14×, *umerōs* 3×), so a rule *humeri → umeri* is
  wanted (the preview keeps *humerī*).
- **7:48 *adposititii***: the settled *apposititiōs* is assimilated; decision 37 would give *apposititiī* here.
- The brief's 6:8 *lanuas* and 6:27 *in templa* are right in the draft (*Januas*, *templo*; draft-fixes 1–2).

## Doubts

- 6:21–22: the full stop (keeping 1738's capital) against the preview's lower case *idque*; either reads.
- 7:1: the full stop before *Quā*; a colon with lower case *quā* would also do (r2 lowered capitals after colons).
- 6:4 *apertilēs*, 6:5 *adstruēbat*, 7:6 *columnātam*, *coassātiōne*, 7:48 *adposititiī*: no source but Gaffiot or
  the lexicon's kin forms; marked by analogy.
