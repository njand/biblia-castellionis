## Doubts

- 16:8 and 17:8: I moved the BSB's headings back to the sentence starts (16:6, 17:7). If Claude prefers the BSB's verse,
  the alternative is `\p` before the clause, as at the moved chapter breaks.
- 17:18 *Venistī ad mē: ut … coarguerēs*: Castellio's colon before the purpose clause kept; a comma would read more easily.
- *Jezabēl* nominative (18:4): I gave it the long e of the settled *Jezabēlem*; the Hebrew אִיזֶבֶל has segol, so the
  settled ē itself may deserve a look.
- Spelling (rules, not mine to change): the preview has *adpellāte … appellābō* side by side at 18:24, and *adferentibus*
  17:6 beside *afferat* 17:11, *adpāreō* 17:1 beside *appāreō* 18:15 (as printed): the ad- rules reach some forms only.
- 18:25: the split for length could fall at 18:20 instead (*Igitur arcessīvit*), giving 62 + 304; 18:25 balances better.
