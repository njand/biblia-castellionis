# 1 Kings 10–15: proofreader p3 (2026-11-22)

I read every verse of 10–15 (204 verses) in `text/draft/1KI.preview.usfm` (built 11:34 from the merged decision
files) against the BSB with its layout, the Hebrew, the Vulgate and 1738 (`text/orig/1KI.usfm`), as p1 and p2 did for
1–9.

**Words.** A script compared the preview's words with 1738's, folding case, macrons, æ/ae, i/j and u/v, and applied
the approved word, stem and phrase rules of `data/rules/orthography.tsv` to 1738's side. Every difference is an
approved rule or a listed draft fix (`1kgs1-22-draft-fixes.md`):
- **Draft fixes:** the marks dropped at 11:5 (*L. … H.*) and 15:6 (*L. Abiamum H.*); 11:33 *Melcōmum* (*Mclcomum*);
  11:38 *tibi* (*tib*); 14:10 *fimus* (*ſimus*); 12:21 *Benjamīticam*; the names *Chamōssum/Chamōssō* 11:5, 11:7,
  11:33 and *Nabātī* 11:26.
- **Rule changes:** *quum → cum* (and *quumque*), *caussa → causa*, *eis → iis* (*eisque*), *adloquitur → alloquitur*,
  *tentandum → temptandum*, *audiveram → audieram*, *adseclæ → adseculæ*, *sexcent- → sescent-*, *adferrent →
  afferrent*, *petiit → petīvit*, *obedīveris → obœdīveris*, *adscendere → ascendere* 12:18, *audivissent → audīssent*,
  *simulac → simul ac* 14:12, *quinetiam → quīn etiam*, *quatuor → quattuor*.

I found no omission or misreading. Every punctuation difference from 1738 is a reader's row with its reason (13:3,
13:6, 14:25) or a dropped mark (11:5, 15:6).

**Spelling gaps: words that need a rule.** The preview keeps 1738's spelling in these, though the approved rules
change their neighbours:
- 10:16 ***īnsūmtīs*** → *īnsūmptīs* (the rules give *insumta → insumpta*, *sumto, sumti* …; the settled text has only
  *sūmpt-*).
- 13:22 ***sūmsistī*** → *sūmpsistī* (the rules give *sumsi, sumsit, sumsisse* …; the settled *sūmpsistis*).
- 12:33 ***adscendisset*** → *ascendisset* (the rules give *adscendissent, adscendissem, adscendissetis*; 12:18 has the
  rule-made *ascendere*).
- 11:18 ***adscītīsque*** → *ascītīsque* (the rules give *adscitis → ascitis*; the settled *ascītīs* 3×).
- 11:9 ***adpāruerat*** → *appāruerat*, as p2's 9:2 (the settled *appāruerat* 2×).
- 10:22 ***adportābat*** → *apportābat* (decision 37; Gaffiot heads *apportō*; no settled form and no rule either way).
- 12:32 ***decimōquīntō*** → *decimō quīntō*, 12:33 ***decimāquīntā*** → *decimā quīntā*: the approved *quartodecimo →
  quarto decimo* and *septimodecimo → septimo decimo*; the settled text writes every such ordinal apart (*quīntō
  decimō*, *sextō decimō* …).

These stay, as they should: *adseculæ* 10:8 (the approved *adsecul-*), *adstantēs, adstābant, adstiterant, adstārent,
adstantem, adstantibus* (*adstō*), *adsignāverat* 11:18 (*adsignō*), *adfuit* 12:3 and *adfore* 14:5 (*sum*);
*arundinis* 14:15 (the settled *arundin-*).

**Macrons: 15 forms rows** (`p3-forms.tsv`):
- 11:9 ***Jōva*** (#1): the first copy is the subject of *minātus est*; r5's *ā Jōvā* row made both copies ablative.
- 12:15 ***Jōvā***: ablative absolute *ita dispēnsante Jōvā*.
- 15:21 ***audītīs***: ablative absolute *Quibus audītīs* (the preview had the verb *audītis*).
- 15:19 ***Baasā***: after *cum*, as r7's 16:1. 11:26 ***Sarēdā***: after *ā*. 11:23 ***Adarezerō*** (after *ab*) and
  15:18 ***Benadadō*** (dative): the second declension's long *-ō*.
- 13:1 ***supervēnit***: perfect, the main verb after 12:33's *cum*-clause, beside *clāmāvit* (r6's note already reads
  it so).
- 14:8–9 ***fuerīs, antecesserīs, dēvēnerīs, fēcerīs***: perfect subjunctives in *tantum abest ut … ut*, as the
  settled *fidēlis fuerīs* (Matt 25:21) and p1's *responderīs*.
- 15:8 ***mānēs***: the shades (Gaffiot *Mānēs*), not *manēs* "you stay".
- 10:22 ***pāvōs***: Gaffiot *pāvus*. 14:4 ***cæcūtientis***: Gaffiot *cæcūtiō*.

I checked every other word in context:
- **Ablatives:** *audītā … fāmā, Hīrāmicā, Hierosolymā, concessā … ūnā tribū, tōtā, illā, eā, eādem … viā, quā,
  hāc, Īsæidā, integrā, omissā, diffissā ārā*.
- **Perfects against presents:** *vēnit* 10:1 and 12:21; *vēnī* 10:7, 13:9; *vēnerant* 11:18, 11:24; *aufūgit*,
  *profūgit* 11:40, *fūgerat*; the presents *capit, discerpit* 11:30, *interficit* 11:24, *invenit* 13:28 (beside
  *mandat, tollit*).
- **Future perfects:** *obœdīveris, fueris* 11:38, *immīseris* 14:12, *concesserint* 12:27.
- ***hīc/hic*:** *Hīc* 11:29 and 13:6, *hīc* 12:28; *Hic* 11:15, 15:7, 15:11, *hic* 14:13.
- **Fourth declension:** *comitātū, gradūs, manūs, domūs saltūs, currūs, suffītūs, tribūs, arbitrātū, conventūs,
  exercitūs, cursū, quercū, lūctū*.
- **Other forms:** *trīnæ, sescentīs, centēnīs quīnquāgēnīs, quadrīgāriīs, pōtōria, fastīgium, prōvēnerat, exāruit,
  exscidit, retrāxerat, fimus* (Gaffiot *fĭmum*), *rūrī*.

`macron-check` on 10–15 flags only settled conventions (*Jōv-, Jōāb-, Ammonīt-, Bēthēl, Dane, usque, quæsō*) and
*īnfestāvit* 11:25 (Gaffiot *īnfēstō*), which I left: the edition marks *īnfestus* short (p1's 5:4, the settled 1 Sam
26:19), and the verb follows it.

**Layout: 2 rows** (`p3-layout.tsv`):
- 14:25: *Sesacus, Ægyptī rēx, expedītiōnem fēcit*. r6's comma opened the apposition and left it open.
- 15:4: *Jōva, Deus ejus, eī lucernam concessit*. 1738 opens the apposition and leaves it open.

The rest is right as the readers set it:
- **Quotation marks:** balanced. The queen 10:6–9; Jova 11:11–13; Ahijah 11:31–39 with Jova's message in ‘…’; Rehoboam
  12:14; Jeroboam's thought 12:26–27; the man of God 13:2–3, 13:6, 13:7, 13:8–9; the old prophet 13:21–22 with ‘…’;
  Ahijah 14:6–16, Jova's words in ‘…’ 14:7–11, the quotation reopened at the paragraph 14:12; Asa 15:19.
- **Reported speech unmarked:** 11:2, 11:21–22, 12:4–11, 12:16, 12:24, 12:28, 13:14–18, 13:26, 13:31–32, 14:2–3, 14:5.
- ***inquit* set off:** 13:3, 13:6.
- **Paragraphs:** every 1738 indent is kept (10:1, 10:21, 11:1, 11:14, 11:26, 12:1, 12:16, 12:25, 12:31, 13:1, 13:11,
  14:1, 14:21, 14:25, 15:1, 15:16). The others open Castellio sentences at BSB breaks. The longest paragraphs are
  11:29–40 (241 words), 13:11–22 (225), 13:1–10 (217) and 12:1–11 (208), so none needs a split.
- **Chapter breaks:** 13:1 and 15:1 are set as r6 did, on 2 Samuel's model.
- **Poetry:** none. 14:10–11 stays prose where the BSB sets verse (r6's reason).

**Left as they stand:**
- 12:6, 12:9, 13:14: 1738's question marks on indirect questions, as p1's 1:16.
- 10:1 *audītā Solomōnis fāmā, ad Jōvæ nōmen vēnit*: the comma as printed. The Hebrew joins the fame to the name
  (לְשֵׁם יְהוָה), so the comma after *fāmā* may be 1738's. I kept it, and it is noted for Nathan.
- *Adarezerō* and *cæcūtientis* differ from settled forms (2 Sam *Adarezero* 4×, 2 Pet 1:9 *cæcutiēns*). Those need a
  proposal, or Nathan's call, to match.

No printer's slips were found beyond the draft fixes.

Rows: 2 layout, 15 forms.
