## Spelling for Claude (not a form row)

- **The preview's *ad-/ap-* is inconsistent**: 9:2 *appāruit* but *adpāruerat* in the same verse, and 8:8
  *adpārērent* (the draft *adparuit*, *adparuerat*, *adparerent*). The settled text has only *appār-*; a rule seems
  to catch *adparuit* alone.
- 8:41 the preview splits the draft's *Quinetiam* into *Quīn etiam* (a spelling rule); the layout row keeps the
  draft's *Quinetiam*.
