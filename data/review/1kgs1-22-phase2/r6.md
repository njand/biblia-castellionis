# r6: 1 Kings 13–15 (verse reader, 2026-11-22)

99 verses (13:1–34, 14:1–31, 15:1–34) read beside the draft, the preview, the KJV, the BSB (layout), the Hebrew and
1738's printed text (`text/orig/1KI.usfm`). Rows: **33 layout** (`r6-layout.tsv`), **70 forms** (`r6-forms.tsv`). Every
layout row was built by script from the draft's own verse, with only quotation marks, capitals, stops, `\p` and joined
line breaks changed, and each row's words were checked against the draft's. I compared the draft's words with the
printed text verse by verse (u/v, i/j and æ/ae folded). The only difference is 15:6, where Castellio's *L. … H.* marks
around *Abiamum* have been taken out (for the notes).

- **The moved chapter breaks**: at **13:1** the draft's `\p` is kept, as 2 Sam 20:1 and r2's 3:1. 12:33 *Cum autem ad
  āram … adscendisset:* (1738's chapter opening) runs into *supervēnit*, with 12:33's colon and 13:1's lower case kept.
  I wrote no row for 12:33, since nothing changes there. At **15:1** the same: the `\p` is kept and *quī annō
  duodēvīcēsimō* stays lower case. 14:31b *Huic in rēgnum successit Abiamus ejus fīlius,* keeps its comma (as 2 Sam
  19:43, r1's 2:46), and the draft's line break before it is removed.
- **Quoted**: the man of God 13:2–3 (*“Āra, āra, …”*; Jova's message reported with *fore, ut*, so there is no inner
  quotation), with 13:3's parenthesis *(inquit, tum prōdigium dāns)* set off by commas and the quotation closed and
  reopened, as 2 Sam 1:15. Jeroboam 13:6 (*adloquitur, “Ōrā, quæsō,” inquit, “…”*: the colon becomes a comma, decision
  65) and 13:7. The man of God's answer 13:8–9. The old prophet 13:21–22 (*“Hæc est Jōvæ sententia: ‘Quoniam tū …’”*).
  Ahijah 14:6–16, with Jova's words in ‘…’ for 14:7–11 (*hæc est … sententia: ‘Quandoquidem ego tē …’*, as r5's 11:31),
  closing before *Nam Jōva locūtus est*. Asa's message 15:19. **Reported and left unmarked** where the BSB quotes:
  13:4, 13:9's command, 13:12, 13:13, 13:14–18, 13:26, 13:27, 13:31–32, 14:2–3, 14:5. 13:30 has no lament's words,
  so there is nothing to quote.
- **Paragraphs**: the draft's kept at 13:1, 13:11, 14:1, 14:19, 14:21, 14:25, 15:1, 15:16, 15:23, 15:25 and 15:33.
  1738 indents 13:1, 13:11, 14:1, 14:21, 14:25, 15:1 and 15:16; the rest are the BSB's headings. I split for length
  (decision 52), each where the BSB breaks and a sentence begins: 13:11–34 (469 words) at **13:23** and **13:33**, giving
  225, 198 and 46; 14:1–18 (377) at **14:6** and **14:12**, giving 86, 163 and 128. 14:12 falls inside Ahijah's speech,
  so the quotation reopens there (1 Sam 2:31). **15:9**: the draft's `\p` sat before a relative clause (*Huic in rēgnum
  successit Asa fīlius ejus, \p quī vīcēsimō …*). I moved it inside 15:8, to *Huic*, where the sentence begins, as 2 Sam
  5:11–12, and 15:9 now has no paragraph (`-`).
- **Poetry**: none. The BSB sets 14:10–11 as verse, but Castellio turns the threat into one result clause (*ut ex
  Jeroboāmicīs nihil … sit, quīn omnia … exstirpem … ; ita ut … dēvorentur*) without the paired lines. Kept as prose.
- **Mid-verse line breaks** removed: 13:14, 13:24, 13:27, 13:30, 14:2, 14:10, 14:13, 14:31, 15:8, 15:10 and 15:30.
- **Capitals and stops**: 14:8 *Dāvīdicæ*; 14:21 *Isrāēlīticīs*; capitals at each speech's start. 14:25: 1738's
  *Sesacus; Ægyptī rēx* has a semicolon between subject and apposition, made a comma.
- **Forms** (the preview's errors):
  - Ablatives: *prōtēnsā* and *audītīs* 13:4; *diffissā*, *eā* 13:5; *hōc* 13:8; *Jōvā* 13:9 and 13:17; *eādem*
    13:9, 13:10, 13:17 (unflagged); *Hōc*, *aliā* 13:10; *audītā* 13:26; *Abiā* 14:1; *illā* 14:6; *tōtā* 14:8;
    *aquā*, *bonā terrā*, *lūcīs* 14:15; *eā* 14:17; *eā terrā* 14:24 and 15:12; *Hōc* 14:25; *integrā* 15:14;
    *postulātīs* 15:18; *eā*, *vītā* 15:29.
  - Nominatives and accusatives the preview made long: *āra* 13:2 (vocative, twice) and 13:3; *manus* 13:4 and 13:6
    (twice); *sua* 13:6 and 15:15; *commissa* 15:3.
  - *vēnī* 13:9 is a perfect, not an imperative. *mellisque* 14:3 has a short i. *modo* 13:33. *irrītandī* 14:9 and
    *irrītāverant* 14:22.
  - *āeris* 14:11: the draft's *aeris* (air) was turned by the preview into *æris*, a diphthong, which is the genitive
    of *æs*. The settled text has *āeris*.
  - *Ammonītis* 14:21 and 14:31: nominative feminine singular, not the ablative plural *Ammonītīs*.
  - Names: *Ābīam-* throughout (14:31, 15:7), from the settled *Ābīamum* and the preview's own 15:6. *Nabātī*,
    *Sīlōnium* as r5. *Rāmamque* as the settled form. From the Hebrew holem (decision 52): *Tabremmōnis*,
    *Heziōnis*, *Cenerōtha*, *Gabathōnem*. Built from their stems: *Jeroboāmicīs/-ōrum*, *sacellānōs*,
    *comminctōrem*, *pōtiōnemve*, *sūmsistī* (the draft's *sumsisti* kept, as r5's *insumtis*). Left unmarked, with no
    source and no long Hebrew vowel to go on: *Baasa*, *Benadadus*, *Ahias/Ahiam*, *Thersa*, *Naama*, *Sesacus* (as
    settled), *Maacha* (as settled).
- **Misprints and slips**: no u/v or i/j slip in the draft in 13–15 (15:20 *Iōnem*, Ijon עִיּוֹן, is a vowel I, rightly
  kept), and no word split in two. **14:10 *simus***: both reads of 1738 (`read-1738/1Kgs1-22.corrections.tsv` row
  2482, voted rows 412 and 414) give *quam simus prōrsus abstergī solet*. The sense needs *fimus*, dung (Hebrew הַגָּלָל;
  KJV *as a man taketh away dung*). Either 1738 prints long ſ for f, or the reading took an f for an ſ. No other
  witness has the verse. Worth a look at the page. If the page has *ſimus*, it is a printer's misprint for *fimus*, to
  be corrected in the edition. My forms row leaves it unmarked and keeps the word. The preview's *sīmus* (we may be) is
  wrong either way.

## Doubts

- 13:6 *Hīc rēx*: I took it as the adverb (thereupon), as r5's 11:29. *Hic rēx* (this king) is possible.
- 13:3: the parenthesis *(inquit, tum prōdigium dāns)* is set off by commas, as 2 Sam 1:15. Claude may prefer to keep
  Castellio's parentheses outside the quotation.
- 13:33 adds a paragraph of only 46 words, at the BSB's break and the story's end. It could instead run on from 13:23
  (244 words in all).
- 14:6 and 14:12: 14:6–16 would be 262 words, just over the measure. 1 Sam 2:27–36 (262) was split, so I split it.
- 15:8/15:9: I moved the paragraph to mid-verse. If Claude would rather keep the BSB's verse start, the alternative is
  `\p` at 15:9 before the relative clause, as at the chapter breaks.
- *Ābīamus*: the settled *Ābīamum* (a note at 2 Sam 14:27) has no long vowel in the Hebrew's אֲבִיָּם to support it
  except the qamets. I followed the settled form.
- 15:6 *inter Abiamum et Jeroboamum* (the Hebrew has Rehoboam) is left for the notes, as asked.
