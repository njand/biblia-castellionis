# 1 Kings 6–9: proofreader p2 (2026-11-22)

I read every verse of 6–9 in `text/draft/1KI.preview.usfm` (built 11:34 from the merged decision files) against the
BSB with its layout, the Hebrew, the Vulgate and 1738 (`text/orig/1KI.usfm`), as p1 did for 1–5.

**Words.** A script compared the preview's words with 1738's. It folded case, macrons, æ/ae, i/j and u/v, and checked
each difference against the approved word, stem and phrase rules of `data/rules/orthography.tsv`. Every difference is
explained by an approved rule or a listed draft fix (`1kgs1-22-draft-fixes.md`):
- **Draft fixes:** 6:8 *Jānuās* (1738 *lanuas*), 6:27 *in templō* (*in templa*), 6:34 *valvās*, 6:38 *atque*
  (*at que*), 7:51 *cōnsecrātum … reposuit*, and the capitals *Isrāēlītic-* 8:1, 8:16, 8:36, 8:52, 9:5 and
  *Solomōniānīs* 9:27.
- **Rule changes:** *quatuor → quattuor* (7:2, 7:27, 7:30, 7:32, 7:34), *quatuordecim*, *eis → iis*, *quemdam →
  quendam*, *septemtriones*, *humeros → umeros* 7:30, *cacabos → caccabos*, *quum → cum*, *quinetiam → quīn etiam*,
  *brachio → bracchiō*, *caussa → causa*, *adparente*, *adparuit*, *adfecerit*, *imo → immo* (see below).

I found no omission or misreading. The only punctuation and capital differences from 1738 are the draft fixes and the
readers' rows (6:12, 6:21–22, 6:38, 7:1, 8:12, 8:15–16, 8:56, 9:3, 9:13), each with its reason.

**A rule that misfires: 6:16 *ab immō*.** The approved word rule *imo → immo* (Gen 23:13) turns *ab imo ad summum*
("from bottom to top") into the adverb *immō*. r3's forms row *īmō* cannot apply while the rule rewrites the word,
so the preview still reads *ab immō*. It needs a phrase rule *ab imo → ab imo*, like the approved *in imo* (Exod 26:24).

**Spelling gaps: words that need a rule** (decision 37: *ad-* assimilated; Gaffiot heads *ascendō, appāreō, afflīgō,
afficiō, appōnō*). The preview keeps 1738's *ad-* in these, though their cousins are assimilated by approved rules:
- 6:8 ***adscendēbātur*** → *ascendēbātur* (the rules give *adscendebat → ascendebat* and many more forms).
- 7:48 ***adposititiī*** → *apposititiī* (the rules give *adposititios, -o, -orum*; the settled *apposititiōs*). r3
  left this to Claude.
- 8:8 ***adpārērent*** → *appārērent*; 9:2 ***adpāruerat*** → *appāruerat*. In 9:2 it stands beside the rule-made
  *appāruit*: *Jōva eī iterum appāruit, ut apud Gabaōnem adpāruerat*.
- 8:35 ***adflīgente*** → *afflīgente* (the rules give *adfligentibus, adfligat* …).
- 8:64, 9:25 ***adposita*** → *apposita* (the rules give *adpositus, adpositae, adposito* …).
- 8:66 ***adfēcerat*** → *affēcerat* (the rules give *adfecerit*, which is 9:8's *affēcerit*, and *adfecit* …).
- 7:34 ***humerī*** → *umerī*. 7:30 has the rule-made *umerōs* four verses earlier (the rules give *humeros, humeris*).

These stay, as they should: *adstruēbat* 6:5 (Gaffiot heads *adstruō*), *adliciat* 8:58 (Gaffiot heads *adliciō*),
*adsurgente* 6:26 (the approved *adsurg-* rule), *adstābat* 8:14 and *adstāns* 8:22 (*ast-* rejected), and *Adsit,
adfuit, adsint* 8:57–59 (compounds of *sum*).

**Macrons: 1 forms row** (`p2-forms.tsv`):
- 7:18 ***mālōrum***: *duōs mālōrum pūnicōrum ōrdinēs* (the pomegranates; Gaffiot *mālum* "apple", long a), as the
  verse's neighbours *mālogrānātōrum* 7:20 and *mālogrānāta* 7:42. The preview had *malōrum*, "of evils".

I checked every other word in context:
- **Ablatives:** *eā, ūnā, quā, suā, tōtā, terrā, plagā, basī, lacū, porticū, circuitū, versū, seriē, speciē*.
- **Fourth-declension nominatives and genitives:** *prōcessūs* 7:35, *porticūs* 7:8, *commeātūs* 9:19, *circuitus*
  7:15; the dative *ostentuī* 9:8.
- **Perfects and futures:** *vēnit* 7:14, *vēnerint* 8:42. Perfect subjunctives: *cōnservāverīs, præstiterīs* 8:24.
  Future perfects: *vīxeris* 6:12, *audīveris* 8:30, *mīseris* 8:44, *addīxeris* 8:46, *cōnservāveris* 9:4,
  *dēscīveritis*, *cōnservāveritis*, *contuleritis* 9:6.
- ***hīc*:** the adverb at 8:22.
- **Other forms:** *quāquā* 6:5, *ædēs* 6:17 (nominative singular), *nemorēnsem* 7:2, *mūtulōs* 7:9, *sīmō* 7:29,
  *aēneās/aēnea* 7:30, 7:38, 8:64, *fīunt* 7:33, *suffiēbat* 9:25 (Gaffiot *suffĭō*), *sībilent* 9:8, *usque*.

`macron-check` on 6–9 flags only settled conventions (*Jōv-*, *usque*, *æris*) and the reading *mediæ* 6:6 (not the
name *Mēdīæ*). Its unknown words are æ-forms and names, all as the names reader and the verse readers decided.

**Layout: no rows.**
- **Quotation marks:** balanced. God's word to Solomon 6:12–13. The saying 8:12–13 is set as verse on the BSB's lines.
  Solomon's address runs 8:15b–21. His prayer runs 8:23–53, reopened at each petition's paragraph (8:31, 33, 35, 37,
  41, 44, 46). The blessing runs 8:56–61, Jova's answer 9:3–9 and Hiram's question 9:13. 9:8–9's question and answer
  are reported inside Jova's words, unmarked.
- **Paragraphs:** every 1738 indent is kept: 6:1, 6:7, 7:1, 7:7, 7:13, 7:15, 7:22b (*Columnārum opere perfectō,*),
  7:27, 7:38, 8:1, 8:15b, 8:31–46, 8:54, 9:1, 9:10. The others (6:5, 6:11, 6:14, 6:23, 6:31, 6:36, 7:40, 7:48, 8:12,
  8:14 \m, 8:22, 8:62, 9:15, 9:24) fall at BSB breaks where a sentence of Castellio's begins. The longest paragraphs
  are 9:1–9 (204 words), 8:22–30 (199) and 7:27–37 (190), so none needs a split.

**Left as they stand:**
- The BSB sets 7:41–45 and 7:49–50 as lists (*li1*). The preview has them as prose in Castellio's sentences, as 2 Sam
  8:16–18.
- 6:9 *Templum exædificāns, atque absolvēns cedrō*: the comma as printed.

No printer's slips were found beyond the draft fixes.
