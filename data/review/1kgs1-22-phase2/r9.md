# r9: 1 Kings 22 (verse reader, 2026-11-22)

(`text/orig/1KI.usfm`, with the read lines of leaves 427–429). Rows: **23 layout** (`r9-layout.tsv`), **15 forms**

## Doubts

- **22:34 *lorieam*** (the draft and the approved printed text, from the read line *lori-* / *eam*, leaf 428 col. 0
  line 53 and col. 1 line 55, a footnote row between): surely *loricam* (the breastplate, the KJV's *harness*; other
  witnesses in the repo print *loricam* elsewhere, e.g. *æream loricam induerat*). Either 1738 misprints *c* as *e* or the reader
  took *c* for *e*: the page should be checked; the word is left unchanged and unmarked.
- **22:15 *venit***: the preview's present (*ubi ad rēgem venit, rēx eum sīc interrogat*, historic present beside
  *interrogat*) kept; *vēnit* (perfect after *ubi*) is equally possible.
- **22:18, 22:20**: question marks proposed for 1738's full stops (above); a reviewer may prefer Castellio's stops.
- **22:13–28**: one paragraph of 289 words, longer than 2 Samuel's measure; a break at 22:19 (*Et Michæās: ergō accipe
  Jōvæ sententiam*) is possible but has no indent in 1738 and no heading in the BSB.
- **22:42 *Azubam*** (עֲזוּבָה, šureq): perhaps *Azūbam* by decision 52's Hebrew vowel; left unmarked as the other names.
- 22:34 *adfectum* → *affectum* and 22:18 *adloquens* → *alloquēns* by the ad- rules, but 22:38 *adlueretur* and 22:12
  *adgrederētur* kept: the rules reach some forms only (as r8 noted).
