## Doubts

- **19:16 *Nansis*** (1738 *Nansis*, as the draft) for Nimshi (Hebrew נִמְשִׁי, Vulgate *Namsi*): perhaps a misprint for
  *Namsis*; kept, for 2 Kings 9 (*Jehu … fīlius Nansis*?) to confirm.
- **20:14**: an alternative is to quote the prophet's first answer too (*“Per prætōrum cālōnēs,” hoc fore prōmittit
  Jōva*), but the sentence is Castellio's narration.
- **20:5**: the envoys' preface *Hæc est Benadadī sententia* made a colon before the inner quotation (1738's full stop);
  *redierunt lēgātī cum hujusmodī mandātīs.* keeps its full stop before the opening mark.
- **21:23–24**: inside Elijah's speech, as the BSB and the Hebrew; read alone, *Dē Jezabēle quoque sīc Jōva prōnūntiat*
  could be the narrator's.
- **21:28 *Thesbitā***: only the ending marked; the stem's vowels (תִּשְׁבִּי, *Thesbītēs*?) left as the earlier readers.
- Spelling (rules, not mine): the preview has *adficit* 20:21 and *adficiam*→*afficiam* 21:29 side by side, *adloquitur*
  →*alloquitur* 20:34 but *adripientēs* 20:33 kept: the ad- rules reach some forms only.
