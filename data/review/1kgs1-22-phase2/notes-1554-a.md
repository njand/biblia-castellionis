**Kind (a) in substance, left out by the rule:** the *cypress* of 6:23 and 6:31–33 (*cupreſſum uerti, ſecutus Græcum*)
is a deliberate choice of the Greek over the Hebrew's "oil tree", which the NRSVue and the BSB render "olive wood" with
no note. It touches only the material of the cherubim and the doors: dropped by the rule of 2026-11-19 (a material,
and the meaning of a tree, which Castellio himself called guesswork). Nothing proposed.

**The ch. 5 heading (d).** 1554 prints *Cupreſsina materia.)* under *EX CAP. V.*, before *EX CAP. VI.*, but the
words are 6:23's (*In penetrali faciebat duos Cherubos cupreſsina materia*, canvas 190, column 356) and 6:31's
(*cupreſsinas … fores*): ch. 5 has *cedrina pineaq;* only. 1738's asterisk stands at 6:23 (*materia,\* in altitudinem*,
reader 05). A misplaced annotation, not a different text. For `docs/editions.md`.

No annotation on chapters 1–4, 8–9 or 11: 1554 has nothing on 1:8, 2:12, 2:28, 11:5 (the marked readings) or 10:27
(*auri*).

## 3. The 1554 text's own margins, and 1738's footnotes

1738 prints four lettered footnotes in chapters 1–11 (`%` rows of `data/witnesses/read-1738/1Kgs1-22.tsv`). **All four
are 1554's marginal glosses**:

| Verse | 1738 (`read-1738/1Kgs1-22.tsv`) | 1554 margin (canvas, column) |
|---|---|---|
| 4:13 | *b) Gaulonim vocat Iosephus.* | *a Gaulo-\|nim uocat \| Ioſephus.* (189, 353) |
| 6:7 | *c) quadrabantur in lapicidinis lapides, quo minores aducherentur.* | *a quadra-\|bãtur in la\|picidinis la\|pides, quo \| minores ad \| ueherẽtur.* (190, 355) |
| 11:5 | *a) idem mox Molochus adpellatur.* | *a idẽ mox \| Molochus \| appellatur.* (194, 364); 1573 prints it (canvas 238, search API, *Molochus*) |
| 11:36 | *b) posteritatem.* | *b poſteri-\|tatem.* (195, 366) |

1738's *aducherentur* (6:7) is a misprint of 1554's *aduehe-|rentur*. At 11:36 the gloss hangs on 1554's *lumen*
(*ut ᵇlumen habeat Dauid meus*, canvas 195, column 366), where 1738 prints *vt lucernam habeat*: a later revision of
the text, the gloss kept. The other margins of canvases 186–195 are side-heads (*Abiſaga Dauidis fotrix*, *Adonias
regnum affectat* …) and cross-references; I saw no other lettered gloss in these chapters that 1738 lacks. None of the
four meets the bar (`notes-a.md` §D).

## 4. Castellio's *G.*, *L.* … *H.* marks in 1554

The marks the 1738 reading found in chapters 1–11 are printed in **1554**'s text, in small capitals:

- 1:8 *& Se-|meis, & Reis, L. & Cerethæi & Phelethæi H. | heroesq; Dauidis ab Adonia non ſtabant.* (canvas 186, column
  348, 1200 px).
- 2:12 *At Solomo in ſolio Dauidis patris ſui | cõſedit, G.natus annos 12, H. eſtq; eius regnum* (canvas 187, column
  350, zoom `z187-2_12.jpg`): the number in figures, where 1738 has *duodecim*.
- 2:28 *qui ad A-|donaim, non ad G.L Solomonẽ H.inclinaſſet* (canvas 188, column 351, zoom `z188-2_28.jpg`; *Adonaim*
  so divided and spelled on the page).
- 11:5 *ſed etiã Aſtorethum Sidoniorũ, L. & Cha-|moſum Moabitarũ H. deum, & ᵃ Melcomum* (canvas 194, column 364, zoom
  `z194-11_5.jpg`).

So they are Castellio's own, from 1554 at the latest. 1573 has 1:8's and 2:28's (canvases 225 and 227, search API).

## For `docs/editions.md` (not written by this reader)

- 1 Kings in 1554 (MDZ bsb10196330): the text begins on canvas 186, column 347 (*REGVM LIB. III.*, *CAPVT I.*, a
  pictorial initial); chapters 1–11 on canvases 186–195 (columns 347–366); chapter 12 begins in column 366. Four
  lettered marginal glosses in chapters 1–11 (4:13, 6:7, 11:5, 11:36). 1554's chapter 4 runs to the KJV's 4:34 and
  chapter 5 begins *Miſit & Hiramus* (the KJV's division, not the Hebrew's 5:1 = 4:21).
- The annotations: *IN III. REGVM* from canvas 827 (column 349) to canvas 833 (column 361), with no preface, and five
  woodcuts and plans of Solomon's temple (*AREA TEMPLI SOLOMONIS*, the inner court, *AREA NEMORENSIS LIBANI domus*,
  *COLVMNA*, *LACVS*, *VNA BASIS* and the laver's diagrams); fifteen annotations on chapters 1–11 (ch. 5, 6, 7, 10).
  Canvas 832's running head is misprinted *IN LIB. IIII. REGVM*. *IN IIII. LIB. REGVM* begins on canvas 833, column 362.
- The 6:23 annotation (*Cupreſsina materia.)*) is printed under *EX CAP. V.*; it belongs to ch. 6. In it Castellio
  says he rendered the Hebrew's "oil tree" as cypress after the Greek, distinguishing it from the olive (Neh 8).
- **1738's asterisks mark Castellio's annotations**: in 1 Kings 1–11 fifteen of fifteen stand at an annotation's
  place, in order.
- **Revisions after 1554** seen in passing: 11:36 *lumen* (1554) → *lucernam* (1738); 2:12 *annos 12* → *annos
  duodecim*; 3:7 *necdum* (1554, zoom `z188-3_7.jpg`) → *nec tum* (1738, 1750): perhaps a misdivision rather than a
  revision (1573 not found by the search API).
- 10:27 *auri* (gold, where the Hebrew has silver) is in 1554 (canvas 194, column 364), 1573 (canvas 238) and 1738;
  his 2 Chr 9:27 has *argenti* (1573, canvas 289, search API).
- Castellio's *G.*, *L.* … *H.* marks in 1 Kings 1–11 are in 1554's text (1:8, 2:12, 2:28, 11:5).
