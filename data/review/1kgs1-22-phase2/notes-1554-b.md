# 1 Kings 12–22: footnotes from Castellio's 1554 annotations, reader B

Proposed 2026-11-22, phase 2 of decision 85, by the method of `notes-1554-a.md` and
`../2sam1-24-phase2/notes-1554-b.md`. Every 1554 annotation on 1 Kings 12–22 was read on the page and sorted as (a) a
deliberate departure from the Hebrew as read today, (b) a word no dictionary has, (c) commentary or a defence of Latin a
reader can follow (no note), or (d) a place where the annotation shows the settled text differs from what he meant.
Decisions 53 and 56; brief.md, "brief Latin footnotes"; the bar of `../ot-notes-process.md` §2–3. Chapters 1–11 are
reader A's. The notes proposed from this are in `notes-b.md`.

Notes on the readings:
- **15:13**: the NRSVue and the BSB keep the Hebrew's Asherah ("an abominable image for Asherah", "a detestable Asherah
  pole"); Castellio names the idol Pan, against the Vulgate's Priapus. His word for an obscure noun (מִפְלֶצֶת), the same
  event: no note (`notes-b.md` §C item 4).
- **19:3**: he reads וַיִּרָא, "he was afraid", for וַיַּרְא, "he saw" (his "change the points"): so the NRSVue ("Gk: Heb *he
  saw*") and the BSB ("Elijah was afraid", no note). Brenton's LXX *ἐφοβήθη*, the Clementine Vulgate *Timuit*. No note.
- **21:23**: he reads חֵלֶק, "field/portion", for the Hebrew's חֵל, "rampart", with *uetus* (the old Latin translation:
  the Clementine Vulgate here has *in agro Jezrahel*) and 2 Kgs 9:36. The NRSVue "within the bounds of Jezreel" (no
  footnote in the fetch); the BSB "by the wall of Jezreel", with the Hebrew (note: "a few Hebrew manuscripts, Vulgate,
  and Syriac (see also 2 Kings 9:36) … at the plot of ground at Jezreel"). One modern Bible with him: no note. The lemma
  matches the preview's 21:23 (*in agrō Jezrælēnsī comēsum īrī*); 1738's asterisk stands after it, at the start of
  its next sentence (21:24).
- **22:30**: the one annotation of kind (a) against both modern Bibles; proposed (`notes-b.md` §B item 1).

**Every lemma matches the preview's words** (spelling aside). No (b) or (d).

**Three are not 1554's margins:**

- **15:4** *e) posteritatem.*: 1554 has the word in its text, *ei poſteritatẽ | cõceſſit* (198, 371, zoom
  `z198-15_4-6.jpg`), where 1738 prints *ei lucernam concessit*. A revision of the text after 1554, the old word kept as
  a gloss: the same as A's 11:36 (*lumen* → *lucernam*, gloss *posteritatem*). Not found in 1573 by the search
  (*lucernam*, *pofteritatem*: no hit on canvases 238–249).
- **15:6** *f) H. Roboamum.*: **1554 reads *Roboamum* in its text**, *Intercesſit autem bellũ inter Ro-|boamum &
  Ieroboamũ per omnem eius uitã.* (198, 371, zoom `z198-15_4-6.jpg`), with no *L.* … *H.* and no margin. **1573** has
  *inter L. Abiamum H.* (canvas 241, search API); whether 1573 prints the footnote was not found (its OCR's *Roboamum*
  hits on canvas 241 are 14:30 and a summary). So the marked reading came in between 1554 and 1573, and 1738's footnote
  gives the word it replaced (`notes-b.md` §C item 2 and the edition summary).
- **15:10** *g) auia.*: 1554 *Eius mater | fuit nomine Maacha, Abſalomi filia.* (198, 371), no letter, no margin (only
  the side-head *Aſa Iudææ rex.* and *2.Par.14*). **1573 prints it**: *10 Eius materfuit nomine Maacha, Abfalomi filia.
  c [auia]* (canvas 241, search API). A gloss added after 1554 reconciling 15:2 and 15:10 (Maacah, mother of Abijam and
  "mother" of Asa).

The other margins of canvases 195–204 are side-heads (*Populus à Roboamo petit, ut ſe mitius tractet quàm Solomo*,
*Ieroboamus rex creatur*, *Abiamus Iudææ rex*, *Elias Iezabelẽ fugit* …) and cross-references (*2.Par.10*, *Eccl.47*,
*4.Reg.23*, *Exo.32*, *Luc.4*, *Rom.11* …); I saw no other lettered gloss in chapters 12–22 that 1738 lacks. None of
the eleven meets the bar (`notes-b.md` §D).

## For `docs/editions.md` (not written by this reader)

- 1 Kings 12–22 in 1554 (MDZ bsb10196330): text on canvases 195–204 (columns 366–383; the book ends in column 383, then
  *REGVM LIB. IIII.*); chapter 13 begins at KJV 12:33 in 1554 as in 1738. Eight lettered marginal glosses (12:16, 12:18,
  18:10, 18:19, 18:21, 20:34, 21:7, 22:34). The annotations on 12–22 are four, on canvas 833, column 361 (15:13, 19:3,
  21:23, 22:30): with A's, the annotations on 1 Kings run from canvas 827 to canvas 833, column 361.
- **1738's asterisks mark Castellio's annotations**: nineteen of nineteen in 1 Kings.
- **15:6**: 1554 *inter Roboamum & Ieroboamum*, no marks; **1573** *inter L. Abiamum H.*; 1738 the same, with the
  footnote *f) H. Roboamum.* A change made between 1554 and 1573 (by Castellio or the 1573 editor: not settled).
- **15:4**: 1554 *posteritatem* in the text; 1738 *lucernam*, with *posteritatem* as footnote *e)*: a revision after
  1554, as 11:36 (A). **15:10**: the gloss *auia* is not in 1554; it is in 1573 (canvas 241, search API).
- **22:30**: Castellio follows the Greek (*ipsiusque Achabi vestitum indueret*, Jehoshaphat in Ahab's clothes) and says
  so in his annotation; his 2 Chr 18:29 the same (1750 OCR). 19:3 (*territus*, with the Greek, the Latin and Josephus,
  by repointing) and 21:23 (חלק for חל, with the old Latin and 2 Kgs 9) are emendations he states; 15:13's *Pan* is
  argued against the Vulgate's Priapus.
- **1573 prints the annotations in the book** for 15:13, 19:3 and 22:30 (canvases 242, 245, 248, search API), as for
  1 Samuel's and 2 Samuel's.
- 1554 reads *fimus* at 14:10 (1738 *ſimus*, a misprint) and *Nansis* at 19:16 (Castellio's own).
