### Misprints and odd forms, kept as printed

- **Row 1152 *adoranit*** (vote and 1750 *adorauit*; 1726 *adoravit*): zoomed 7–8×; a paper crease crosses the
  letter, but both stems have foot serifs and the top looks closed, i.e. a turned *u* (n). Written *adoranit*.
  **Doubt: please check on the page**; if the top is open it is *adorauit*.
- **Row 1154 *adposito.epulatus***: a full stop (same size as the one after *est.*) sits in the space, close to
  *epulatus*; written as printed, no space after it. Could be a stray mark; 1750 *appofito epulatus* (no stop).
- **Rows 1196–1197 *oppidis Mo-|abitarum*** (KJV/BSB "the cities of the Ammonites"): as printed; 1726 also

### Omission check (against the BSB)

12:18b–31 and 13:1–34a checked clause by clause: nothing the page prints is lost in the reading. Castellio
paraphrases and merges (13:1–2, 13:5–6 into reported speech), and the robe of many colours (13:18) stands in the
parenthesis of 1738's v.18; 13:16 "he would not listen to her" = *noluit obtemperare*; 13:27 "Absalom urged him" =
*impetrauit tamen*. 12:21 "the child is dead, you got up and ate" is all there. No clause of the Hebrew missing from
the page.
