interficiam*; 17:25 covers KJV 25–26), but nothing of the Hebrew is missing from the page and no clause of the page
is missing from the rows. 17:3 has the LXX's plus marked G. … H. 18:13's second half (*Verum tu iam apud illum
ades.*) is on leaf 377.
