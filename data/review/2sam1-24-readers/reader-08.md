### Omission check (against the BSB)

13:34–39 (1738 34–38), 14:1–33, 15:1–9a: every clause of the English is present on the page and in my rows. The
G. … H. passages at 14:27 (Tamar wife of Rehoboam, mother of Abijah) and 14:30 (Joab's servants come with rent
clothes) are Castellio's additions from the Greek, not in the Hebrew. At 15:7 the page reads *quatuor annis*
against the Hebrew's forty, with note e) saying so. 14:1's *Interea … aueret* stands where the Hebrew has Joab
perceiving the king's heart; the sense is carried in v. 2 (*intelligens, regem animum adiecisse ad Absalomum*).
