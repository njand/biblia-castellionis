### Numbers placed by sense within the line (judgment calls)

- Row 202 *clamauit: #26 sem-|perne*: margin 26 on this line; the only break is after *clamauit:*. Castellio has
  fused KJV 25–26 into one sentence (*Abner, ad quem … ad Ioabum huiusmodi verbis clamauit:*); 26 placed at the
  speech. Alternative (whole line, *huiusmodi*) has no break before it.
- Row 212 *destiterunt: #29 o-|missoque prœlio*: margin 29; the wide space before *o-* is the only break. (KJV 28
  ends "neither fought they any more", which *omissoque prœlio* renders, but the page's break is here.)
- Row 247 *tuebatur) #7 repre-|hendit*: margin 7 beside this line (slightly low); wide space after *)*. Castellio's
  6 = KJV 6, his 7 starts mid-sentence with Ishbosheth's rebuke = KJV 7.
- Rows 240 / 242: *#4 quartus*, *#5 sextus* (margin 4 between rows 240–241, 5 between 242–243; KJV 4 = Adonijah,
  5 = Ithream). The vote had #3 / #4 / #5 at line starts.
- Row 287 *filio: #16 qui*: margin 16; wide space before *qui* (KJV 16 the husband follows).
- Row 355 *interfectus) #31 omnibusque*: margin 31 on this line, no wide space in it. Castellio's 30 *Deinde Ioabo
  (qui Ioabus … interfectus)* holds KJV 30's parenthesis; 31 placed after the parenthesis, at *omnibusque, qui
  aderant, præcepit* (KJV 31 "David said to Joab and to all the people"). The vote had it at *qui ad-*. **Doubt**:
  31 could also be meant at *omnibusque* vs *qui*; I chose the word after the parenthesis.
- Row 364 *#33 nequaquam* (margin 33; Castellio's 32 ends *in hæc verba:*), row 365 *#34 Non tibi*.
