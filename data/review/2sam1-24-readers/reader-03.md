### Wording doubts (1738 against 1750)

- 4:2 *Berotha* (1738) / *Berothae* (1750).
- 4:3 *Gethaimum* (1738 read under a blot) / *Gethaimum* (1750); the vote's *Gethannum* came from the blot.

- 5:20 1750's OCR *Boalpba-|raimum* is noise; 1738 *Baalpha-|rasimum*, as 5:20's second form *Baalpharasimum*.
- Otherwise 1738 and 1750 agree in wording through this range (spelling apart).

### Omission check (against the BSB)

Every verse 3:35–39, 4:1–12, 5:1–25, 6:1–2 has its clauses on the page. Notes:
- 5:8: the page's sentence has no main clause after *quisquis … vicisset … inuisos,*; the Hebrew is elliptical here
  too, and the asterisk marks it. Not a lost line.
- 6:2: the BSB's *to Baale of Judah* is rendered by Castellio as *cum suis omnibus iudaicis copiis*; nothing lost in
  the reading.
- 4:7: the BSB's *by way of the Arabah* is Castellio's *per campos*.
