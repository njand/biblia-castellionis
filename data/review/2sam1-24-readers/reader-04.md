### Omission check (against the BSB)

Every verse of 6:3–8:2 compared: each has its clauses. Castellio's own paraphrases (6:5 *pineis instrumentis*; 6:13
*singulos boues atque arietes opimos* for the ox and fatling; 7:11 *(inquies)*; 7:19 *humano more* for *torat
ha-adam*; 7:23 *dii* for *elohim*) are his, not losses. 6:4 has no repeat of the ark "on the hill" clause, as
Castellio condenses 3–4 into one sentence; the page has nothing more. Nothing lost by the reading.
