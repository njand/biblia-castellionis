### The main finding: 23:1–7 lost by the vote (25 lines added)

The OCR/vote has nothing between `\c 23` (row 2536, leaf 383 col. 0 line 14) and row 2537 (line 42, *Sequuntur
eorum nomina*): **the whole of David's last words, 23:1–7, is missing** (25 printed lines, from the drop capital
*SEquuntur Dauidis verba nouis-* to *cremantur.*). Added from the page as `+ 383 0 900`–`924`, in place after `\c 23`.
Because of this gap the vote's word alignment slipped badly in the next lines (rows 2537–2547): it had 23:3–7's and
23:8–9's witness words mixed (*Iafobaa-|inus Haemonis nilus*, *pluuia nepos, vnus de tribus pe-*, *s. Is quum apud
posteritati cum*, *Dedis nilus*): every one of these lines re-read whole from the page (*Iasobaa-|mus Hacmonis
filius*, *A-|chois nepos, vnus de tribus fortissi-|mis. Is quum apud Horpam cum*, *Dodis filius*). 1750 has the
same 23:1–7 text (checked line by line in `sources/archive/1750/text.txt` around line 46190; spelling only differs).
