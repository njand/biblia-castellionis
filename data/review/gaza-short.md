# *Gaza* short: the settled *Gāzam* respelled

Found while re-checking Joshua's names (`josh1-24-names.md` §"Evidence re-checked", 2026-11-19): the settled *Gāzam*
(Gen 10:19, Deut 2:23, Acts 8:26) has a long ā that every source denies. Gaffiot heads *Găza, æ* (via `python -m
pipeline latin macron Gaza`); the Hebrew עַזָּה has patah; the Greek Γάζα has no long letter. The Acts forms row had
kept the ā only "for one form across the books" (Claude, 2026-11-09). Joshua's *Gazæ* (11:22), *gazēa* (13:3) and
*Gaza* (15:47) now take the short a, and these three settled words with them. The printed texts keep the print's
*Gazam* (no marks).

**Decision:** accept (Nathan, 2026-11-19: "Apply all seven", the seventh being *Gaza* short with the settled *Gāzam*)

1. **Gen 10:19** *Gāzam* → *Gazam*.
    **Apply:** `Gen.10.19` la: `attingunt Gāzam;` → `attingunt Gazam;`
2. **Deut 2:23** *Gāzam* → *Gazam*.
    **Apply:** `Deut.2.23` la: `ūsque ad Gāzam` → `ūsque ad Gazam`
3. **Acts 8:26** *Gāzam* → *Gazam*.
    **Apply:** `Acts.8.26` la: `Hierosolymā Gāzam dēscendit` → `Hierosolymā Gazam dēscendit`
