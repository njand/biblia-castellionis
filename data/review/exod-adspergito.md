# Exodus 29:21 *adspergitō*: the one *ad-* form left in the settled text (1)

Found 2026-11-16 while checking the Leviticus spelling rules (`data/review/lev1-27-stages3-5.md`, question 1). With
the approved Leviticus rules *adspergat → aspergat*, *adspersam → aspersam* and *adspersum → aspersum* (decision 37),
Leviticus writes *asperg-* (6:27, 16:15, 16:19), and so does the rest of the settled text. A search of `text/*.usfm`
for *ad* + f, l, p, s(p) found one *ad-* form other than the compounds of *sum*, which decision 37 keeps: Exod
29:21. It is in a Wikisource-based Exodus chapter settled before the rules for these words existed.

1. **Exod 29:21** *adspergitō* → *aspergitō* (decision 37; Gaffiot *aspergō*).
   **Decision:** accept (Nathan, 2026-11-16: "Approve both")
   **Apply:** `Exod.29.21` la: `vestīmenta adspergitō` → `vestīmenta aspergitō`
