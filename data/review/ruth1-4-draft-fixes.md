# Ruth 1–4: 1738's slips taken out of the draft

*2026-11-19, phase 2. The draft (`text/draft/RUT.usfm`) was built from the read lines, which follow the page exactly, so
three of 1738's printing slips came through into the edition's starting text. The printed text (`text/orig/RUT.usfm`)
keeps them, as decision 58 requires; the edition reads the words Castellio meant. Each was read on the page in phase 1
(`ruth1-4-transcription.md`, "Checked by Claude on the page").*

**Applied to the draft** under decision 30's standing rule.

## 1. Words run together or broken by the printer (3)

**Decision:** accept (Claude, under the standing rule of decision 30, 2026-11-19)

1. **Ruth 1:15** *etNoemis* → *et Noemis*. 1738 prints *hærebat,etNoe-* (leaf 319) with no spaces; Wikisource
   *et Noemis*.
    **Apply:** `Ruth.1.15` la: `etNoemis sic eam` → `et Noemis sic eam`
2. **Ruth 3:2** *pull is* → *puellis*. 1738 *pu-* / *ll is fuisti* (leaf 320): letters dropped at the line break;
   Wikisource *puellis*.
    **Apply:** `Ruth.3.2` la: `tu pull is fuisti` → `tu puellis fuisti`
3. **Ruth 3:7** *QuumqueBoozus* → *Quumque Boozus*. 1738 prints the two words with no space (leaf 320); Wikisource
   apart.
    **Apply:** `Ruth.3.7` la: `QuumqueBoozus cibo` → `Quumque Boozus cibo`

## 2. Names brought to the settled forms (5)

**Decision:** accept (Claude, under the standing rule of decision 30, 2026-11-19; the names reader's calls, `ruth1-4-names.md`, hard calls 2–4)

4. **Ruth 1:2** *Ephrattaei* → *Ephrataei*. settled Judg 12:5 Ephrātæī, Gen 35:19 Ephrātæ (Gaffiot Ephrāta; אֶפְרָתִים single t); 1738 Ephrattæi kept in the printed text.
    **Apply:** `Ruth.1.2` la: `Ephrattaei` → `Ephrataei`
5. **Ruth 4:18** *Hezronem* → *Hesronem*. settled Gen 46:12 Hesrōnem, Num 26:21 Hesrōn (the same son of Perez, חֶצְרוֹן); 1738 Hezronem kept in the printed text.
    **Apply:** `Ruth.4.18` la: `Hezronem` → `Hesronem`
6. **Ruth 4:19** *Hezron Ramum* → *Hesron Ramum*. settled Gen 46:12, Num 26:21 Hesrōn; 1738 Hezron.
    **Apply:** `Ruth.4.19` la: `Hezron Ramum` → `Hesron Ramum`
7. **Ruth 4:20** *Nahassonem* → *Nahasonem*. settled Exod 6:23 Nahasōnis, Num 1:7, 2:3, 10:14 Nahasōn (נַחְשׁוֹן); 1738 Nahassonem.
    **Apply:** `Ruth.4.20` la: `Nahassonem` → `Nahasonem`
8. **Ruth 4:20** *Nahasson Salmonem* → *Nahason Salmonem*. settled Num 1:7, 2:3, 10:14 Nahasōn; 1738 Nahasson.
    **Apply:** `Ruth.4.20` la: `Nahasson Salmonem` → `Nahason Salmonem`

## 3. The book's title

The draft's `\h`, `\toc1`, `\toc2` and `\mt1` read *Rutha*; set to *Rūtha* by hand in the draft (the names reader's
form, as Judges' title reads *Jūdicēs*). The printed text's title is left as it stands (*Rutha*).
