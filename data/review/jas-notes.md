# James: light notes and brackets

Built 2026-11-17 by the process in `nt-notes-process.md`, with the test and wording of `nt-notes-form.md`: "the modern
editions" means SBLGNT and NA28, against the TR.

**Sources read:** `jas-notes-candidates.md` (`python -m pipeline notes Jas`); the letter, read verse by verse
against the TR, SBLGNT, BSB, WEB, the Vulgate and 1573 as printed, with every line of the SBLGNT apparatus (one reader for James and 1–2 Peter);
the NRSVue (Bible Gateway, read 2026-11-17) for every verse below: it reads with the modern text against Castellio,
and none of its footnotes gives his sense as an alternative, except where an item says so. No word of the edited

## A. Notes where the Greek text differs (the modern editions against the TR)

**Decision:** accept (Nathan, 2026-11-17: "Apply them")

1. **2:20** *mortuam*: TR νεκρά; the modern editions ἀργή (NRSVue "faith apart from works is worthless", footnote "dead or empty"). "Dead" stays at 2:17 and 2:26.
   **Apply:** `Jas.2.20` la: `fidem sine operibus mortuam esse?` → `fidem sine operibus mortuam\f + \fr 2:20 \ft Cōdicēs antīquiōrēs habent ἀργή, ‘inertem’.\f* esse?`
2. **5:12** *in simulātiōnem*: the TR, SBLGNT and NA28 ἵνα μὴ ὑπὸ κρίσιν πέσητε, "so that you may not fall under condemnation" (NRSVue, footnote "Other ancient authorities read *into hypocrisy*"). Castellio follows the Byzantine εἰς ὑπόκρισιν, which the TR does not print; *in simulationem* in 1573 and 1758.
   **Apply:** `Jas.5.12` la: `nē in simulātiōnem cadātis.` → `nē in simulātiōnem\f + \fr 5:12 \ft Cōdicēs antīquiōrēs habent ὑπὸ κρίσιν, ‘sub jūdicium’.\f* cadātis.`

## B. Notes where Castellio's Latin says what the Greek does not

**Decision:** accept (Nathan, 2026-11-17: "Apply them")

3. **4:2** *invidētis*: the Greek (TR and SBLGNT) φονεύετε, "you commit murder" (NRSVue; the Vulgate *occiditis*). Castellio follows Erasmus's conjecture φθονεῖτε; *inuidetis* in 1573, 1747 and 1750.
   **Apply:** `Jas.4.2` la: `invidētis et æmulāminī` → `invidētis\f + \fr 4:2 \ft Græcē φονεύετε, ‘occīditis’.\f* et æmulāminī`

## C. Brackets: words the modern editions leave out

**Decision:** accept (Nathan, 2026-11-17: "Apply them")

4. **1:26** *apud vōs*: TR ἐν ὑμῖν (NRSVue "If any think they are religious"), as Gal 3:1.
   **Apply:** `Jas.1.26` la: `esse religiōsus apud vōs, neque` → `esse religiōsus [apud vōs], neque`

## Considered and left out

- 1:12 *Dominus*: the modern editions have no subject, but the NRSVue supplies "the Lord" (footnote "Gk *he*"), so a bracket would mislead.
- 3:9 *Deum* (the modern editions κύριον; NRSVue "the Lord and Father", footnote "God"): the same person.
- 1:19 Ὥστε/Ἴστε, 4:4 μοιχοὶ καὶ μοιχαλίδες: no change a reader needs; 3:5 *magnās vīrēs habet* (μεγάλα αὐχεῖ): his paraphrase.
- 4:12 *(atque jūdex)*: 1573 prints the words without parentheses (and a stray *(* at the end of 4:11); 5:5 *lascīvīstis*, 5:11 *audīstis* (1573 *lasciuijstis*, *audiuistis*): for the next spot check.
