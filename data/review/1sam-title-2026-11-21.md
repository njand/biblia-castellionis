# 1 Samuel's title on two lines: *Samuēlis* over a smaller *Liber Prior*

*2026-11-21.* Nathan: "I would like to maintain secondary headers where possible. Also, I think I would like to keep
'liber prior' on a second line, not all as one line."

**The convention.** A book's `\mt1` holds the name a reader looks for, its `\mt2` the rest of the title, set smaller
(0.55 of the main size, letterspaced) and read in order with it: *Petrī Apostolī / Epistula Prior*, *Jōhannis Apostolī /
Epistula Secunda*, *Euangelium Auctōre / Matthæō*, *Paulī Apostolī Epistula Prior / ad Corinthiōs*. 1 Samuel was the one
title with a generic part (*Liber Prior*) in its main line. Split, it reads like the catholic letters. 2 Samuel and Kings
are to follow it (*Samuēlis / Liber Posterior*, and so on).

**The evidence** (page scans seen 2026-11-21 on archive.org):
- Hetzenauer's Clementine Vulgate (Innsbruck 1914, `bibliasacravulga00helzuoft`): *EPISTOLA / BEATI PETRI APOSTOLI* large,
  *PRIMA.* small beneath (leaf 1201); *EPISTOLA / BEATI PAULI APOSTOLI* large, *AD CORINTHIOS PRIMA.* smaller (leaf 1134);
  *LIBER PRIMUS SAMUELIS,* large, *QUEM NOS PRIMUM REGUM DICIMUS.* smaller (leaf 247). The ordinal can go in the smaller line.
- Weber–Gryson, *Biblia Sacra iuxta Vulgatam versionem* (`vulgata_weber_gryson`): *INCIPIT LIBER SAMUHELIS* over a
  smaller *ID EST REGUM PRIMUS ET SECUNDUS* (leaf 407); *INCIPIT EPISTULA PETRI ·I·* on one line (leaf 1905).
- The Revised Version (Oxford 1906, `holybiblerevised00oxfo`): a small *THE* over a large *FIRST BOOK OF SAMUEL.* (leaf 192).
In each, the large line is the name, the smaller line the rest, read top to bottom as one title. The running heads and
contents (*Samuēlis I*) keep the number on every page.

**The printed text** (`text/orig/1SA.usfm`) carries the edition's title unmarked, not 1738's own heading (*REGVM LIBER I.*,
leaf 322), as decided 2026-11-20, so it is split the same way.

**Not by `review apply`:** its Apply lines reach verses only, and the title lines stand above chapter 1. The two changes
below were made by hand, exactly as written, and checked once each.

**Decision:** accept (Nathan, 2026-11-21: "Yes, split it")

1. **text/1SA.usfm**, header: `\mt1 Samuēlis Liber Prior` → `\mt1 Samuēlis` `\mt2 Liber Prior` (two lines).
2. **text/orig/1SA.usfm**, header: `\mt1 Samuelis Liber Prior` → `\mt1 Samuelis` `\mt2 Liber Prior` (two lines).

`\h` (*Samuēlis I*), `\toc1` (*Samuēlis Liber Prior*) and `\toc2` (*Samuēlis I*) unchanged.
