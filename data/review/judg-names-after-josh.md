# Judges' names after Joshua's: one form, the Hebrew's vowels (proposed 2026-11-19)

Found on merging Joshua with Judges (both settled 2026-11-19 on different workstations; `josh-judg-merge.md`): four
names Judges marks otherwise than Joshua, now that decision 82 lets the Hebrew decide where the Greek has no long
letter behind it, and one slip. Compared by stripping the marks from every capitalized word in both books; the
differences of case (*Bēthlehema/-ā*, *Masphā*, *Saraā*) are the Latin endings. Hebrew from the WLC in the verse.
**Waiting for your decision**; nothing applied.

**Decision:**

1. **Shiloh *Silūnt-* → *Sīlūnt-*** (4 words): Judges took the short i from the Hebrew שִׁלֹה (hireq, no yod);
   Joshua's *Sīlūnt-* (decision 82's check) from the spelling with yod, שִׁילוֹ, in Judg 21:21 itself (twice) and Jer
   7:12, which shows the i long, with the Greek Σηλώ η. One form for the place (decision 46).
    **Apply:** `Judg.18.31` la: `Silūnte` → `Sīlūnte`
    **Apply:** `Judg.21.12` la: `Silūntem` → `Sīlūntem`
    **Apply:** `Judg.21.19` la: `Silūnte` → `Sīlūnte`
    **Apply:** `Judg.21.21` la: `Silūntiās` → `Sīlūntiās`
2. **Aijalon *Ajalōn-* → *Ajālōn-*** (2 words): אַיָּלוֹן patah, doubled y, **qamets**, holem; Joshua 10:12,
   19:42, 21:24 *Ajālōn-*. Judges' rows gave only the holem.
    **Apply:** `Judg.1.35` la: `Ajalōnem` → `Ajālōnem`
    **Apply:** `Judg.12.12` la: `Ajalōne` → `Ajālōne`
3. **Kiriath-jearim *Cariathjarīm-* → *Cariathjārīm-*** (2 words): יְעָרִים shewa, **qamets**, hireq-yod; Joshua
   9:17, 15:9, 15:60, 18:14–15 *Cariathjārīm-*.
    **Apply:** `Judg.18.11` la: `Cariathjarīmum` → `Cariathjārīmum`
    **Apply:** `Judg.18.12` la: `Cariathjarīmum` → `Cariathjārīmum`
4. **Judg 5:15 *Issacharianis* → *Issāchariānīs***: unmarked by a slip; the settled *Issāchariān-* (Num 1:8, Deut
   27:12, Josh 17:10), and the ablative plural's -īs.
    **Apply:** `Judg.5.15` la: `Issacharianis,` → `Issāchariānīs,`
5. **Gibeah: a question, not an Apply line.** Joshua 15:57 has *Gabāa* (גִּבְעָה hireq, silent shewa, **qamets**: the
   stem's second a is the qamets); Judges has *Gabaam, Gabaā, Gabaæ, Gabaēnsēs, Gabaānīs* short (23 words in chapters 19–20 and
   elsewhere). By the Hebrew, Judges' would take *Gabā-* (*Gabāam, Gabāēnsēs*); by Judges' majority, Joshua's 15:57
   would become *Gabaa*. Yours to choose.

Also seen, in older settled books, for a later look: Gen 30:18 *Issachārem* (the second a marked, unlike the settled
*Issāchar*) and Rev 7:7 *Issacharis* (unmarked; the settled *Issācharis*).
