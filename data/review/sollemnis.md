# *sollemnis*, as Gaffiot heads it

Nathan, 2026-11-18: "Gaffiot says that "sollemnis" is *mieux que sollenis, solemnis*. Let's add a rule to standardize to
that form." Castellio prints *solemnis* (and once *solennis*); the edited text takes Gaffiot's preferred spelling, as
decision 18's *Gaffiot's headword wins*. The marking follows: *sollemn-* with no long vowel in the stem (Gaffiot
sollemnis, sollemnĭtās; Wiktionary and Macronizer *sollemnis, sollemnitātibus* …), where the settled text had *sōlemn-*
(Wiktionary's and Macronizer's marking of the one-l spelling). The printed texts (`text/orig/`) keep Castellio's
spelling (decision 58).

- **Stem rules** in `data/rules/orthography.tsv`, approved: *solemn → sollemn*, *solenn → sollemn*, *sollenn → sollemn*
  (every word that begins so; no other Latin word does in the edition).
