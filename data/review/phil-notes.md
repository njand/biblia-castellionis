## A. Notes where the Greek text differs (the modern editions against the TR)

**Decision:** accept (Nathan, 2026-11-17: "Apply them")

1. **3:3** *Spīritū Deum colimus*: TR πνεύματι Θεῷ λατρεύοντες, "worship God in spirit"; the modern editions πνεύματι θεοῦ (NRSVue "who worship in the Spirit of God", footnote "worship God in spirit"). The TR alone.
   **Apply:** `Phil.3.3` la: `Spīritū Deum colimus,` → `Spīritū Deum colimus\f + \fr 3:3 \ft Cōdicēs antīquiōrēs habent πνεύματι θεοῦ λατρεύοντες, ‘Deī Spīritū colentēs’.\f*,`

## C. Brackets: words the modern editions leave out

**Decision:** accept (Nathan, 2026-11-17: "Apply them")

2. **3:16** *idem sentiāmus*: TR τὸ αὐτὸ φρονεῖν. NRSVue "let us hold fast to what we have attained". *ad rēgulam* stays, so that the Latin still reads.
   **Apply:** `Phil.3.16` la: `adeptī sumus, idem sentiāmus, eandem` → `adeptī sumus, [idem sentiāmus,] eandem`
3. **4:13** *Chrīstum*: TR ἐν τῷ ἐνδυναμοῦντί με Χριστῷ. NRSVue "through him who strengthens me".
   **Apply:** `Phil.4.13` la: `corrōborat, Chrīstum.` → `corrōborat[, Chrīstum].`
4. **4:23** *Āmēn*: TR ἀμήν. NRSVue footnote "Other ancient authorities add *Amen*".
   **Apply:** `Phil.4.23` la: `omnibus: Āmēn.` → `omnibus: [Āmēn.]`

## Considered and left out

- 4:23 *vōbīs omnibus* (the modern editions "your spirit"): no change a reader needs.
- 3:12 Ἰησοῦ: NA28 keeps it.
- 1:16–17: the verses are swapped, not the sense.
