# Macrons on proper names: Melchisedech and the names like it

Built 2026-11-16 after your spot check of Heb 5:6. Decision 52 (brief.md) orders the evidence for a name's
vowels: Gaffiot's marks; then the Greek's long letters (η, ω, ει); then another dictionary that marks the word
(Wiktionary's bare vowel counts as short); then the Hebrew vowel.

## Why *Melchisedēchī* happened

- Your 2019 Genesis (WordPress, Gen 14:18) has *Melchisedēchus*. Genesis 1–35 was settled from that text, so the
  gold had *Melchisedēchus*.
- In Hebrews (2026-11-12) the lookup was on the inflected form *Melchisedēchī*, which no source knows. Gaffiot has
  the name only as the indeclinable headword *Melchisedech* (unmarked), and an inflected-form lookup never reaches
  it. The decision (`data/decisions/Heb.forms.tsv`, Heb.5.6) then cited "your Gen 14:18" as the precedent and
  never got to the Greek (Μελχισεδέκ, ε) or the Hebrew (צֶדֶק, segol).
- So the 2019 macrons, and the settled forms copied from them, were treated as evidence for names. Under decision
  52 they are not: a name's macrons have to come from Gaffiot, the Greek or the Hebrew.

## How the rest of the text was checked

1. Every capitalized word with a macron that no source knows in that form: 750 forms. Each stem was checked
   against Gaffiot's headwords (indeclinable names included).
2. Every name in the settled text was matched to its Greek word in the same verse (TR for the NT, Brenton's LXX
   for Genesis–Leviticus) and its vowels compared: ē/ō over ε/ο, and e/o unmarked over η/ω.
3. Every name whose stem is marked differently in different places.

The checks found the same pattern again: segolate Hebrew names (*Lamēch*, *Jarēd*, *Phalēg*, *Zorobabēl*,
*Eliezēr*) given a long e that neither the Greek nor the Hebrew has, plus a few names Gaffiot marks differently
(*Labān*, *Zabūlōn*, *Lysānias*). Some long vowels are also missing where the Greek has η or ω. Section B lists
them all. Names whose evidence agrees with the text are left alone: *Abēl* (Gaffiot *Ăbēl*), *Japhētus*
(Wiktionary), *Bēliale* (Gaffiot *Bēlĭal*), *Ēnōs* (Gaffiot), *Pōtīphar*, *Cōra*, *Gērsōn*, *Gōssen*, *Jethrō*,
*Ētham* (Hebrew ḥolem or ṣere, the Greek silent). *Nāzareth* stays as you decided on 2026-11-12 (Lewis & Short
*Nāzăreth*), though Gaffiot marks *Nazărĕth*.

Left for your call (decided 2026-11-16: your "Accept recommendations"; section C):
- *Galaad-* (*Galaadō* twice, *Galaādō* once; Hebrew גִּלְעָד has qamats, no dictionary marks it).
- *Bala* (*Bālā* four times, *Balā* once, Gen 30; no source marks it).
- *Dodan-* (*Dodānem* and *Dodanem*, Gen 10:4 and 25:3; two different men).
- *Nephthalim* (Gen 30:8) and *Nephthālis*/*Nephthālim* elsewhere (Hebrew qamats; no dictionary).

## Keeping it from happening again

- The macron decisions for later books should look names up by stem as well (Gaffiot's indeclinable headwords),
  and check the Greek's long letters every time. A 2019 or settled form is never the evidence for a name.
- The two checks above (stem against Gaffiot, vowels against the Greek) can run as part of stage 4 on each new
  draft, and list every disagreement for review.
