# Jude: light notes and brackets

Built 2026-11-17 by the process in `nt-notes-process.md`, with the test and wording of `nt-notes-form.md`: "the modern
editions" means SBLGNT and NA28, against the TR.

**Sources read:** `jude-notes-candidates.md` (`python -m pipeline notes Jude`); the letter, read verse by verse
against the TR, SBLGNT, BSB, WEB, the Vulgate and 1573 as printed, with every line of the SBLGNT apparatus (one reader for 1–3 John and Jude);
the NRSVue (Bible Gateway, read 2026-11-17) for every verse below: it reads with the modern text against Castellio,

## A. Notes where the Greek text differs (the modern editions against the TR)

**Decision:** accept (Nathan, 2026-11-17: "Apply them")

1. **1** *sānctificātīs*: TR ἡγιασμένοις, "sanctified"; the modern editions ἠγαπημένοις (NRSVue "who are beloved", footnote "sanctified"; the Vulgate *dilectis*).
   **Apply:** `Jude.1.1` la: `sānctificātīs in Deō Patre,` → `sānctificātīs\f + \fr 1:1 \ft Cōdicēs antīquiōrēs habent ἠγαπημένοις, ‘dīlēctīs’.\f* in Deō Patre,`
2. **5** *Dominus*: TR ὁ Κύριος; the modern editions Ἰησοῦς (NRSVue "that Jesus, who saved a people out of the land of Egypt", footnote "the Lord").
   **Apply:** `Jude.1.5` la: `quod Dominus, cum populum` → `quod Dominus\f + \fr 1:5 \ft Cōdicēs antīquiōrēs habent Ἰησοῦς, ‘Jēsūs’.\f*, cum populum`
3. **22–23** *et aliōs miserāminī, dēlēctum habentēs: aliōs religiōsē servāte, ex igne rapientēs*: the TR has two groups, the modern editions three (NRSVue "have mercy on some who are wavering; save others by snatching them out of the fire; and have mercy on still others with fear"), with no footnote calling the Greek uncertain.
   **Apply:** `Jude.1.23` la: `ex igne rapientēs,` → `ex igne rapientēs\f + \fr 1:22–23 \ft Cōdicēs antīquiōrēs habent οὓς μὲν ἐλεᾶτε διακρινομένους, οὓς δὲ σῴζετε ἐκ πυρὸς ἁρπάζοντες, οὓς δὲ ἐλεᾶτε ἐν φόβῳ, ‘aliōs dubitantēs miserāminī, aliōs servāte ex igne rapientēs, aliōs cum metū miserāminī’.\f*,`
4. **25** *Servātōrī nostrō … glōria*: the modern editions add διὰ Ἰησοῦ Χριστοῦ τοῦ κυρίου ἡμῶν and πρὸ παντὸς τοῦ αἰῶνος (NRSVue "through Jesus Christ our Lord … before all time and now and forever"); the TR and Castellio lack both.
   **Apply:** `Jude.1.25` la: `Deō Servātōrī nostrō glōria` → `Deō Servātōrī nostrō\f + \fr 1:25 \ft Cōdicēs antīquiōrēs addunt διὰ Ἰησοῦ Χριστοῦ τοῦ κυρίου ἡμῶν, ‘per Jēsum Chrīstum Dominum nostrum’, et πρὸ παντὸς τοῦ αἰῶνος, ‘ante omne sæculum’.\f* glōria`

## C. Brackets: words the modern editions leave out

**Decision:** accept (Nathan, 2026-11-17: "Apply them")

5. **4** *Deum*: TR τὸν μόνον δεσπότην Θεὸν καὶ Κύριον ἡμῶν (NRSVue "our only Master and Lord, Jesus Christ"). Without Θεόν, "only Master" is Christ.
   **Apply:** `Jude.1.4` la: `sōlum erum Deum, Dominumque` → `sōlum erum [Deum], Dominumque`
6. **25** *sapientī*: TR μόνῳ σοφῷ Θεῷ (NRSVue "to the only God our Savior").
   **Apply:** `Jude.1.25` la: `sōlī sapientī Deō` → `sōlī [sapientī] Deō`
7. **19** *sē ipsōs*: TR ἑαυτούς, the TR alone (NRSVue "who are causing divisions").
   **Apply:** `Jude.1.19` la: `quī sē ipsōs sēcernunt` → `quī [sē ipsōs] sēcernunt`

## Considered and left out

- 15 *eōrum impiōs*: SBLGNT and NA28 disagree.
- 14–15: the TR's words sit in v14, Castellio's in v15; versification only.
- 7 *impudīcī corporis libīdinibus obsecūtæ*: his paraphrase.
