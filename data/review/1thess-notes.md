# 1 Thessalonians: light notes and brackets

Built 2026-11-17 by the process in `nt-notes-process.md`, with the test and wording of `nt-notes-form.md`: "the modern
editions" means SBLGNT and NA28, against the TR.

**Sources read:** `1thess-notes-candidates.md` (`python -m pipeline notes 1Thess`); the whole letter, read verse by verse
against the TR, SBLGNT, BSB, WEB, the Vulgate and 1573 as printed, with every line of the SBLGNT apparatus (one
reader for 1–2 Thessalonians, Titus and Philemon); the NRSVue (Bible Gateway, read 2026-11-17) for every verse below: it reads with the modern
text against Castellio, and none of its footnotes gives his sense as an alternative. No word of the edited text
differs from 1573 in more than spelling.

## A. Notes where the Greek text differs (the modern editions against the TR)

**Decision:** accept (Nathan, 2026-11-17: "Apply them")

1. **3:2** *Deī ministrum, ac nostrum … collēgam*: TR διάκονον τοῦ Θεοῦ καὶ συνεργὸν ἡμῶν; the modern editions συνεργὸν τοῦ θεοῦ (NRSVue "God's coworker", footnote giving the TR's reading).
   **Apply:** `1Thess.3.2` la: `euangeliō collēgam,` → `euangeliō collēgam\f + \fr 3:2 \ft Cōdicēs antīquiōrēs habent συνεργὸν τοῦ θεοῦ, ‘Deī collēgam’.\f*,`
2. **4:1** *Deōque placēre dēbeātis*: the modern editions add καθὼς καὶ περιπατεῖτε (NRSVue "as, in fact, you are doing").
   **Apply:** `1Thess.4.1` la: `Deōque placēre dēbeātis, in eō` → `Deōque placēre dēbeātis\f + \fr 4:1 \ft Cōdicēs antīquiōrēs addunt καθὼς καὶ περιπατεῖτε, ‘quemadmodum et geritis’.\f*, in eō`

## B. Notes where Castellio's Latin says what the Greek does not

**Decision:** accept (Nathan, 2026-11-17: "Apply them")

3. **2:14** *quæ nōs ā Jūdæīs*: the Greek (TR and SBLGNT) καθὼς καὶ αὐτοί, the churches in Judea (NRSVue "as they did from the Jews"; the Vulgate *sicut et ipsi*). *quæ nos à Iudæis* in 1556 (OCR), 1573, 1681, 1715, 1726, 1747, 1750 and 1758: Castellio's own.
   **Apply:** `1Thess.2.14` la: `quæ nōs ā Jūdæīs,` → `quæ nōs\f + \fr 2:14 \ft Græcē καθὼς καὶ αὐτοί, ‘quæ et illī’; Castelliōnis ēditiōnēs ‘nōs’.\f* ā Jūdæīs,`

## C. Brackets: words the modern editions leave out

**Decision:** accept (Nathan, 2026-11-17: "Apply them")

4. **1:1** *ā Deō Patre nostrō, et Dominō Jēsū Chrīstō*: TR ἀπὸ Θεοῦ Πατρὸς ἡμῶν καὶ Κυρίου Ἰησοῦ Χριστοῦ. NRSVue ends "Grace to you and peace".
   **Apply:** `1Thess.1.1` la: `grātiam et pācem ā Deō Patre nostrō, et Dominō Jēsū Chrīstō.` → `grātiam et pācem [ā Deō Patre nostrō, et Dominō Jēsū Chrīstō].`
5. **1:3** *cōram Deō Patreque nostrō*: in no Greek here. The Greek has "before our God and Father" once, at the end of the verse, where Castellio has it too; 1573 prints both.
   **Apply:** `1Thess.1.3` la: `continenter cōram Deō Patreque nostrō recolentēs` → `continenter [cōram Deō Patreque nostrō] recolentēs`
6. **5:28** *Āmēn*: TR ἀμήν. NRSVue footnote "Other ancient authorities add *Amen*".
   **Apply:** `1Thess.5.28` la: `grātia, Āmēn.` → `grātia[, Āmēn].`

## D. Corrections to the edited text

**Decision:** accept (Nathan, 2026-11-17: "Apply them")

7. **5:8** a comma: *sōbriī sīmus indūtī, fideī* → *sōbriī sīmus, indūtī fideī*, as 1573 (*sobrij simus, induti fidei*) and the Greek (νήφωμεν, ἐνδυσάμενοι θώρακα πίστεως). As it stands, the comma cuts *indūtī* off from the breastplate.
   **Apply:** `1Thess.5.8` la: `sōbriī sīmus indūtī, fideī` → `sōbriī sīmus, indūtī fideī`
