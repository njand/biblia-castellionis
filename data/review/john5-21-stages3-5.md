## Calls worth a look

- **Scripture in `\qt`**: 6:31 (Ps 78:24), 6:45 (Isa 54:13), 10:34 (Ps 82:6), 12:13 (Ps 118:26, inline as Matt
  21:9), 12:15 (Zech 9:9, poetry as Matt 21:5), 12:38 and 12:40 (Isaiah, poetry), 13:18 (Ps 41:9), 15:25, 19:24
  (Ps 22:18, inline as Matt 27:35), 19:36, 19:37. Not marked: 7:38 (*ut perhibent litterae*, woven into Jesus'
  sentence), 7:42 and 8:17 (reported).
- **Reported speech left unmarked** where the BSB quotes, e.g. 5:15, 6:12, 6:14, 6:41, 7:11–12, 7:40–42, 8:27,
  13:33, 14:28, 16:17, 20:17 (the message to the brothers), 20:18, 20:25a, 21:3, 21:23 (the rumour).
- **Indirect questions lose their ?**: 5:12, 9:10, 9:15.
- **Capitals**: *Pater* for God throughout (also 8:19 *tuus Pater*, 8:41 *ūnum Patrem habēmus Deum*), *Fīlius*,
  *Deī Fīlius*, *Fīlius Hominis* (also in the crowd's mouth, 12:34), *Spīritus Sānctus* (7:39, 20:22) and the
  Spirit of truth (14:17, 15:26, 16:13); *cōnfirmātor* (the Paraclete) lower case, as decision 20 does not list
  it; *rēx* lower case in Pilate's and the soldiers' mouths.
- **Paragraph moved**: 10:40's BSB paragraph to *Sed* in 10:39 (mid-sentence otherwise).
- 16:18 *‘paulō post’* in ‘…’ (his very words) could equally be left unmarked; 16:31 *Nunc crēditis* kept a
  statement (the BSB makes it a question).
