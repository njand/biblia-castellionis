# Hebrews: light notes and brackets

Built 2026-11-17 by the process in `nt-notes-process.md`, with the test and wording of `nt-notes-form.md`: "the modern
editions" means SBLGNT and NA28, against the TR.

**Sources read:** `heb-notes-candidates.md` (`python -m pipeline notes Heb`); the letter, read verse by verse
against the TR, SBLGNT, BSB, WEB, the Vulgate and 1573 as printed, with every line of the SBLGNT apparatus (one reader for the letter);
the NRSVue (Bible Gateway, read 2026-11-17) for every verse below: it reads with the modern text against Castellio,
and none of its footnotes gives his sense as an alternative, except where an item says so. No word of the edited
text differs from 1573 in more than spelling.

## A. Notes where the Greek text differs (the modern editions against the TR)

**Decision:** accept (Nathan, 2026-11-17: "Apply them")

1. **9:11** *futūrōrum bonōrum*: TR τῶν μελλόντων ἀγαθῶν, "of the good things to come"; the modern editions τῶν γενομένων ἀγαθῶν (NRSVue "of the good things that have come", footnote "good things to come").
   **Apply:** `Heb.9.11` la: `futūrōrum bonōrum pontifex` → `futūrōrum bonōrum\f + \fr 9:11 \ft Cōdicēs antīquiōrēs habent τῶν γενομένων ἀγαθῶν, ‘bonōrum quæ jam advēnērunt’.\f* pontifex`
2. **10:34** *meōrum vinculōrum*: TR τοῖς δεσμοῖς μου, "my bonds"; the modern editions τοῖς δεσμίοις (NRSVue "you had compassion for those who were in prison").
   **Apply:** `Heb.10.34` la: `Nam et meōrum vinculōrum vicem` → `Nam et meōrum vinculōrum\f + \fr 10:34 \ft Cōdicēs antīquiōrēs habent τοῖς δεσμίοις, ‘vīnctōrum’.\f* vicem`
3. **4:2** *nōn conjūnctus cum eōrum fidē*: TR μὴ συγκεκραμένος, the word not mixed with faith; the modern editions (and RP) μὴ συγκεκερασμένους, the hearers not united by faith (NRSVue "because they were not united by faith with those who listened", footnote "it did not meet with faith"). The TR alone; missed by the candidates file.
   **Apply:** `Heb.4.2` la: `nōn conjūnctus cum eōrum fidē, quī audierant.` → `nōn conjūnctus cum eōrum fidē, quī audierant\f + \fr 4:2 \ft Cōdicēs antīquiōrēs habent μὴ συγκεκερασμένους, ‘nōn conjūnctōs fidē cum iīs, quī audierant’.\f*.`

## C. Brackets: words the modern editions leave out

**Decision:** accept (Nathan, 2026-11-17: "Apply them")

4. **2:7** *et operibus præfēcistī manuum tuārum*: TR καὶ κατέστησας αὐτὸν ἐπὶ τὰ ἔργα τῶν χειρῶν σου (NRSVue footnote "Other ancient authorities add *and set them over the works of your hands*"). Two poetry lines.
   **Apply:** `Heb.2.7` la: `honōre corōnāstī,` → `honōre corōnāstī[,`
   **Apply:** `Heb.2.7` la: `manuum tuārum.` → `manuum tuārum].`
5. **3:6** *ad extrēmum firmam*: TR μέχρι τέλους βεβαίαν (NRSVue footnote "Other ancient authorities add *to the end*").
   **Apply:** `Heb.3.6` la: `glōriātiōnem ad extrēmum firmam tenēmus` → `glōriātiōnem [ad extrēmum firmam] tenēmus`
6. **7:21** *ad ratiōnem Melchisedechī*: TR κατὰ τὴν τάξιν Μελχισεδέκ (NRSVue footnote "Other ancient witnesses add *according to the order of Melchizedek*").
   **Apply:** `Heb.7.21` la: `ad ratiōnem Melchisedechī.’”` → `[ad ratiōnem Melchisedechī].’”`
7. **12:20** *aut missilibus cōnfīgātur*: TR ἢ βολίδι κατατοξευθήσεται, the TR alone (NRSVue "it shall be stoned to death").
   **Apply:** `Heb.12.20` la: `lapidētur, aut missilibus cōnfīgātur.”` → `lapidētur[, aut missilibus cōnfīgātur].”`
8. **11:37** *temptātī*: TR ἐπειράσθησαν (NRSVue footnote "Other ancient authorities add *they were tempted*").
   **Apply:** `Heb.11.37` la: `lapidātī, sectī, temptātī sunt,` → `lapidātī, sectī[, temptātī] sunt,`
9. **10:34** *in cælīs*: TR ἐν οὐρανοῖς (NRSVue footnote "Other ancient authorities add *in heaven*").
   **Apply:** `Heb.10.34` la: `opēs in cælīs, et dūrābilēs` → `opēs [in cælīs], et dūrābilēs`
10. **8:12** *et scelera*: TR καὶ τῶν ἀνομιῶν αὐτῶν (NRSVue footnote "Other ancient authorities add *and their lawless deeds*"). At 10:17 the modern editions have the words, so 10:17 stays.
   **Apply:** `Heb.8.12` la: `peccāta et scelera deinceps` → `peccāta [et scelera] deinceps`
11. **3:1** *Chrīstum*: TR Χριστὸν Ἰησοῦν; the modern editions Ἰησοῦν (NRSVue "consider Jesus").
   **Apply:** `Heb.3.1` la: `nostræ Chrīstum Jēsum,` → `nostræ [Chrīstum] Jēsum,`
12. **11:13** *crēdidērunt, et*: TR καὶ πεισθέντες, the TR alone (NRSVue "from a distance they saw and greeted them").
   **Apply:** `Heb.11.13` la: `cōnspicātī crēdidērunt, et amplexī` → `cōnspicātī [crēdidērunt, et] amplexī`
13. **11:11** *peperit*: TR ἔτεκεν (NRSVue "even though he was too old"; its footnote, for the subject, has Sarah "past the age", without the birth). Who the subject is stays open: SBLGNT and NA28 differ there.
   **Apply:** `Heb.11.11` la: `ætātis peperit, quandoquidem` → `ætātis [peperit], quandoquidem`
14. **12:18** *montem*: TR ὄρει (NRSVue "something that can be touched", footnote "Other ancient authorities read *a mountain*").
   **Apply:** `Heb.12.18` la: `ad palpandum montem, ārdentemque` → `ad palpandum [montem], ārdentemque`
15. **10:30** *inquit Dominus*: TR λέγει Κύριος (NRSVue footnote "Other ancient authorities add *says the Lord*").
   **Apply:** `Heb.10.30` la: `rependam,”\qt* inquit Dominus. Item` → `rependam,”\qt* [inquit Dominus.] Item`
16. **6:10** *labōremque*: TR καὶ τοῦ κόπου (NRSVue footnote "Other ancient authorities add *labor of*").
   **Apply:** `Heb.6.10` la: `opus vestrum, labōremque cāritātis` → `opus vestrum, [labōremque] cāritātis`
17. **13:25** *Āmēn*: TR ἀμήν (NRSVue footnote "Other ancient authorities add *Amen*").
   **Apply:** `Heb.13.25` la: `omnibus grātia. Āmēn.` → `omnibus grātia. [Āmēn.]`

## Considered and left out

- 12:7 *Sī castīgātiōnem sustinētis* (TR Εἰ, the modern editions εἰς: NRSVue "Endure trials for the sake of discipline"): one letter, and the sense is near enough.
- 3:16 *nōn omnēs*: the modern editions differ only in accents and punctuation (a question, "was it not all…?"); punctuation is left out, as at 1 Cor 1:13.
- 1:3 *nostrōrum*, 10:8 *Deus* (the TR's 10:9): a word that changes nothing a reader needs.
- 2:9 χωρὶς θεοῦ, 3:2 ὅλῳ, 11:39, 12:3, 13:21: SBLGNT alone; NA28 with the TR.
- 9:14 *nostram*, 9:1, 12:3: Castellio already reads with the modern text.
- 7:5 *genus dūcentēs* (1573 without *ducentes*) and 11:29 *quod* (1573 *quost*): already decided in `data/decisions/Heb.orig.tsv`.
