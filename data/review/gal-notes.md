## A. Notes where the Greek text differs (the modern editions against the TR)

**Decision:** accept (Nathan, 2026-11-17: "Apply them")

1. **4:7** *hērēs Deī per Chrīstum*: TR κληρονόμος Θεοῦ διὰ Χριστοῦ; the modern editions κληρονόμος διὰ θεοῦ (NRSVue "an heir through God", footnote "an heir of God through Christ").
   **Apply:** `Gal.4.7` la: `etiam hērēs Deī per Chrīstum.` → `etiam hērēs Deī per Chrīstum\f + \fr 4:7 \ft Cōdicēs antīquiōrēs habent κληρονόμος διὰ θεοῦ, ‘hērēs per Deum’.\f*.`
2. **4:14** *meī temptātiōnem*: TR τὸν πειρασμόν μου; the modern editions τὸν πειρασμὸν ὑμῶν (NRSVue "though my condition put you to the test"). Missed by the candidates file.
   **Apply:** `Gal.4.14` la: `meī temptātiōnem, quam` → `meī temptātiōnem\f + \fr 4:14 \ft Cōdicēs antīquiōrēs habent τὸν πειρασμὸν ὑμῶν, ‘temptātiōnem vestram’.\f*, quam`

## B. A note where Castellio's Latin says what the Greek does not

**Decision:** accept (Nathan, 2026-11-17: "Apply them")

3. **4:17** *exclūdere nōs*: the Greek (TR and SBLGNT) ἐκκλεῖσαι ὑμᾶς, "they want to exclude you" (NRSVue; the Vulgate *vos*). 1573 and 1726 print *nos*; 1715 and 1747 correct it to *vos*. The note gives the Greek only, as at Luke 15:12.
   **Apply:** `Gal.4.17` la: `exclūdere nōs volunt` → `exclūdere nōs\f + \fr 4:17 \ft Græcē ἐκκλεῖσαι ὑμᾶς, ‘exclūdere vōs’.\f* volunt`

## C. Brackets: words the modern editions leave out

**Decision:** accept (Nathan, 2026-11-17: "Apply them")

In each, the NRSVue lacks the words. In item 9, *nostrī* and *Chrīstī* are in no Greek, and NA28 and SBLGNT lack κυρίου.

4. **3:1** *ut vēritātī nōn obtemperētis* and *apud vōs*: TR τῇ ἀληθείᾳ μὴ πείθεσθαι and ἐν ὑμῖν (NRSVue "Who has bewitched you? … publicly exhibited as crucified").
   **Apply:** `Gal.3.1` la: `fascināvit, ut vēritātī nōn obtemperētis? quibus` → `fascināvit[, ut vēritātī nōn obtemperētis]? quibus`
   **Apply:** `Gal.3.1` la: `fuit, apud vōs crucī fīxus` → `fuit, [apud vōs] crucī fīxus`
5. **3:17** *spectāns in Chrīstum*: TR εἰς Χριστόν (NRSVue "a covenant previously ratified by God").
   **Apply:** `Gal.3.17` la: `sānctum, spectāns in Chrīstum, lēx` → `sānctum[, spectāns in Chrīstum], lēx`
6. **5:19** *adulterium*: TR μοιχεία (NRSVue "sexual immorality, impurity").
   **Apply:** `Gal.5.19` la: `vidēlicet adulterium, stuprum` → `vidēlicet [adulterium,] stuprum`
7. **5:21** *homicīdia*: TR φόνοι (NRSVue footnote "Other ancient authorities add *murder*").
   **Apply:** `Gal.5.21` la: `invidiæ, homicīdia, ēbrietātēs` → `invidiæ, [homicīdia,] ēbrietātēs`
8. **6:15** *in Chrīstō Jēsū*: TR ἐν γὰρ Χριστῷ Ἰησοῦ (NRSVue footnote "Other ancient authorities add *in Christ Jesus*").
   **Apply:** `Gal.6.15` la: `Etenim in Chrīstō Jēsū neque` → `Etenim [in Chrīstō Jēsū] neque`
9. **6:17** *Dominī nostrī … Chrīstī*: TR τοῦ Κυρίου Ἰησοῦ; the modern editions τοῦ Ἰησοῦ.
   **Apply:** `Gal.6.17` la: `ego enim Dominī nostrī Jēsū Chrīstī notās` → `ego enim [Dominī nostrī] Jēsū [Chrīstī] notās`

## Considered and left out

- 4:6 *in vestrōs animōs* and 4:28 *Nōs*: a change of person only.
- 1:18, 2:11, 2:14 *Petrus* for Κηφᾶς: the same man (Castellio has *Cēphās* at 2:9).
- 4:26 *omnium*: a word that changes nothing a reader needs.
- 1:15 ὁ θεός: NA28 prints it.
