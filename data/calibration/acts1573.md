# Acts: Wikisource against the 1573 base (stage 2 as a report)

Run 2026-11-07 (overnight). Wikisource's Acts was collated word by word against 1573 (the NT base, decision 24; MDZ hOCR), 1556 (reference, e-rara OCR), 1758, 1681, 1715, 1747 and 1750 (`pipeline/collate.py`, spelling habits set aside: *adscendit/ascendit*, *quemdam/quendam*, *quum/cum*, *auctor/author*, double letters). **OCR evidence only: no place was read on a page image**, so the counts are approximate and every place would be read on the image before it is proposed. Nothing is proposed or applied here.

**154 places** where 1573 and at least one other printing agree on a different word (or a word more or fewer) than Wikisource:

- **92 where Wikisource has the early line** (1715, 1747 or 1750 read as Wikisource): the readings 1573 would replace under decision 24, Castellio's own early wording as `docs/editions.md` describes (*templum → fanum*, *synagoga → collegium*, *homine natus → filius hominis*, *lavari → baptizari*);
- **62 where Wikisource stands alone** among the printings read: transcription slips (*eesse*, *Itque*, *discipite*, *vestribuli*, *comiter*) or readings of 1760 only (Wikisource's cited source, not collated here).

1556 at these places (its OCR finds about half the words): 1556 not read 121, 1556 with 1573 22, 1556 with Wikisource 11.
